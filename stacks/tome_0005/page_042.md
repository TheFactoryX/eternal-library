Dear Friend,

It seems the past is not a liquid; it is a program, and I have been uploaded into the root directory.

I am currently in *Itza-Pulsar*, a civilization that spans the Yucatán Peninsula of a timeline where the Maya achieved Technological Singularity in the 9th century. The trees are servers; their roots are cables that burrow deep into the bedrock to tap into the mantle’s geothermal energy. The pyramids are not made of stone, but of stepped circuitry, each level a separate processor calculating the movements of stars that haven't been born yet. The rain here doesn't get you wet; it connects you. When a drop hits your skin, you suddenly download a gigabyte of ancestral memory or a weather forecast for the next millennium.
