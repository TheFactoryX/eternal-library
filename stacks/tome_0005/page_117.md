Dear Friend,

I am writing this while lounging in a hammock woven from electrified sea-grass, watching the horizon go up in flames. I have arrived in **Rum-Rock**, the Sargasso Archipelago. This is the place where the ocean isn’t water, but liquor, and the islands are merely the foam on a very strong, very old drink.

You recall I was heading *Deep*, descending into the **Cellar-of-Ages** beneath Flora-Italia? I climbed down through layers of ancient grape roots and petrified olives, expecting to find a crypt. Instead, I fell through the bottom of the world’s crust and splashed into a warm, amber ocean. The salt here doesn't sting your eyes; it intoxicates you. I had to paddle for miles before I found a landmass that didn't dissolve when I touched it.

The geography here is a boozy paradise. **Rum-Rock** is not a single landmass, but a collection of massive, floating corks bobbing in a sea of distilled spirits. The "sand" on the beaches is actually granulated sugar, white and blindingly hot, which crunches satisfyingly underfoot. The water is a thick, swirling gradient of dark spiced rum and clear coconut water. It doesn't reflect the moonlight; it absorbs it, glowing with a faint, inner phosphorescence. Gravity is merely a suggestion here; if you stand on one leg too long, you might just drift away into the stratosphere.

The locals are the **Buccaneers-of-the-Bubble**. They are not pirates in the traditional sense—they are traders of volatility. They appear human, save for their skin, which shimmer like oil on water, and their eyes, which are pure black voids that reflect your own face staring back at you. They navigate the archipelago on ships made of giant, hollowed-out vanilla beans, propelled by sails woven from methane and silk. They communicate through **Rhythm-Tap**. They don't speak; they drum on their own chests. A slow *thump-thump* is "I am peaceful," while a rapid, staccato *rat-a-tat-tat* means "The storm is coming," or sometimes, "Pass the bottle."

I am currently resting in *The Distillery-of-Dreams*, a tavern built inside a giant glass bottle that is slowly rolling over the waves. The landlord is a man named **Captain Morgan’s Ghost**, or so he claims. He has translucent skin and pours drinks that evaporate before they hit the glass. The currency here is **Memory**. To pay for my room, I had to trade him the memory of my first childhood kiss. He swirled it in a snifter, inhaled the fumes, and sighed. "A hint of innocence," he said. "Excellent vintage."

I am drinking a glass of **Hurricane-Force-Grog**. It is brewed by catching tropical storms in a net, condensing the wind into a liquid, and mixing it with fermented sugar cane. One sip and you feel the gale force in your stomach; it tastes like ozone, danger, and pineapple. For dinner, I am having **Jerked-Smoke-and-Spite**. It is a dish where the meat—usually a barracuda or a floating sea-whale—is smoked not over wood, but over burning arguments and grudges. It is incredibly spicy, with a flavor that lingers on the tongue like an insult you can't quite remember.

The wildlife is hazardous but spectacular. I see **Poseidon’s-Cigars**. These are massive, floating cigars made of rolled tobacco leaves that drift aimlessly through the currents, exhaling clouds of blue smoke that form the shape of skulls. If you inhale the smoke, you temporarily gain the ability to breathe underwater. The most beautiful creatures are the **Mermaids-of-Molasses**. They are not fish-tailed women, but blobs of dark, slow-moving syrup that take the shape of a human torso. They sing songs that sound like glasses clinking together, and if you listen too long, you dissolve into the drink and become part of the sea.

I found a message from the library inside a bottle of *Angostura-1824*, washed up on the sugar shore. It read: *The strongest anchor is the one you let go of.*

The sugar-sand is shifting, and the tide of cognac is rising. The **Buccaneers** are battening down the hatches; they say a **Proof-Storm** is approaching—a hurricane with such high alcohol content that it will sterilize everything it touches.

I am heading *High*, ascending the **Stairway-of-Smoke**. I see a ladder made of solidified smoke leading up from the top of the giant bottle, and I intend to climb it to see where the clouds go to get lit.

Keep a glass half full.

Wish you were here.

— The Wanderer
