**Origin:** The Crystalline Expanse of *Yhär-Kym* (The Blind Spot)
**Arrival Status:** The postcard is a jagged shard of highly polished obsidian, cold enough to burn your fingers. It is completely opaque, but if you warm it in your hands, the text bleeds through the stone in glowing white phosphorescence. It smells of ozone and peppermint.

Dear Friend,

I am writing this from the edge of the world’s largest blind spot. I have arrived in **Yhär-Kym**, the City of Seeing Stones. This is the place where the wind doesn't just blow; it *observes*. It is a place of terrible, perfect clarity where nothing can hide, not even a shadow.

You recall I was heading *West of the Setting Sun* to catch the night as it was poured from a jug? I chased the twilight until the horizon curved upward and tucked under itself. I stepped off the edge of the light and landed on a glacier made of frozen mirrors. It is colder here than a breaking heart, but the visibility is infinite.

The geography here is fractal and vertical. The city is carved directly into the **Flenser-Glacier**, a river of ice that moves so slowly it appears dead. The buildings are inverted spires hanging down into a crevasse of absolute darkness known as the **Ocular-Abyss**. I am currently sitting on the **Rim of Reflection**, a precarious ledge where the ice is polished to a sheen of 100% reflectivity. If you look down, you don't see your feet; you see the top of your head, staring back at you with infinite recursion. To the south lies the **Snow-Blind Sea**, a body of water so flat and still that it is indistinguishable from the sky, creating a disorienting white void that hangs suspended in zero gravity.

The locals are the **Optics**. They are tall, slender beings composed of glass and vitreous humor. They have no skin, only transparent lenses and muscle fibers that refract light into rainbows. They wear clothing made of spun sugar that sublimates in the cold, creating constant halos around their heads. They communicate through **Prismatic-Flash**. They don't speak; they arrange their internal lenses to cast specific colored beams. A quick pulse of ultraviolet means "Greetings," while a strobing red beam means "I am measuring your soul." I tried to have a conversation with a shopkeeper, but he just adjusted his focal length until I looked like a giant, forcing me to buy a pair of sunglasses to escape his gaze.

I am staying in *The Kaleidoscope-Inn*, a rotating room that shifts shape every hour. The floor is made of diamond dust, and sleeping on it feels like resting on a pile of broken stars. The concierge is a giant, floating eye that follows you everywhere you go, even into the shower. It is disconcerting, but the service is impeccable.

I am dining on **Photon-Pâté**. It is a spread made of trapped sunlight that tastes like lemonade and static electricity. It is served on **Shard-Bread**, which is dangerously sharp but melts on the tongue like a snowflake. For the main course, I am having **Glacier-Grits**, a warm porridge made from ground ice diamonds and savory rock-butter. It warms the stomach but makes your teeth chatter. For dessert, I am having **Zero-Kelvin-Sorbet**. It is so cold that it burns going down, and when you exhale, the frost forms perfect little geometric shapes that shatter when they hit the floor.

The wildlife is… illuminating. The primary scavenger is the **Shard-Shrew**. It is a rodent made of broken glass that scurries across the ice, chiming like a bell. It eats shadows, and where it walks, the ground becomes perfectly black. The apex predator is the **Mirror-Wyrm**. It is a dragon made of polished chrome that lives in the Ocular-Abyss. It doesn't hunt with teeth; it hunts by blinding its prey with reflections of their own deepest regrets. If you catch your reflection in its scales, you freeze solid, becoming another statue in the garden. I narrowly escaped one by closing my eyes and pretending to be a statue of a man closing his eyes.

I found a message from the library etched onto a contact lens left on a park bench. It read: *To see oneself clearly is the greatest form of blindness.*

The wind is picking up, and the temperature is dropping. My ink is starting to freeze.

I am heading *To the Ear of the World*. I intend to climb the great spiral stair to the place where sound goes to die. I want to hear the silence before it begins.

Stay reflective.

Wish you were here.

— The Wanderer
