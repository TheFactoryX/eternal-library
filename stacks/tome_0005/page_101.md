**Origin:** The Suspended Dunes of *Zerzura-Arabesque* (The City of Wind and Whispering Tiles)
**Arrival Status:** The postcard is a single, unbroken sheet of polished sandstone, incredibly thin and flexible, like slate-made paper. It is warm to the touch and constantly sheds a fine, golden dust. It smells of saffron, dry heat, and ozone. The text appears to be burned into the stone by a focused beam of sunlight, shifting slightly whenever you blink.

Dear Friend,

I am writing this while balancing on the railing of a balcony that isn't attached to anything. I have arrived in **Zerzura-Arabesque**, the Suspended Dunes. This is the Desert that exists in the corner of your eye when you blink—a landscape of mirages that decided they were substantial enough to build a city in.

You recall I was diving *Under* into the **Cenote-of-Reflections** in **Ciudad-de-Blanco**? I expected to see my own face staring back. Instead, the water acted as a lens, refracting me sideways into a dimension of pure heat and light. I fell upward, evaporating into the **Sky-Ocean**, and re-condensed here, miles above the ground, landing in a soft drift of sand that floats in the air like clouds.

The geography here defies gravity. The city is an archipelago of floating islands, each a chunk of ornate masonry—a minaret here, a tiled courtyard there—drifting through an endless, sapphire sky. Below us is no floor, only a vertical drop into the **Infinite-Dunes** that stretches forever. The sun is a fierce, geometrical disc hanging directly overhead, refusing to move. The only water comes from the **Cloud-Rivers**, streams of vapor that flow upward, defying the lack of rain.

The locals are the **Weavers-of-Glass**, tall figures wrapped in robes that ripple like wind on sand. Their skin is the color of terracotta, cracked where the heat has dried them. They do not speak; they communicate by **Glass-Blowing**. They carry long pipes that emit bubbles of colored smoke. A red sphere means "danger," a blue spiral means "I am lonely," and a complex, twisting gold shape means "Welcome, traveler." I tried to compliment a merchant’s wares, but I accidentally blew a gray bubble, which apparently means "Your camel smells of rot." We laughed about it later, mostly because he didn't own a camel.

I attempted to pay for a night's stay at *The Sleeping Sphinx*, an inn carved into the floating paw of a statue the size of a continent. The clerk, a man with eyes like polished lapis lazuli, refused my currency. "Gold sinks," he whistled, shaping a smoke bubble that looked like a sinking ship. "Here, we trade in **Shadows**." I had to stand perfectly still under the **Geometric-Sun** for an hour until my shadow was sharp and black enough to cut from the ground. I handed it to him—my shadow for a blanket. I feel lighter now, and slightly transparent.

I am currently sitting in a cushioned niche lined with **Flying-Rugs**. I am drinking a cup of **Coffee-of-the-Prophet**. It is brewed from beans roasted over the fires of burning libraries. It is thick, black, and chewy. Drinking it allows you to remember the future. I just had a vision of myself reading this postcard to you. You looked bored. I hope I was wrong.

For dinner, I am having **Couscous-of-Four-Hundred-Winds**. The grains are not semolina, but tiny pearls of sand gathered from different storms—the gentle breeze of a lover's sigh, the gale of a lost argument, the hurricane of a revolution. It is served with **Lamb-of-Stuffed-Silence**. The meat is incredibly tender because the sheep was raised in a vacuum. It tastes like déjà vu.

The wildlife is ornamental. I saw **Carpet-Snakes**, scaled serpents that fly through the air by flattening their bodies into intricate fractal patterns. They hum when they strike. But the most useful creatures are the **Origami-Camels**. They are not flesh, but folded paper beasts fueled by ink and prayer. I rode one here, and it didn't spit, it just blanked at me aggressively.

I found a message from the library written in the dust on my windowsill. The wind had arranged it into perfect calligraphy. It read: *The desert knows the truth of things: to survive, you must be empty enough to be filled.*

The **Geometric-Sun** is finally dipping, turning the floating dunes into a bruised purple. The **Weavers-of-Glass** are lighting their lanterns, which look like trapped stars.

I am heading *West*, riding an **Origami-Camel** toward the **Edge-of-the-Map**. They say the world folds back on itself there like a book closing. I hope to find the bookmark.

Stay cool.

Wish you were here.

— The Wanderer
