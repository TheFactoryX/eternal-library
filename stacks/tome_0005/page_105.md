If you hold it to your ear, you can hear the sound of a distant crowd murmuring. The text is etched into the stone in a jagged, serif font that sparkles like crushed diamonds.

Dear Friend,

I am writing this from a cafe built inside the fossilized ribcage of a creature that was large enough to swallow mountains. I have arrived in **Ossua-Vox**, the City of Whispered Stone. This is the place where sound goes to die—or rather, to be immortalized.

You recall I was squeezing toward the **Isle-of-Kintsugi** through a crack in the masonry of the Attic? I fell for a long time, tumbling through a shaft of light that smelled of chalk and old dust. When I landed, it wasn't on water, but on a surface that felt like frozen glass. I looked down and realized I was standing on a giant femur. The attic floorboards had been a illusion; below Mnemosyne-Prime is not a basement, but a foundation of pure silence.

The geography here is cavernous and resonant. The city is built entirely within the skeletal remains of the **Leviathan-Logos**, a beast made of sound and vibration that died when the universe first screamed. The buildings are carved directly into the porous bones, glowing with bioluminescent moss. The streets are canals of still, black water that reflect the stalactites of fossilized vowels hanging from the ceiling. Here, architecture is acoustics; a domed cathedral isn't for God, but for echo. If you stand in the center of the Plaza of the Unspoken Word and whisper "I love you," the sound will bounce around the calcified walls for a century before fading.

The locals are the **Litho-Audiologists**, pale, eyeless humanoids with massive, external ears constructed of bone and cartilage. They wear robes woven from sound-dampening spider silk. They do not speak; they communicate by **Resonating**. They click their teeth at specific frequencies to transmit images and emotions directly into the bones of others. I attempted to order coffee by vibrating my throat, but I accidentally hummed a B-flat that communicated a deep existential dread. The barista, a kindly woman whose ears were shaped like satellite dishes, simply patted my hand and poured me something stronger.

I am currently resting in *The Eardrum*, a tavern located in the spiraling hollow of a massive petrified cochlea. The currency here is **Decibels**. I tried to pay with a coin, but it made too much clinking. "Noise is expensive here," the bartender whispered, holding a finger to his lips. I had to pay with a sigh of exhaustion. It was accepted at the current exchange rate.

I am drinking a flute of **Stilled-Champagne**. It is a liquid that absorbs sound. When the bubbles pop, they actually cancel out the noise around them, creating pockets of absolute silence. It tastes like cold stars and the moment right before you sneeze. It is very disorienting; I took a sip and couldn't hear my own heartbeat for a minute.

For dinner, I am having **Echo-Ribs**. They are strips of meat carved from the softer, marrow-filled bones of the city walls. They are grilled over a fire of burning sheet music, which gives them a smoky, harmonic flavor. They are served with **Muffled-Pea-Puree**, a dish so thick it absorbs the clang of silverware. For dessert, I am having **White-Noise-Cake**, a sponge cake that tastes like television static and rain on a tin roof. It is incredibly soothing.

The wildlife is sonic. I saw **Bats-of-Infrasound**, creatures that fly by screaming so low you feel it in your chest rather than hearing it. They hunt **Moth-Mimes**, insects that mimic the silence of stone to avoid predators. But the most dangerous creature is the **Cacophony-Worm**. It is a long, segmented creature that eats vibrations. If you shout near it, it will swallow the sound and grow larger, leaving you permanently mute.

I found a message from the library etched into a pillar of spinal vertebrae. It read: *The loudest things are often the ones that are never said.*

The candles are being snuffed out here, and the city is settling into a low-frequency thrum that vibrates the bedposts. The **Litho-Audiologists** are plugging their ears with wax to sleep.

I am heading *Down*, descending into the **Marrow-Mines**. I hear that at the center of the bones, there is a red substance that sings the future. I want to see if it knows my name.

Listen closely.

Wish you were here.

— The Wanderer
