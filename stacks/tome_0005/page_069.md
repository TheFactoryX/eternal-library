**Origin:** The Ornithological-Geological Spires of *Sky-Serengeti* (The Roost)
**Arrival Status:** The postcard is a sheet of pale, vellum-thin pumice stone, surprisingly light and warm to the touch. It is covered in fine, golden-grey down that sheds when you turn it over. It smells of ozone, prehistoric dust, and expensive cologne.

Dear Friend,

I am writing this whilst clinging to the dorsal ridge of a hovering granite mesa, three miles above what used to be the Tanzanian plain. I have arrived in **Sky-Serengeti**, the Roost. It is an archipelago of floating islands that drift with the high-altitude currents, mirroring the migration routes of the earth below, but inverted in the air.

You recall I was taking the *Great-Ascension-Elevator* to the **Floating-Market-Of-Saints**? The elevator, unfortunately, suffered a catastrophic failure of its halo-hydraulics just as we passed the troposphere. The car detached, but instead of falling back to the world of men, it was caught in a thermal updraft so powerful it carried us upward into the stratosphere. I bailed out just before the carriage dissolved into a cloud of guilt and incense, parachuting onto the back of a passing *Boulder-Turtle*.

The geography here is breathtakingly vertical. We live on the backs of the *Leviathan-Clouds*, massive creatures that resemble cumulus clouds but possess the physiology of jellyfish. Their tentacles trail down for miles, harvesting rain and dust, which they digest to keep the islands floating. The terrain is a patchwork of savannah grasslands and sheer cliff faces. Gravity is a polite suggestion rather than a law; rivers flow in lazy spirals, and waterfalls sometimes cascade *upward* into the dry air, turning into mist before they can hit anything.

The locals are the **Aerial-Maasai**, nomadic warriors who herd the *Sky-Gnu*—large, shaggy beasts with fur made of condensed water vapor and hooves of hardened lightning. They dress in robes of red shuka fabric that has been woven with copper wire to conduct the static electricity of the air, allowing them to stick to the vertical cliffsides. They communicate through a series of clicks and whistles called *Echo-Locating*, which sounds like a conversation between two flint stones striking together.

I met a warrior named *Nanye* who invited me to his Manyatta, a circular enclosure built inside the ribcage of a fossilized thunderbird. He offered me a drink of *Fermented-Milk-and-Ozone*, a tart, fizzy liquid that makes your hair stand on end and your thoughts race at the speed of sound.

Dining here is a matter of timing. I attended a feast at the *Obsidian-Oasis*, a sinkhole lake filled with fresh water that never freezes. I ordered the **Meteor-Strike-Steak**, a cut of meat from a *Sky-Buffalo* that had been seared instantly by a stray bolt of lightning. It was smoky, rare, and tasted of pure energy. For a side dish, I had *Cloud-Couscous*, which is literally harvested from the softer parts of the Leviathan-Clouds. It absorbs the flavor of whatever sauce you use; I chose a spicy chili sauce made from crushed *Pepper-Volcanoes*.

The wildlife is majestic and predatory. The true lords of this domain are the **Solar-Eagles**, raptors with wingspans of thirty feet. Their feathers are made of polished gold that reflects the sun, blinding their prey before they strike. They hunt by diving at speeds that ignite the air around them, turning themselves into living missiles. I also saw a herd of *Giraffe-Gliders*, creatures with elongated necks that act as stabilizers, allowing them to drift between islands like living kites. They eat the leaves of the *Baobab-Balloons*, trees that shed their heavy bark and float when they are full of water.

I found a message from the library etched onto the wing of a dead Solar-Eagle that had fallen to the center of the island. It read: *The sky is not the limit; it is the floor.*

The sun is setting, and the horizon is a bruised purple. The Leviathan-Clouds are bioluminescing, turning a soft, sleepy blue. The wind is picking up, whistling through the hollows of the rocks. I feel light here, unburdened by the heavy gravity of the ground. I almost don’t mind that my coffee keeps trying to float out of the cup.

I am heading *Sideways*, riding the *Jet-Stream* to the **Labyrinth-of-Mirrors-in-Babel**. I hear the conversation there is deafening, but at least nobody interrupts.

Watch your head.

Wish you were here.

— The Wanderer
