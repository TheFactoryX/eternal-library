**Origin:** The Mycelial-archipelago of *Fungal-Siam* (The Rotting Lotus)
**Arrival Status:** The postcard is a slice of dried, woody bracket fungus, light as balsa wood and smelling faintly of anise and damp earth. The surface is fuzzy and soft to the touch. If you look closely, the white spore-print text seems to be slowly crawling across the surface, rewriting itself. Small mushroom gills on the edges pulse with a faint, neon-blue bioluminescence.

Dear Friend,

I am writing this while reclining on a patio made of giant, spongy shelf-fungi, watching the rain fall upwards. I have arrived in **Fungal-Siam**, the floating archipelago that drifts upon the **Sea-of-Spores**. This is the place where the jungle decided to stop climbing trees and start *being* the architecture.

You recall I was heading *To the Archives of the Unwritten* to fill in a few chapters of my life? I took a shortcut through a layer of dense fog, but the fog turned out to be the breath of a sleeping giant. I slipped on a patch of moss and tumbled down the throat of a cavern, landing softly in a pile of damp mulch. When I looked up, I saw that the "stars" were actually glowing dewdrops suspended from the ceiling of a root-system the size of a planet.

The geography here is hyphal and interconnected. The ground is not ground, but a dense, woven mat of mycelium that absorbs every footstep with a gentle *foomph*. I am currently in the **Stalk-City**, a metropolis built entirely inside the hollowed-out ribs of a single, deceased agaric that stretches miles into the sky. The streets are lined with caps of jelly-fungi that glow to provide heat. To the west lies the **Lagoon-of-Milk**, a stagnant body of nutrient-rich fluid where the **Lotus-Eaters** bathe to maintain their symbiotic relationship with the local mold. It is humid here, smelling of monsoons, fermentation, and the sweet rot of falling leaves.

The locals are the **Mycelians**. They are tall, pale humanoids whose skin is patterned with lacy veins of blue and green. They do not speak; they release pheromones. They communicate through **Scent-Syntax**. To say "Welcome," they smell of cinnamon and cardamom. To say "Danger," they smell of ozone and burnt hair. I tried to ask a street vendor for directions, but I accidentally smelled of fear (and unwashed socks), causing him to immediately spore a defensive cloud of purple dust. I spent the next hour sneezing glitter.

I am staying in *The Spore-Suite*, a hotel room that grows larger the longer you stay in it. I have to be careful not to stand still too long, or the furniture will try to assimilate my shoes. The concierge is a large, bipedal toad named *Bufo*, who wears a silk waistcoat and demands payment in nitrogen-rich soil.

I am dining on **Stir-fried Noodles-of-Kelp-Gill**. It is a slippery, savory dish where the noodles are actually living fungus that wriggle on the way down. For the main course, I am having **Durian-of-the-Damned**. It is not fruit, but a large, spiky orb that smells like dirty gym socks but tastes like custard and pure adrenaline. It is rumored to grant you visions of the future if you can survive the aftertaste. For dessert, I am having **Ice-Cream-of-Moss**. It is cool, green, and tastes like sitting on a riverbank in spring. It grows back in the bowl as you eat it, which is terribly convenient.

The wildlife is… collaborative. The most beautiful sight is the **Butterfly-Bombardier**. Insects with wings of stained glass flutter from flower to flower. When they land, they explode in a small puff of red smoke, only to reconstitute themselves moments later from the ash. The true nuisance is the **Argument-Mushroom**. They grow in dark corners and whisper passive-aggressive comments about your outfit as you walk by.

I found a message from the library growing in the fur of a local sloth-moth. It read: *Decay is just life remembering how to dance.*

The humidity is rising, and the giant slugs that act as the city's garbage disposal are beginning to march. I must pack my bags before the mist rolls in; the damp here has a habit of making people take root.

I am heading *To the Glass-Mountains of Shard-Siam*. I hear the sand there is made of powdered diamonds and cuts your shadow to ribbons.

Hold your breath.

Wish you were here.

— The Wanderer
