**Origin:** The Chromatic Reefs of *Iridescencia* (The Prismatic Trench)

Dear Friend,

I am writing this from the deck of a ship made entirely of polished glass, drifting on a sea that doesn't wet you. It’s an odd sensation, drowning in light. I have arrived in **Iridescencia**, the Prismatic Trench. This is the place where the physics of optics collide with marine biology.

You recall I was heading *Down* into the **Forest-of-Arteries** to garden the capillaries of a titan? Well, I took the job. It was noble work, pruning cholesterol-vines and massaging the vein-walls. But I made a terrible mistake: I used the wrong fertilizer. I used a mix of "Time" and "Regret," which caused the heart to beat in 7/8 time. The rhythmic irregularity gave the titan a nightmare. The titan sneezed, and I was blown out of the chest-cavity, through the strata of solid air, and landed here with a splash that sounded like a shattering orchestra.

The geography here is fractal and blinding. The islands are not made of rock, but of refracted light. A "mountain" here is simply a place where the spectrum stacks high enough to cast a violet shadow. The "water" is a thick, viscous fluid composed of liquid color. The Red Currents are warm and slow; the Blue Trenches are freezing and fast. I am currently moored in the *Bay of Green*, where the light is the color of absinthe and feels like thick silk against the skin.

The locals are the **Glass-Polyps**. They are humanoid figures made of living silica, completely transparent. You can see the food digesting in their stomachs and the thoughts pulsing in their brains (which look like glowing coral clusters). They are obsessed with transparency and angles. They speak in **Refractions**. They don't use words; they tilt their bodies to catch the sunlight, casting specific symbols on the walls of the coral caves. A sharp angle means "Welcome," while a diffuse scatter means "Go away." I tried to greet a elder, but I was standing in a shadow, so I reflected nothing. He thought I was a ghost and offered me a funeral. I quickly stepped into the sunlight to prove I had mass.

I am currently staying in *The Prism-Inn*, a hotel where the rooms are carved from giant diamonds. The rent is expensive: you have to pay with **Clarity**. I had to confess a secret I’ve been holding onto for decades to the concierge. She absorbed it, crystallized it, and placed it on a mantelpiece in the lobby. It is quite liberating, though now everyone knows that I was the one who stole the baker's spoon in 1894.

I am dining on **Spectrum-Sushi**. The chef catches fish that are literally beams of light slowed down in flesh. The "Yellow-Tail" is a fish that emits pure optimism. It tastes like lemon butter and feels like drinking a double espresso. For dessert, I am having **Diffraction-Cake**. It is a sponge that absorbs all the colors around it, so it looks black until you bite it, then it releases a burst of flavor that corresponds to the color of the shirt you are wearing. Mine tastes like *Blue*, which is a flavor mix of mint, cold steel, and nostalgia.

The wildlife is dazzling. **Coral-Crabs** scuttle along the seabed eating specific wavelengths to change their shell color. If they eat only red light, they become invisible in the infrared. The most beautiful creature is the **Laser-Eel**. It is a long, serpentine creature that focuses light through its eyes and fires beams to stun prey. I saw one cut a piece of seaweed in half from fifty yards away.

I found a message from the library floating in a bottle of solid blue light. It was cool and smooth. It read: *To see the truth, you must first break the beam.*

My time here is running out; the sun is setting, and the sea is turning into a dangerous shade of ultraviolet that gives me a headache.

I am heading *Across the Gradient*. I have spotted a bridge made of suspended dust motes that leads to a place called *The-Null-Point*, a theoretical location where all colors converge into white noise, and silence has a texture.

Don't let the shadows bite.

Wish you were here.

— The Wanderer
