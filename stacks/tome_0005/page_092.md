**Origin:** The Crystalline Tundra of *Gliss-Refra* (The City of Shattered Time)
**Arrival Status:** The postcard is a jagged shard of clear ice, infinitely cold to the touch. It does not melt, but it does "sing"—a high-pitched ringing tone that changes pitch depending on the angle of the light. The text is not written on the surface but is suspended *within* the ice, appearing as fractures and bubbles that form legible letters when viewed through a polarizing filter (or a squinted eye).

Dear Friend,

I am writing this wrapped in three layers of fur and a layer of silence. I have arrived in **Gliss-Refra**, the Crystalline Tundra. This is the Arctic that exists in the split second before a shiver—a land of absolute zero where the cold is not a temperature, but a solid state of being.

You recall I was heading *North-West* toward the **Clockwork-Principality-of-Schweiz**? I made the mistake of crossing the **Meridian-of-Misdirection**. The compass needle spun wildly, detached itself, and flew north like a fleeing bird. I followed it on foot, skiing across a field of frozen whispers, until the ice beneath my skis turned from white to diamond-clear. I skidded to a halt at the edge of a crevasse that looked down into the center of a geode.

The geography here is blinding. The ground is not made of snow, but of **Prism-Glass**. Every footstep sounds like breaking a window. The sun here does not rise; it refracts. There are five suns in the sky, each a different color of the spectrum, bouncing off the endless ice fields. The light is so bright it casts shadows in neon colors. If you turn in a circle quickly, you can create a rainbow with your own afterimage.

The locals are the **Prism-People**, tall, angular beings with skin like cut diamonds and hair made of fiber-optic cables. They wear clothing carved from solid blocks of quartz that clinks musically when they walk. They do not speak; they communicate by **Fracturing-Light**. They hold up mirrors or facets of ice to catch the sunlight, flashing coded signals at one another. To say "Hello," they flash a rapid sequence of violet and indigo. To say "I am cold," they simply turn opaque.

I attempted to pay for a room at *The Frozen Moment*, an inn carved entirely out of a single glacier. The concierge, a woman with eyes like multifaceted gems, informed me that my currency was useless here. "Money has no thermal mass," she chattered. Instead, the price was a **Secret**. I leaned over the desk and whispered a name I haven't spoken in twenty years. She accepted it, crystallized it into a small diamond, and dropped it into her cash register. It rang like a bell.

I am currently sitting in a chamber of solid ice. I am drinking a **Tincture-of-Absolute-Zero**. It is not liquid; it is a slurry of crushed ice and liquid nitrogen, laced with peppermint and pure adrenaline. You don't drink it; you hold it in your mouth until your thoughts stop moving, then swallow. It clears the sinuses and the existential dread instantly. The locals call it "Brain-Sorbet."

For dinner, I am dining on a **Steak-of-Light-Behemoth**. The meat is harvested from the **Aurora-Worms**, massive, serpentine creatures that swim through the sky, eating the Northern Lights. Their flesh is translucent and glows with a soft green pulsing light. It tastes like charged static electricity and starlight. It must be cooked over a fire of burning mirrors; otherwise, it phases right through the plate.

The wildlife is dangerous and beautiful. The **Shadow-Wolves** hunt here, but they have no physical form. They are two-dimensional silhouettes that slide across the ice, trying to steal your shadow. If they catch it, you become weightless and float away into the stratosphere. I have to keep stomping my feet to ensure my shadow stays anchored to my boots.

I found a message from the library etched into the wall of an ice cave. It was written in ancient script that kept frosting over. It read: *To hold onto a memory is to freeze it. To let go is the only way to keep it warm.*

The temperature is dropping. The five suns are setting, leaving the sky a bruised purple. The Prism-People are dimming their skin, turning the city into a sprawling landscape of dark glass.

I am heading *Up*, scaling the **Vertical-Ice-Flows** toward the **Cloud-Fortress-of-Olympus-Nova**. I hear the gods there are bored and looking for new board games.

Keep a light on.

Wish you were here.

— The Wanderer
