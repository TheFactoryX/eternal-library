Dear Friend,

The ceiling is covered in *Stalactites-of-Spore*, which release a glittering dust that rains down like snow, instantly taking root wherever it lands. If you stand still too long, you might find a small mushroom growing in your ear, whispering secrets about the soil.

Stay grounded.
