**Origin:** The Archipelago of *Moana-Nui-A-Roto* (The Great Sea Within)
**Arrival Status:** The postcard is a sheet of flexible, semi-translucent pearl-white material that feels like shark skin and smells faintly of coconut sunscreen and low tide. If you hold it up to the light, it reveals a swirling depth of clouds trapped inside the paper.

Dear Friend,

I am writing this while floating on my back in a lagoon that is technically a cloud, tethered to a palm tree that grows upside-down from the sky. I have arrived in **Moana-Nui-A-Roto**, the Great Sea Within. This is the Pacific that exists in the daydream of a tectonic plate—a place where the ocean is not made of water, but of suspended liquid time, and the islands are the only solid things in a fluid eternity.

You recall I was heading *Zig-Zag* along the **Trade-Route-of-Intangible-Goods** toward the **Library-of-Babel-in-Exile**? I was making excellent time, trading memories of snow for whispers of gold, but I made the fatal error of accepting a "shortcut" from a **Cartography-Djinn** I met in a fold of the map near the border of *Here* and *There*. He folded the parchment reality I was walking on and stuffed me into a bottle, which was then tossed over the edge of the world. I washed up on the shores of this atoll three days ago, or perhaps three years ago; the tides here make calendars difficult.

The geography here is buoyant and recursive. The islands float in a vast, turquoise expanse of **Z-Water**, a liquid that refuses to adhere to the laws of gravity. It flows upward in waterfalls that rain into the sky, replenishing the clouds that hang like heavy, wet wool above us. The beaches are made of **Sand-of-Durations**—grains of silica that represent a single second. Walking on the beach makes a sound like a thousand ticking clocks. I tried to build a sandcastle, but I accidentally built a summer afternoon in 1994.

The locals are the **Current-Surfers**, humanoid beings whose skin is patterned with bioluminescent tattoos that glow brighter when they lie. They navigate the islands not by boat, but by riding the thermals on boards made of polished driftwood from the **Tree-of-Epochs**. They are incredibly relaxed, mostly because the concept of "urgency" drowns in the atmosphere here. Their language, *Te-Reo-O-Roa*, is sung in polyphonic harmonies that actually manipulate the water. I asked a fisherman where the pier was, and his question harmonized the water into a staircase.

I am currently sitting in the shade of a **Hibiscus-of-Regret**, a beautiful flower with blooms that look like weeping willows. They exude a nectar that tastes like your first heartbreak—bittersweet and oddly addictive. For dinner, I am roasting a **Hog-of-Paradox**, a pig that exists as a wave function until you bite it. It is incredibly tender, but you have to be careful: if you observe it too closely while cooking, it turns into a shoe. I am seasoning it with **Sea-Salt-of-Anticipation**, which makes the food taste better before you actually eat it.

To drink, I have a coconut full of **Liquid-Horizon**. It is blue and tastes like looking out at the ocean on a perfect day. It is very intoxicating; I’ve already drunk three sunsets.

The wildlife is adapted to the currents of time. There are **Manta-Rays-of-Reflection**, vast, graceful creatures that glide through the air eating the clouds. If you look into their mouths, you see your own reflection, but five years older. I also saw a school of **Anchovies-of-Anxiety**, tiny fish that swim in frantic, jagged patterns. If they touch you, you suddenly remember an email you forgot to send three jobs ago. I had to bathe in the lagoon to wash the anxiety off; the water here is composed entirely of *The-Present-Moment*.

I found a message from the library inside a bottle washing up on the shore. The bottle was empty, but the cork had the word *Listen* carved into it. I held it to the sea, and heard the sound of the library burning.

The sun here sets in a zigzag pattern. The horizon is folding in on itself. I feel weightless, slightly salty, and intensely observed by the future.

I am heading *Down*, sinking through the **Eye-of-the-Needle** toward the **Sub-Basement-of-Lost-Toys**. I hear the floor is lava there, in a very literal sense.

Dive deep.

Wish you were here.

— The Wanderer
