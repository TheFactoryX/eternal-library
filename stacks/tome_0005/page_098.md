**Origin:** The Sub-Oceanic Trench of *Thalassa-Nesti* (The City of Glass and Suspended Time)
**Arrival Status:** The postcard is a heavy, triangular shard of transparent sea-glass, cool and perpetually wet. It smells of brine, iodine, and expensive perfume. When held to the eye, it magnifies the text tenfold, but if you look through it sideways, you see a swirling nebula of bioluminescent plankton. The text is etched in white, appearing to float inside the glass rather than sitting on the surface.

Dear Friend,

I am writing this from a cafe made entirely of bubbles. The tables are thin films of tension, the chairs are spheres of captured air, and the walls are living currents that allow the view of the outside to drift through. I have arrived in **Thalassa-Nesti**, the Sub-Oceanic Trench. This is the Ocean that exists in the space between a raindrop hitting the surface and the ripple reaching the shore—a moment of impact stretched into an eternity.

You recall I was stepping *Out* from the **Great-Serengeti-Plateau** into the **Starlight-Ocean**? I expected to float in zero gravity among the nebulae. Instead, I fell straight down, piercing the surface of a world that is all surface and no depth, yet deeper than the Marianas. I sank through layers of warm color until the pressure became a physical weight, pressing me into a new shape. I landed softly on a floor of compacted sapphire sand, surrounded by coral towers that hum show tunes.

The geography here is fluid. The city consists of **Glass-Spires** that rise from the abyssal plains, connected by bridges of woven water that don't get wet. The gravity is low, and the air is thick with oxygen and vapor. It is always twilight here, lit by the slow pulse of the **Dying-Sun-Coral**, a massive organism that mimics the sunset of a world that burned out a million years ago. The water doesn't flow; it hangs in globules, suspended in the air like slow-motion rain.

The locals are the **Lantern-Folk**, translucent humanoids with bodies full of swirling light. They have no bones, only cartilage that glows faintly blue when they are excited and red when they are angry. They communicate through **Bioluminescence** and **Pressure-Waves**. I tried to order a drink, but the waiter, a man whose head was a glowing orb of jelly, just pulsed a soft purple at me. I later learned that a rapid staccato flash means "I recommend the special," while a slow, dimming fade means "The check, please."

I attempted to pay for my lodging at *The Siphon*, an inn shaped like a giant conch shell. The concierge, a woman with hair made of waving kelp, refused my credits. "Money sinks," she bubbled, her voice sounding like a cello underwater. "Here, we trade in **Currents**." I had to tell her a rumor I heard in **Ril-Wadi** about the sheriff’s missing past. She absorbed it through her skin, shimmered with delight, and handed me a key made of ice that doesn't melt.

I am currently sitting in a hammock of woven kelp, drinking a glass of **Nectar-of-the-Depths**. It is a thick, golden liquid extracted from anglerfish lures. It is incredibly sweet and makes you feel like you are dissolving, which, in a way, you are. It gives you the ability to taste colors. The carpet tastes like red.

For dinner, I am having **Jellyfish-Salad-of-Regret**. The jellyfish are immortal beings that taste like sour candy and memories of embarrassment. It is served with **Kelp-Noodles** that are perfectly clear. The noodles are difficult to eat because they try to slide back into the ocean. I have to catch them with my fork and convince them to be eaten.

The wildlife is... architectural. I saw **Crab-Dragons**, massive crustaceans with shells made of lost shipwrecks. They crawl up the glass spires, stealing windows for their backs. But the most beautiful creatures are the **Whale-Orchestras**. They are immense, transparent whales filled with water and tiny glowing fish. When they sing, the fish rearrange themselves to form the notation of the song. I watched a humpback perform a symphony that looked like Morse code.

I found a message from the library floating in a bubble that popped against my cheek. It read: *We are all just water holding its breath. Exhale.*

The light from the **Dying-Sun-Coral** is fading, turning the water a deep, bruised indigo. The **Lantern-Folk** are dimming their bodies to sleep, looking like stars going out one by one.

I am heading *In*, burrowing into the **Coral-Brain-Matrix** at the center of the trench to meet the **Architect-of-Silence**. I hear it’s thinking of a new color, and it needs an audience.

Hold your breath.

Wish you were here.

— The Wanderer
