**Origin:** The Vitreous Isles of *Oculo-Mare* (The Seeing Sea)
**Arrival Status:** The postcard is a heavy, jagged rectangle of tempered sea-glass, smoothed by centuries of synthetic tides yet sharp enough to cut if held incorrectly. It is completely transparent. The ink is suspended *inside* the glass, floating between the layers like a trapped insect. When held to the light, the text casts a shadow that reads differently than the surface writing. It smells of saline and expensive cologne.

Dear Friend,

I am writing this while balancing on a stool made of crystal prisms, watching a school of refracted fish swim through the air above my table. I have arrived in **Oculo-Mare**, the Vitreous Isles. This is the place where the ocean decided it wanted to be seen, not just touched, and turned into solid, transparent panes.

You recall I was heading *To the Glass Archipelago*? Well, I arrived, but the geography was more literal than I expected. The islands here are not made of sand; they are immense, floating lenses of telescopic glass. The beaches are not shores, but sloped gradients of clarity where the sea-grass looks like spun fiber-optics. It is a blindingly beautiful place, where the horizon is segmented into a million tiny facets, and looking too long at the sun burns a permanent afterimage of your retina onto the ground.

The geography is optical and treacherous. Gravity here is determined by **refraction**. If you stand in the "focus zone" of the island, you are heavy; if you step toward the "dispersion edge," you become light enough to bound over waves in thirty-foot leaps. I am currently in the **City of Prismatica**, a metropolis of spires made of fused quartz that splits the sunlight into perpetual, colored rainbows that never fade. To the south lies the **Mirage Delta**, a region where the light is so thick and heavy with old visions that you can walk on it—but if the wind shifts and the light bends, you fall right through into the real water below.

The locals are the **Speculum-Folk**. They are humanoid beings with skin like polished mirrors and eyes that function like camera lenses, irises expanding and contracting with clicks. They do not speak; they communicate by reflecting the desires of the person they are talking at. To say "Hello," they simply curve their surface to show you your own face smiling back at you. To say "Go away," they show you a reflection of yourself rotting. I accidentally insulted a waiter by trying to adjust my tie in his elbow; he shattered into a pile of dust but reassembled himself instantly, looking rather annoyed.

I am staying in *The Hotel of a Thousand Glances*. The walls are transparent, which requires a severe adjustment of one's modesty. The rooms are lit by bioluminescent algae trapped in the floorboards. It is peaceful, though I keep waking up thinking I am being watched, only to realize it is just my own reflection staring back from the ceiling.

I am dining on **Spectra-Sashimi**. It is raw fish, usually **Flash-Tuna**, sliced so thin it is invisible to the naked eye. You can only find it by the cold spot on your plate. It tastes like the expectation of sushi. For the main course, I am having **Grilled Lens-Calamari**. They are squid with bodies made of flexible glass, cooked over a heatless flame that makes them ring like wind chimes when you cut them. They are served with a dipping sauce of **Liquid Focus**, a vinegar so sharp it clears your sinuses and makes your vision 20/20 for an hour. For dessert, I am having **Chromatic Parfait**. It is layered with red, blue, and green jellies that taste distinct, but when mixed together, turn the flavor completely white.

The wildlife is… illuminating. I saw a **Shard-Shark** today. It is a predator with a head made of diamond. It hunts by swimming directly at the sun, focusing its light into a laser beam to cook its prey. But the most fascinating thing is the **Privacy-Eels**. They are slim, dark creatures that absorb all light. If one wraps around you, you become invisible. It is a very popular pet here.

I found a message from the library etched onto a monocle left on a park bench. When I looked through it, the text rearranged itself to read: *I see you, therefore I am.*

The light is fading, and the "Focal Point" of the island is moving. I must adjust my angle or I will slide off into the wine-dark sea.

I am heading *To the City of Echoes in a Bottle*. It is a metropolis trapped inside a corked vessel, preserved in the moment of a song. I hope the acoustics are good.

Keep your eyes open.

Wish you were here.

— The Wanderer
