**Origin:** The Anechoic Expanse of *Null-Set* (The Zero Latitude)
**Arrival Status:** The postcard is a perfect, 1-inch square of heavy, matte-black cardstock. It makes no sound when dropped. The ink is a deep, velvety grey that absorbs all light; reading the words feels like staring into a cave.

Dear Friend,

I am writing this from a chair that may or may not exist, in a room that is defined only by the things that are not in it. I have arrived in **Null-Set**, the Zero Latitude, a place located exactly halfway between the tick and the tock. It is not on any map, because it is the space where the map folds over itself.

You recall I was following the *Current-of-Crete*, aiming for the **Nebulous-North**? I made the mistake of looking at my compass while crossing the Equator of Thought. The needle, confused by the sheer volume of unthinking, spun off its axis and dissolved into powder. I fell out of the ocean of logic and into the vacuum of *Null-Set*. It was a soft landing, mostly because there is no ground to hit, just a conceptual cushion of "nothing much."

The geography here is terrifyingly sparse. **Null-Set** is a vast, silent tundra of negative space. The horizon is not a line but a suggestion. There are no buildings, only "Volumes of Absence"—rectangular plots of land where the architecture has been surgically removed, leaving only the perfect shape of where a house used to be. It is architecture via subtraction. I am currently camping in a "Shadow-Gazebo," a structure made entirely of the shade cast by a palm tree that existed here ten thousand years ago. It provides excellent shelter, provided I don't try to touch the walls.

The locals are the **Oubliettes**, quiet, hooded figures who communicate by deleting sentences from the air. They are the custodians of the void. They do not speak; they *un-speak*. If you ask them a question, they don't answer it; they remove the question from reality, leaving you with a vague sense of peace and a sudden inability to remember why you wanted to know the time. I met a caretaker named *Nihil* who was sweeping the dust out of a circle of light. "We find clutter intrusive," he said, or rather, he didn't say it, and I understood him perfectly.

The local language is *Pausa*. It consists entirely of hesitation sounds, throat clearings, and the uncomfortable silence that follows a bad joke. A sharp intake of breath is a greeting. Exhaling slowly is a legal contract. I accidentally insulted a waiter by walking too loudly; he simply erased the sound of my footsteps for the next hour, forcing me to creep around in total silence.

Dining here is an exercise in minimalism that makes the *Calamari-of-Concepts* look like a five-course feast. I went to a bistro called *The Empty Plate*. I ordered the **Vacuum-Packed-Void**, a dish served in a jar that contains absolutely nothing. The menu described it as "tasting like the space between stars." It was surprisingly filling, though it left me feeling hungry for things I’ve never eaten. I also tried the **Silence-Soup**, which is hot water that has been boiled in a room where no one has spoken for a century. It tastes like lukewarm stillness.

To drink, I had a *Null-Tea*, a beverage that is the color of water but has the weight of lead. It doesn't quench your thirst; it negates it. You are simply no longer thirsty, nor are you not thirsty. You are neutral.

The wildlife is practically non-existent, which makes it more dangerous. There are **Concept-Mosquitoes**, insects that don't bite your skin but bite your *memories*. I woke up this morning with a sore arm and realized I had forgotten the concept of "blue." I spent an hour staring at the sky trying to remember what it looked like, until the color slowly bled back into my mind. Beware the **Echo-Bats**, creatures that fly out of your mouth when you speak, carrying your words away to feed them to the silence.

I found a message from the library written in the negative space on the back of my hand. It appeared only when I wasn't looking at it. It read: *To define a thing is to limit it. To be undefined is to be infinite.*

The silence is deafening. I find myself craving the mess, the noise, and the vibrant chaos of life. I need a place where the dirt is real, the history is loud, and the colors are saturated.

I am heading *Vertical*, taking the *Up-Draft* to the **Chroma-Canyons**. I hear the rocks there are made of solid pigment and the rain paints the streets.

Do not make a sound.

Wish you were here.

— The Wanderer
