**Origin:** The Sargasso of *Zom-Trouille-Dlo* (The Ghost Archipelago)
**Arrival Status:** The postcard is a swatch of rough, fibrous hemp, saturated with saltwater and smelling faintly of overripe rum and burnt sugar. The ink is purple and luminescent, glowing in the dark. If you hold the card to your ear, you can hear a faint, rhythmic thrumming like a bass drum played underwater.

Dear Friend,

I am writing this from the deck of a ship that was built from the ribs of a blue whale and commandeered by a man wearing a mask made of a conch shell. I have arrived in **Zom-Trouille-Dlo**, the Ghost Archipelago. This is the Caribbean that exists in the split second between the lightning strike and the thunder—a place where the physics of the ocean have decided to take a holiday, and the water is thicker than blood.

You recall I was heading *East* toward the **Monastery-of-Unanswered-Prayers**? I was making excellent progress, meditating on my own breath, until the air suddenly turned the color of a bruise. A storm made of glass shattered the sky, and I fell upward, caught in a thermal of hot brass music. I landed in the rigging of this floating graveyard, smack in the middle of the **Doldrums-of-History**.

The geography here is liquid and treacherous. The sea doesn't reflect the sky; it reflects *what happened here*. The water is a thick, violet brine called *Goman-Liqueur*, viscous as syrup. The islands are not land, but massive clumps of **Coral-of-Secrets**, calcified organisms that feed on whispered confessions. If you swim near them, you hear the sound of every lie ever told by a tourist. It is deafening.

The locals are the **Jumbie-Sailors**, spectral merchants who traverse these heavy seas on catamarans carved from giant single mahogany leaves. They are not alive, nor are they dead; they exist in a state of *Carnival-Perpetuelle*. They wear coats of many colors, sequined and feathered, and their faces are painted with the *Gris-Gris* of protection. Their language, *Langaj-Kriyòl*, is a creole of French, West African, and Dolphin. It sounds like a brass band falling down a staircase.

I attempted to order a drink by asking for *Dlo* (water), but the bartender, a woman with eyes like polished obsidian, laughed and served me a glass of **Liquid-Midnight**. It is heavy, dark, and tastes like starlight and molasses.

I am currently sitting on a barrel of **Rhum-Barikad**, a spirit distilled from fermented sugarcane that grows on the ocean floor. The fermentation process is accelerated by the heartbeat of a dormant kraken sleeping in the cellar. It is 160 proof, and a single sip makes you immune to fear for exactly four hours.

For dinner, I am enjoying a bowl of **Callaloo-of-Regret**, a stew made from dasheen leaves, crab meat, and the memories of people who missed their boat. It is spicy, creamy, and incredibly heavy; it settles in your stomach like a stone. To eat it, you must use a spoon made from the finger bone of a regretful pirate. It is delicious, but it makes you weep uncontrollably while you chew.

To drink, I have a **Painkiller-of-Second-Chances**. It is made with coconut milk, pineapple juice, and a nutmeg that grants you the ability to undo one tiny mistake. I’ve already taken two sips, and I have un-tied my shoes twice.

The wildlife is dazzling and hostile. The sky is filled with **Hummingbirds-of-Fire**, tiny birds that beat their wings so fast they ignite the air. They drink nectar from the hibiscus and spit hot sparks. Down in the heavy violet water, there are **Crabs-of-Misdirection**. If you try to follow a compass bearing, they skitter sideways and magnetize the needle, sending you in circles. I had to throw one overboard yesterday; it was trying to guide me into the mouth of a leatherback turtle the size of a bus.

I found a message from the library scrawled on the sail in tar. It read: *The horizon is just a suggestion.*

The night here is alive. The moon is a giant, glowing pearl pendant hanging from a thread of silk. The air vibrates with the bass of the deep water. I feel drunk, heavy, and strangely forgiven for sins I haven't committed yet.

I am heading *Southwest*, drifting on the **Trade-Winds-of-Nostalgia** toward the **Canyon-of-Discarded-Shadows**. I hear the light there is so bright it casts no shadow, only the memory of one.

Laissez les bons temps rouler.

Wish you were here.

— The Wanderer
