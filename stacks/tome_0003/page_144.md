Woke up at 8:45 AM. I slept on the floor last night. Because the ceiling is the floor and the floor is the ceiling, and it was just getting too confusing for my inner ear. I lay on the rug. It was actually the chandelier, but it’s flat now. I have a waffle pattern on my face. Grid marks. Like a graph. I look like a piece of graph paper. Very mathematical. $0.00.

Put on the "Invisible" wig. It’s made of glass fibers. You can’t see it, but it bends the light. So my head looks like it’s in a fishbowl. It’s very discreet. Nobody looks at you. They look *through* you.

Cabbed to the Factory at 10:00 AM. $9,100.00. The streets are still messy with all the orange and blue yarn. The sanitation department is using giant Zamboni machines to slurp it up. *Slurp, slurp*. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of Truman Capote. He was tiny. He could barely see over the steering wheel.

"It’s a mess, Andy," Truman squeaked. "It’s a lace doily exploded."

He ran over a pile of yarn. *Thump*.

"It’s just texture," I said.

When we got to the Factory, the rubber floor was gone. It was liquid today. Mercury. Quicksilver. The whole floor was a shimmering mirror. I couldn't walk in. I would sink. Or maybe float.

"It’s a reflecting pool," Bob said. He was standing on the surface. He looked like he was walking on water. He didn’t leave any footprints. Just ripples. *Shimmer*.

"I can’t get my shoes wet," I said. "They’re Calvin Klein."

"It’s not wet," Bob said. "It’s heavy. It’s just heavy metal."

We had to take the boat. The Factory gondola. Sammy paddled me across the mercury sea. *Slosh, slosh*.

At 11:15 AM, the rat arrived. He was paddling a bottle cap.

"I'm filing for bankruptcy," the rat said. "Rat Inc. crashed. The stock went to zero."

"Did you sell?" I asked.

"I held," the rat said. "I'm a long-term investor. Now I'm poor."

He dove into the mercury. *Splash*. He swam to the bottom.

The squirrel was there. He was fishing. He had a rod made of a coat hanger.

"I caught a penny," the squirrel said. He pulled up a coin. It was all slimy.

"Keep it," I said. "It's good luck."

At 12:30 PM, Jean-Michel called. He was on a phone that was shaped like a spray can.

"Andy?" he said. *Hiss*. "I'm out of paint. I'm painting the air."

"Is it expensive?" I asked.

"It's invisible," he said. "But I'm charging extra for the concept."

"It's a good concept," I said. "Send me the bill."

At 1:45 PM, we went to lunch. We went to "The Odeon". But we couldn't get a table. The restaurant was underwater. The waiters were swimming around with trays.

"I'll have the 'Steak Frites'," I said. "$55,000.00."

The waiter swam over. He was wearing goggles. He brought a plate. The steak was raw. It was moving. It throbbed. *Thump, thump*.

"It's Angus," the waiter said. "It's still alive."

"I can't eat this," I said. "It has a pulse."

"It's protein," Bob said. He cut a piece. The steak flinched. "It's fresh."

I ate a french fry. It was made of polymer plastic. It crunched. *Crunch*.

"It's plastic," I said.

"It's forever," Bob said. "It never goes bad."

At 3:30 PM, the Man in the Grey Suit came in. He was wearing a life jacket.

"The checks are bouncing," the Man said. "The mercury isn't liquid enough."

"Write them on the mirrors," I said. "Use a lipstick."

"It's not legal," he said.

"It's art," I said. "Art is a tax write-off."

At 5:00 PM, I worked on the "Portrait" paintings. I was painting R2-D2. I love robots. They are so consistent. They don't talk back. They just beep.

I painted him silver. Then I added a screen. It played a loop of him screaming.

"It's a commentary," I said.

"It's annoying," Sammy said. "Turn it down."

"I can't," I said. "It's wired into the canvas."

The R2-D2 painting started rolling around the floor. It knocked over a can of orange paint. *Sploosh*. It mixed with the mercury. The floor turned orange.

"Now it's soup," I said. "Campbell's Orange Soup."

At 7:30 PM, we went to a party. It was at the "Top of the Rock". Rockefeller Center. But we weren't on the roof. We were hanging off the edge. Bungee cords.

"Jump!" the host said. It was a guy named Elon. Or maybe a clone of Elon.

"Jump?" I asked. "I have a bad knee."

"I'll catch you," Elon said.

I put on the harness. It hurt my crotch. I jumped. *Boing*. I bounced. Up and down. I saw the whole city. The orange yarn. The carpet sky remnants.

"I'm a bird," I said. *Boing*.

"You're a yo-yo," Bob said. He was bouncing next to me. *Boing*.

Bianca Jagger was there. She was just hanging. She wasn't bouncing. She was suspended in mid-air. She looked like a spider.

"It's very still," she said.

At 10:00 PM, I unhooked. My feet touched the ground. I felt heavy. Very heavy. Like I was filled with lead.

"I need a cab," I said. "A heavy cab."

Cabbed home at 10:30 PM. $12,000.00. The driver charged me for weight. We sank into the asphalt. The tires melted.

Went into the townhouse. The floor was floor again. But the walls were breathing. In and out. *Whoosh, whoosh*.

I walked to the kitchen. The fridge was open. The light was out.

I took out a bottle of ketchup.

I looked at the label. "Heinz".

I squeezed the bottle. No ketchup came out. Just diamonds. Tiny, crushed diamonds.

"Again?" I thought.

I poured them onto a plate. They sparkled in the dark.
