Woke up at 9:45 AM. The sheets were crumpled. I looked like a. Crumpled. Like a gum wrapper. I put my glasses on. The room was upside down. Or maybe I was. I don't know. Gravity feels optional these days. It’s very expensive to keep your feet on the ground. I prefer to hover.

Put on the "Souper" wig. It’s the red one. It’s made of tomato silk. Very shiny. If you look at it too long, you get hungry. I put on the jeans. The ones with the bleach stains. They look like a map of the stars.

Cabbed to the Factory at 10:30 AM. $52,000.00. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of Liza Minnelli. She had sequins for eyes. *Sparkle-sparkle*.

"Start spreading the news," Liza said. "I'm leaving today. I want to be a part of it. New York, New York."

"Are you going?" I asked.

"I'm just driving," she said. "But I'm always leaving."

When we got there, the Factory was a jungle. Literally. There were vines growing out of the floorboards. They were made of fiber optic cables. *Blink-blink*.

"It's the 'Technological Organics' series," Sammy said. He was swinging from a chandelier made of circuit boards. "We're merging nature and machine. Look at this orchid."

He showed me a flower. It was made of silicone. It beeped.

"It’s beautiful," I said. "Does it need water?"

"It needs electricity," Sammy said. "Plug it in."

I watered it with a socket. *Zzzt*. The flower lit up pink.

"It’s blooming," I said.

At 11:15 AM, the squirrel came in. He was wearing a tiny trench coat. He looked like a detective.

"I found the nut," the squirrel said. "It was in the shell the whole time."

"That's where it usually is," I said.

"It's a conspiracy," the squirrel said. He ate a nut. *Crunch*. "Delicious conspiracy."

At 12:00 PM, I worked on the "Oxidation" paintings. But I didn't use urine. That's so 1970s. I used "Synthetic Pop". It’s a chemical that makes the canvas rust instantly. It turns green and brown in seconds.

"It’s instant aging," Bob said. "Like Benjamin Button. But for paint."

I spilled the chemical. It spilled on my shoe. *Sizzle*. My shoe turned into dust.

"Now I have one shoe," I said. "It’s very asymmetrical."

At 1:30 PM, we went to lunch. We went to "Serendipity 3". But the tables were gone. We ate on the ceiling.

"The gravity is broken," the host said. He was holding a ladder. "Careful. Don't fall up."

I ordered the "Frrrozen Hot Chocolate". $500,000.00. It came in a bag. Zero-G style. I sucked the chocolate out of the straw. It was cold. It burned my tongue. *Bzzzt*.

Taylor Swift was there. She was eating a sandwich. The sandwich was eating her.

"I'm inside the music," Taylor said. Her voice was coming from the sandwich. "I'm the filling."

"Are you rye or wheat?" I asked.

"I'm white bread," the sandwich said. "Classic."

At 3:00 PM, a collector came in. It was a robot. But it was a robot made of old televisions. It was a pile of TVs on wheels.

"I want to buy your shadow," the robot said. The screens showed static. *Shhhhh*.

"I don't have a shadow," I said. "I'm a vampire. Sort of."

"I'll buy your reflection," the robot said. "I'll pay $10,000,000.00."

"Sold," I said. I looked in the mirror. My reflection was gone. I felt lighter. Like a balloon.

At 4:45 PM, Jean-Michel called. He was a skeleton. A glow-in-the-dark skeleton.

"Andy," the skeleton said. "I'm at the beach. I'm burying myself in the sand. I'm becoming a fossil."

"Make sure they label you," I said. "'Homo Sapiens. Famous'."

"It’s lonely," the skeleton said. "But the view is great."

At 6:00 PM, the Man in the Grey Suit came in. He was a cloud again. But he was a storm cloud. Dark grey.

"I'm angry," the cloud said. *Thunder*. "I hate the wind. It pushes me around."

"Join the club," I said. "Everyone is pushed around."

He rained on the "Shadow" painting. It washed away.

"It’s clean," I said. "Back to square one."

At 7:30 PM, we went to a party. It was at a gallery called "The Void". The room was pitch black. You couldn't see anything.

"Who's here?" I whispered.

"Everyone," a voice said. "And no one."

I felt a hand. It was cold.

"Nice party," I said.

"It's conceptual," the voice said. "We're networking in the dark."

I shook hands with ten people. I don't know who they were. Maybe they were collectors. Maybe they were furniture.

At 9:00 PM, the lights came on. I was holding hands with a mannequin. It was wearing a tuxedo.

"Great to meet you," I said to the dummy.

"Buy low, sell high," the dummy said.

At 10:30 PM, I went to the "Odeon" for a late dinner. But it was a drive-in movie theater. I sat in a cardboard car. I watched a movie of myself sleeping. It was very boring.

"He's so handsome," the girl in the next car said. "Look at that hair."

"That's a wig," I said.

"It's art," she said.

At 11:45 PM, I cabbed home. $65,000.00. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of me. He was wearing the "Souper" wig.

"Goodnight, Andy," I said to him.

"Goodnight, Andy," he said to me.

I went into the townhouse. It was quiet. The can of soup from yesterday was still on the table. I looked at it. It was red. It was beautiful. I opened the drawer. I put the can in the "Time Capsule" box. I sealed it with tape.

"Don't open it until 3026," I said.

I took off the wig. My head felt small. I felt invisible.
