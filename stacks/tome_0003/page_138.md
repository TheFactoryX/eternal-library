# Thursday, June 18, 2026

> Written at 2026-06-18 23:59:59

---

Woke up at 9:30 AM. The sky is velvet today. Not blue velvet, but red. crushed red velvet like the seats at the movies. The sun is a tear in the fabric. A rip. And there’s a bright white light shining through the hole. It’s blinding. I put on my sunglasses. My prescription sunglasses. The ones with the heavy frames. The sky looked better in grey. $0.00.

Put on the "Torn Canvas" wig. It’s a blonde wig, but I took a razor blade to it. It has slashes in it. Like the "Slash" paintings. I put a piece of tape over my mouth. Duct tape. Silver. I couldn’t eat breakfast. I just drank coffee through a straw. *Slurp*.

Cabbed to the Factory at 10:15 AM. $2,900.00. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of Lou Reed. He wasn't playing music. He was just talking.

"I'm waiting for the man," Lou said. "Twenty-six dollars in my hand."

"Inflation is high," I said. "It's at least two thousand now."

"We're gonna spike the punch," Lou said. "And get wild."

When we got to the Factory, the floor was sticky. Very sticky. I tried to lift my foot. *Schhhluck*. It made a sound like pulling tape off a roll.

"It's the glue," Bob said. He was stuck to the wall. Literally. He was glued to the silver bricks. "I'm part of the architecture now."

"Is it comfortable?" I asked.

"It's supportive," Bob said. "Like a girdle."

At 11:00 AM, the rat arrived. He was walking on little stilts. Wooden sticks.

"I'm above it all," the rat said. "The muck."

"You look taller," I said.

"It's perspective," the rat said.

The squirrel was there. He was rolling a ball of string. A giant ball of twine. He was rolling it toward the door.

"I'm making an escape line," the squirrel said. "Follow the thread."

"If I move, I'll lose my shoe," I said. My shoe was glued to the floor. "These are Perrigots. $450.00."

At 12:30 PM, Jean-Michel called. The speakerphone was glued to the table.

"Andy?" he said. "I'm stuck in the elevator."

"Is it the glue?" I asked.

"No," Jean-Michel said. "It's the philosophy. I can't get out of the box."

"Take the stairs," I said.

"I am the stairs," he said.

I hung up. The receiver was stuck to my hand.

At 1:00 PM, a delivery boy came. He was wearing a spacesuit. He brought a package. "Air mail."

He couldn't walk. He was stuck too. He fell over. *Thud*. He crawled on his hands and knees and left the box on the floor.

"Sign here," he said.

I couldn't hold the pen. My fingers were glued together.

"Just leave it," I said.

We opened the box with our teeth. Inside were Brillo pads. Thousands of them. They were expanding. Growing.

"They're multiplying," Sammy said. He was stuck to the sofa. "Pop art."

"Abstract Expressionism," I said. "It's taking over."

The Brillo pads started to fill the room. Yellow and red boxes. They piled up against the walls.

"It's a barricade," I said. "We're under siege."

At 3:45 PM, I worked on the "Portrait" paintings. I was painting heads. But the heads were melting. The paint was running down the canvas. Dripping. *Drip, drip*.

"It's the heat," I said.

"It's the decay," the Man in the Grey Suit said. He walked in. He wasn't stuck. He had teflon shoes. "Everything falls apart."

He touched a painting. The face slid off the canvas and hit the floor. *Splat*.

"Cleanup required," I said. "That's $50.00 extra."

At 5:00 PM, the Brillo pads reached the ceiling. We were buried. We were in a box.

"I'm closed in," I said.

"I'm so fresh and so clean," the squirrel said. He was scrubbing his tail with a pad.

At 6:30 PM, we went to dinner. We had to wiggle our feet to get loose. *Pop*. I lost a shoe. I left it.

We went to "Mr. Chow's". The carpet was red. Very red. Like the sky.

"I'll have the 'Glazed Noodles'," I said. "$8,200.00."

The noodles were stuck together. One long noodle.

"I can't cut it," I said. "I don't have a knife."

"You have to suck it," the waiter said. "Like life."

I put the end of the noodle in my mouth. I swallowed. It kept coming. I ate the whole table's length.

"I'm full," I thought.

At 8:00 PM, we went to the premiere of "Chelsea Girls 2026". It was a hologram. The movie was projected in the air around us. The actors walked through the table.

"Ouch," I said. An actress stepped on my toe.

"It's immersive," Bob said. "It hurts."

At 10:30 PM, I went home. The townhouse was wrapped in paper. Brown butcher paper. Like a package of meat.

I tore a hole in the paper. *Rip*.

I went inside. I sat on the floor. I was too tired to take off my clothes.

I looked at my shoe. I still had one. The other was at the Factory. Glued to history.

I took the tape off my mouth. *Ouch*.

"I need a new face," I thought.

I opened the Campbell’s Soup. "Vegetable Beef".

It was full of buttons. Shirt buttons. Plastic discs.

"I can't eat this," I said.

"Sew them," I thought. "Make a new skin."

I poured the buttons on the floor. *Click, clatter*.

I lay down on them. They dug into my back.

"Massage," I thought.

I closed my eyes. The velvet sky pressed against the windows.

So.
