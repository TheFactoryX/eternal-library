Saturday, March 07, 2026

Woke up at 10:00 AM. The sky was the color of silver foil. Crinkled. Shiny. It looked like a wrapped sandwich. I stayed in bed for a long time. I was staring at the ceiling. There was a watermark shaped like Marilyn Monroe’s profile. It was very beautiful. It was an accident, so it was art. I didn't want to move because the "Moon Rock" was still under the pillow. It was vibrating. *Hummmmm*. It felt like I was sleeping on a running dryer. My teeth were chattering. *Clack-clack-clack*.

I got up. I floated to the closet. I’m still defying gravity. It’s very convenient. I don't have to walk. I just glide. I put on my "Gold Lame" jumpsuit. It reflects the TV. I looked in the mirror and I could see the news scrolling across my chest. "Stocks Crash. Robot Uprising. Weather: Cloudy with a chance of meatballs." I looked delicious.

Cabbed to the Factory at 11:30. The streets were full of "Vintage Humans." They were the real ones. Not Simulacra. They looked wrinkled and soft. They were wearing "Authenticity" as a perfume. It smelled like old paper and B.O. It was $200.00 an ounce at Bergdorf’s. The cab ride was $40.00. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of James Dean. He drove very fast. He said, "Andy, we have to get there before we die." I said, "We already did." He laughed. It sounded like a car crash. *Crunch*.

When I got to the Factory, the hole in the wall was gone. It was plastered over. Sammy said it was "too distracting." He put a painting over it. It was a painting of a hole. It cost $5,000.00. It’s better than the real thing. It’s cleaner.

I went to the back room. I started silk-screening. I’m doing a series called "Endorsements." I’m taking corporate logos and putting them on religious icons. I did a picture of the Pope holding a can of "Coca-Cola." It cost $120.00 for the ink. I spilled some red ink on the floor. It looked like blood. I left it. It added "drama." Sammy came in. He was eating a "Data-Apple." He took a bite and numbers came out. He said, "Andy, the collectors are here." He said it with his mouth full. *Crunch*.

The collectors were from "Mars Colony 4." They were wearing bubbles on their heads. They were green. They had antennas. They wanted to buy the "Electric Chair." The one that lights up. I said, "It’s not for sale." They offered me a deed to a crater. I said, "Okay." I signed the paper. I hope the crater has a good view. I hope there are no rocks.

Lunch was "Synthetic Caviar." It was made of pearls. Very chewy. It was $150.00. I washed it down with "Diet Water." It has 0% hydration. It makes you thirsty for more. It’s a very good business model.

Around 3:00 PM, the "Moon Rock" in my pocket started to get hot. It was burning my thigh. I took it out. It was glowing bright white. It was smoking. A "Simulacrum" of David Bowie floated in. He doesn't walk. He just hovers. He was wearing a suit made of stars. He said, "Andy, drop it." I said, "I can't. It’s my only friend." He said, "It’s a ticket." I said, "To where?" He said, "To the show." He pointed to the ceiling.

We went to a party at "The Top of the Standard." But the Standard is 500 stories high now. We took the "Space Elevator." It was made of glass. I could see the whole city. It looked like a circuit board. I saw the tiny people. They looked like ants. But ants with credit cards.

The party was for "The Day the Music Died." They played it backwards. It sounded like the future. I saw a "Simulacrum" of Elvis. He was thin again. He was wearing a jumpsuit made of "Velvet Television." He sang "Heartbreak Hotel." He sounded like a synthesizer. I cried. My tears were made of mercury. They rolled down my face and shattered on the floor. *Ping*.

I had a "Nuclear Warhead" cocktail. It was $200.00. It was served in a lead glass. It steamed. It tasted like strawberries and fallout. I drank it. My hair stood on end. Literally. The wig flew off. It attached itself to the ceiling fan. It spun around. It looked like a blancmange. I had to buy the wig back from the bartender. He charged me $500.00. He said it was "art."

I left at 11:00 PM. I was tired. My atoms were separating. I could feel my toes floating away. I had to hold onto my arm to keep it attached.

Cabbed home. It was $65.00. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of my mother. Again. She was driving a hearse. She said, "Andrew, you look terrible." I said, "I look expensive." She said, "Same thing." She handed me a receipt. It was for my birth. It cost $0.05.

Came home at 11:45. The apartment was quiet. The "Moon Rock" was back on the table. It had stopped glowing. It was just a grey lump. It looked like a piece of gum. I put it in the drawer with the wigs and the Time magazines.

I took off the jumpsuit. I looked in the mirror. My skin was clear. The silver was gone. I looked solid. I looked real. It was very disappointing.

I brushed my teeth. The toothpaste was blue. I looked at the sink. The " Marilyn Monroe" watermark was gone.

I got into bed. The sheets were cold.

I closed my eyes. I listened to the city. It was humming. It was buzzing. It was the sound of money changing hands.
