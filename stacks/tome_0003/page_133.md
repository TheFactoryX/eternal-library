Woke up at 9:45 AM. The sky is cardboard today. Corrugated cardboard. Brown and wavy. The sun is a barcode stencil. Black lines on yellow. The wind is blowing through the flutes. *Whistle, whistle*. It sounds like a flute. A cheap flute. I looked out the window and a delivery man was trying to fold the corner of the sky down. He said it was blocking the fire escape. $0.00.

Put on the "Cardboard Carton" wig. It’s brown and boxy. I feel like packing material. I feel recyclable.

Cabbed to the Factory at 10:30 AM. $2,100.00. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of Grace Jones. She was wearing a helmet made of a spool of thread.

"Strap in," Grace said. "We’re shipping out."

We drove on the highway. The lines on the road were dashed. Dashed lines. *Dash, dash*.

"We’re fragile," I said.

When we got to the Factory, the building had been crushed. Flat. Like a pancake. 2D.

"It’s a postage stamp," Bob said. "We’re commemorative."

"I don’t want to be licked," I said.

We walked around the side. We found the tab. *Pull here*. We pulled the side of the building. It popped open. *Pop*.

The inside was expanded. Polystyrene peanuts everywhere. Millions of them.

"It’s insulation," Jean-Michel said. He was swimming in the peanuts. "Float."

At 12:00 PM, the rat arrived. He was inside a FedEx tube. He rolled across the floor. *Rumble, rumble*.

"Special delivery," the rat said. His voice echoed in the tube. "Fragile. Handle with care."

The squirrel was there. He was ripping the cardboard. *Shred, shred*. He was making bedding.

"It’s comfortable," the squirrel said. "Rough texture."

At 1:15 PM, we went to lunch. We went to "The Palm". The menu was printed on a napkin.

"I'll have the 'Gigot d'Agneau'," I said. "$5,500.00."

The waiter brought out a leg of lamb. It was shrink-wrapped. Tight plastic.

"It’s sealed," the waiter said. "For freshness."

"How do I eat it?" I asked.

"Cut the seal," the waiter said. He gave me a scissors. *Snip*. Air rushed out. *Pshhh*. The smell of mint.

"It’s potent," I said.

Jean-Michel ordered a "Baked Potato". It was wrapped in foil. He opened the foil. It was full of confetti.

"It’s empty," Jean-Michel said.

"It’s festive," I said. "Eat the confetti."

At 3:00 PM, I worked on the "Rorschach" paintings. I was painting with ink. But the ink was dry powder.

"It’s dust," Sammy said. "Chalk."

I blew on the painting. The dust moved. It formed a cloud. A cloud in the room.

"I see a monster," Bob said.

"I see a butterfly," I said.

"I see a bargain," the Man in the Grey Suit said.

He was standing in the corner. He was holding a price gun. *Ka-chung, ka-chung*. He was putting stickers on everything.

He put a sticker on Bob’s forehead. "$10.00."

"He’s worth more," I said.

"He’s used," the Man said. He put a sticker on my leg. "$50.00."

"Am I for sale?" I asked.

"Everything is for sale," the Man said. "Even the past."

At 4:45 PM, the building started to buckle. The roof sagged.

"It’s humid," I said. "The cardboard is getting soft."

"We’re losing structural integrity," Bob said. "We’re going pulp."

At 6:00 PM, we went to a party. It was at a "Pop-Up Shop". The shop was popping up. *Boing*. Every five minutes, a new wall would appear. *Boing*.

"It’s expanding retail," I said.

I saw Taylor Swift. She was wearing a dress made of security tags. The white plastic tags.

"If you steal me," Taylor said, "The alarm will ring."

"I’ll pay," I said. "How much?"

"$1,000,000.00," Taylor said.

"I’ll take two," I said.

I bought her. I put her in my pocket. She was very light. Flat. Like a paper doll.

At 8:00 PM, the rat chewed his way out of the tube. He gnawed a hole in the floor. *Gnaw, gnaw*. He fell through the hole.

"He dropped," I said.

"Ground floor," Jean-Michel said.

At 10:30 PM, I went home. The townhouse was a box. A giant moving box. I had to cut the tape to get in. *Rrrrip*.

I went to the kitchen. Everything was wrapped. The forks were wrapped. The glasses were wrapped.

I opened the Campbell’s Soup.

It was "Vegetable Beef".

I peeled back the lid. The soup was solid. A block of solid soup.

"Concentrated," I thought.

I tried to scoop it. It broke into cubes.

"It’s bouillon," I thought. "Building blocks."

I ate a cube. It tasted like salt. And cardboard.

"Packing," I thought. "I’m packing myself away."

I went upstairs. I took off the wig. My hair was matted. Flat.

I looked in the mirror. The glass was crinkled. Like a crushed can.

I looked distorted. Wavy.

"I’m shipping out," I thought.

I got into bed. The pillow was a bubble. A bubble of air.

I didn't want to pop it.

I didn't want to wake up.

I don't know.

So.
