Woke up at 9:30 AM. The sky is carpet today. Shag carpet. Orange and electric blue swirls. Thick, plush piles of clouds. It looks like the inside of a 1970s basement. Or a cheap motel room. God must be remodeling. He’s going for a retro look. It’s cozy, but I worry about the dust mites. Do they have dust mites in heaven? Or just angel dander? I sneezed. *Achoo*. The echo lasted for ten minutes. The acoustics in the carpet sky are terrible. Very dead. $0.00.

Put on the "Shag" wig. It’s a wig made of shredded aluminum. It looks like tinsel. It catches the light. Every time I move my head, I blind myself. It’s very disco. Very dangerous.

Cabbed to the Factory at 10:45 AM. $6,500.00. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of Liza Minnelli. She had spangles glued to her eyelids.

"Turn left," I said.

"I can't turn left," Liza said. "I'm doing a pivot."

She spun the car around on two wheels. *Screech*.

"We're dancing," I said.

"It's choreography," she said. "The grid is our stage."

When we got to the Factory, the floor was gone. Just a big hole. A black void.

"It's an open floor plan," Bob said. He was floating in the corner. He had a little jetpack on. *Fwoosh*.

"I can't work like this," I said. "I need gravity."

"Gravity is old fashioned," Bob said. "Get with the G-force."

I jumped. I didn't come down. I floated up to the ceiling. My face hit the plaster. *Bonk*.

"I feel sick," I said.

"It's vertigo," Sammy said. He was upside down, walking on the ceiling. "It's the new normal."

At 11:30 AM, the rat flew in. He had little wings. Bat wings. He looked like a mouse-bat.

"I'm a vampire now," the rat said. "I suck electricity."

He bit the extension cord. *Zzzzt*. The lights dimmed.

The squirrel was there. He was holding an acorn, but he couldn't eat it because he was floating. The acorn floated away.

"Come back," the squirrel said. "That's my portfolio."

At 12:15 PM, the dentist arrived. Dr. Silverstein. But he wasn't a dentist anymore. He was a "Smile Architect".

He set up a drill in mid-air. It floated on a drone.

"Open up," Dr. Silverstein said.

I opened my mouth.

"Your teeth are boring," he said. "They’re too white. They need branding."

He drilled a logo into my molar. *Whirrrr*.

"What is it?" I asked. I couldn't feel my face.

"It's a barcode," he said. "Now your mouth is a point-of-sale terminal."

"Will it hurt?" I asked.

"Only when you chew," he said.

At 1:00 PM, Jean-Michel called. He was on the intercom. The intercom was inside my filling. I could hear him vibrating through my jaw.

"Andy?" he said. *Vibrate, vibrate*. "I'm painting the shadow of the Empire State Building."

"Is it dark?" I asked.

"It's invisible," he said. "But I'm charging by the inch."

"Don't forget the tip," I said. My jaw ached.

At 2:30 PM, we went to lunch. We went to "The Carnegie Deli". But it was a drive-thru. You drive your car right through the middle of the restaurant.

"I'll have the 'Pastrami on Rye'," I said. "$25,000.00."

The waiter handed me a sandwich through the cab window. But the bread was made of foam rubber. The meat was magnetic tape.

"It's a tape sandwich," I said.

"It's recordings," the waiter said. "Listen to the lunch."

I put the sandwich to my ear. *Munch, munch*. I heard people eating.

"It’s delicious," I said. "Very audio."

Bob ordered the "Dr. Brown’s Cel-Ray Tonic". It came in a hypodermic needle.

"For quick energy," Bob said. He injected the soda into his arm. *Burp*.

At 4:00 PM, I worked on the "Disaster" paintings. I was painting car crashes. But the cars were floating. Bumping into each other in zero gravity. It looked like a slow-motion ballet.

"It's a fender bender," Sammy said. "In space."

"I call it 'Auto Accident #5' (Floating)," I said.

I spilled some silver paint. It floated up in a perfect sphere. A silver bubble.

"Don't pop it," I said. "It's worth more than the painting."

At 5:30 PM, the Man in the Grey Suit came in. He was wearing magnetic boots. He clunked across the ceiling.

"We need to sign the checks," the Man said.

"I'm upside down," I said.

"So is the economy," the Man said.

He handed me a pen. It floated out of my hand. It drifted away.

"Come back," I said. "That’s a Montblanc."

At 7:00 PM, we went to a party. It was on a blimp. The "Good Year Blimp". But it was filled with helium. We all talked in squeaky voices.

*Hi Andy. Nice to see you. I love your wig.*

"Pass the potato chips," I said. *Squeak*.

The chips were weightless. They floated like Frisbees. We had to catch them in our mouths.

"I caught a Dorito," Bob said. *Crunch*.

"It's a banquet," I said.

At 9:30 PM, the carpet sky started to unravel. A loose thread. It hung down over 5th Avenue. A giant orange string. Someone pulled it. The whole sky started to come undone. *Zip*.

"Run," I said. "The set is collapsing."

We jumped into the cab. $8,000.00. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of Madonna. She was wearing a corset made of subway grates.

"Hang on," Madonna said. "We're going to express."

We drove through the unraveling sky. Threads of orange and blue whipped against the windows. *Thwack, thwack*.
