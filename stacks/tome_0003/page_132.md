Woke up at 8:30 AM. The sky is mesh today. Metal mesh. Chain-link fence fabric. It’s gray. The sun is stuck in one of the diamonds. It’s wobbling. Like a trapped bird. Or a balloon. The air smells like ozone and pretzels. I looked out the window and the sky was unraveling. A loose thread was hanging down from a cloud. I wanted to pull it. I wanted to see what would happen if the sky came apart. Would there be another sky underneath? Or just wood paneling? $0.00.

Put on the "Chicken Wire" wig. It’s very industrial. Very structured. My hair is in a grid. I put a pigeon in the wig. He sat on the top of my head. He cooed.

"You're heavy," I said.

"I'm an accessory," the pigeon said. "It's Fashion Week."

Cabbed to the Factory at 9:15 AM. $2,000.00. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of Truman Capote. He was tiny. Only six inches tall. He was standing on the seat to reach the pedals.

"The traffic is abysmal," Truman squeaked. "Simply abysmal. It’s a conspiracy."

When we got to the Factory, the building was a sieve. A giant colander. Water was pouring through the walls. *Drip, drip, splash*.

"We're leaking," Bob said. "The ideas are getting out."

"Or the rain is getting in," I said.

We went inside. The floor was wet. The water was rising. It was up to our ankles.

"It's filling up," Jean-Michel said. "We should stock fish."

At 10:30 AM, the rat arrived. He was wearing scuba gear. Tiny little flippers. A tiny little tank.

"I'm exploring the depths," the rat said. He dove into the water. *Splash*. He swam past my leg. "The visibility is poor."

The squirrel was there. He was in a boat. A tiny rowboat made of a walnut shell.

"Stroke," the squirrel said. He was rowing in circles. "We're going nowhere."

At 11:45 AM, I had a meeting with the "Time Brokers". They were selling yesterday.

"We have a bulk lot of Tuesdays," the Broker said. He was wearing a watch on every finger. "Slightly used. Good weather."

"I'll take three hours of last October," I said. "I liked the light."

"$5,000.00 per hour," the Broker said. "Plus tax."

"I'll pay," I said. "Put it on my tab."

I bought the time. They put it in a jar. It was a blue mist. I put the jar on the shelf.

"It's beautiful," Sammy said. "It looks like smoke."

"It's memories," I said. "Expired."

At 1:00 PM, we went to lunch. We went to "The Odeon". The restaurant was sinking. Slowly.

"I'll have the 'Salade Niçoise'," I said. "$4,500.00."

"The kitchen is flooded," the waiter said. He was wearing waders. "We only have tuna. Canned."

"Perfect," I said. "I'll take the can."

I ate the tuna with my fingers. The water in the restaurant was up to my waist.

"It's refreshing," I said.

"My pants are wet," Jean-Michel said. "This fabric shrinks."

"It's performance art," I said.

At 2:30 PM, I worked on the "Portrait" paintings. I was painting a picture of a face. But the face kept changing. Every time I looked at it, it was someone else.

"Is that me?" Bob asked.

"No, it's you," I said to Sammy.

"Now it's me," the pigeon said.

"It's everyone," I said. "It's a crowd."

I painted a mustache on the face. Then glasses. Then a wig.

"It's a disguise," I said. "In plain sight."

At 4:00 PM, the Man in the Grey Suit came in. He was holding a net. A butterfly net.

"I'm catching the stray thoughts," the Man said. He swung the net through the air. *Swish*. "Got one."

"What did you catch?" I asked.

"A thought about dinner," the Man said. "And a fear of dying."

"Throw them back," I said. "I'm not hungry."

At 5:15 PM, the water drained away. *Gurgle, gurgle*. It went down the drain in the center of the floor. It made a whirlpool. Bob's shoe almost got sucked in.

"Grab it," I said.

We watched the water disappear. The floor was dry. Except for a pile of sludge. Grey sludge.

"It's the residue," I said. "The aftermath."

At 7:30 PM, we went to a party. It was on a roof. The roof of the Seagram Building. The wind was blowing. The sky-mesh was rattling. *Shing-a-ling*.

I saw Elon Musk. He was floating. He had helium balloons tied to his wrists.

"I'm lighter than air," Elon said. "I'm ascending."

"Don't let go," I said. "Space is cold."

"I'll take my chances," Elon said. He let go of a balloon. He drifted up. Higher and higher. Until he was just a dot in the mesh.

"Bye," I said. "Send a postcard."

At 9:00 PM, the rat tried to climb the mesh wall. He got stuck. His claws were caught in the links.

"I'm hammocked," the rat said. "I'm hanging art."

"You're a net," I said. "A safety net."

At 11:45 PM, I went home. The townhouse was quiet. The windows were open. The mesh sky was glowing. Moonlight filtering through the diamonds.

I went to the kitchen. I opened the Campbell’s Soup.

It was "Cream of Mushroom".

I opened the can. I poured it into a bowl.

The soup was full of holes. Like a sponge. There were gaps in the liquid. Little pockets of nothing.

"It's aerated," I thought.

I took a sip.

It tasted like air. And mushrooms. And metal.

"Efficient," I thought.

I went upstairs. I took off the wig. The pigeon flew out the window. *Flap, flap*.

I looked in the mirror. My face was mesh. I could see through my cheek to the wall behind.

"I'm transparent," I thought. "Finally."

I brushed my teeth. The toothbrush went right through my teeth. No resistance.

"Ghostly," I thought.
