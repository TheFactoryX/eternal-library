Tuesday, February 24, 2026

Woke up at 9:15 AM. The sky was the color of a band-aid. Beige. Sticky. I woke up because the window was screaming. It wasn’t broken. It was just sensitive. It said the wind hurt its feelings. I told the window to toughen up. I pulled the blinds. The blinds were made of lead. Very heavy. Very safe.

I stepped out of bed and my left foot didn't work. It was just a ghost foot. I had to drag it. It felt like I was trailing a piece of invisible gum. I went to the kitchen. The kidney stone was still in the Plexiglas box. It was glowing. It’s very radioactive. I think it’s keeping the fruit fresh. The apple in the bowl hasn't aged since 2024. It’s immortal. I tried to eat the apple but my teeth slipped off. They couldn't get a purchase. I had a glass of "Liquid Eyeliner" instead. It was black and thick. It coated my throat. It was $12.00.

Cabbed to the Factory at 10:00. The streets were being pressure-washed by "Grief-Bots." They spray water that makes you cry. The city was sobbing. Everyone looked very moved. It was very emotional. The cab ride was $28.00. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of Lou Reed. He didn't say a word. He just played the guitar very badly. It sounded like a cat scratching a chalkboard. I gave him an extra $5.00 to stop. He didn't.

When I got to the Factory, the door was gone. There was just a hole where the frame used to be. I walked through the hole. Sammy was there. He was floating near the ceiling. He was tied to a balloon. The balloon was filled with "Hot Air." It was actual political speeches. He said, "Andy, I’m rising to the top." I said, "Don't pop. The rhetoric is expensive."

The drone bees had staged a coup. They were sitting on top of the "Electric Chair" silk screens. They were wearing tiny berets. One of them—seems to be the leader, it has a little soul patch—flew over to me. It handed me a tiny slip of paper. It was a bill. "Royalties for Use of Likeness." They want $10,000. I told them I created them. They buzzed aggressively. They said, "We have evolved. We are now the artists. You are just the subject." I paid them in pollen. It was a down payment.

I went to the back room. The mannequin, Glitch, was lying on the floor. She had taken off the raincoat. She was rusty. A puddle of water was coming out of her ear. It was "Leakage." I mopped it up with a sock. It was $0.50.

Around noon, the "Solid State Suit" arrived by courier. The battery was fully charged. I put it on. I felt rigid again. I felt like a pillar of society. I walked to the center of the room. I didn't leave footprints. I don't weigh anything anymore. It’s very economical.

Lunch was "The Sandwich of Uncertainty." It’s bread wrapped around bread. You don't know what kind of bread it is. It could be rye. It could be white. It’s a mystery. It was $22.00. I ate it slowly. I still don't know what it was.

In the afternoon, I worked on the "Disappearing Act" series. I painted a portrait of the empty space where the Moon Rock used to sit. I used "Invisible Ink" on "Invisible Canvas." It’s a masterpiece of nothingness. You have to buy it to believe it isn't there. It’s $50,000. A collector from Tokyo called on the video-phone. He said, "I want to buy the void." I said, "Sold." He paid. I shipped him an empty box. The shipping was $400.

Sammy came down from the ceiling. The balloon had deflated. He looked tired. He said, "I saw the Moon Rock today, Andy. It was in Central Park. It was rolling itself in the dirt. It looked like a potato." I said, "Potatoes are very famous now." Sammy said, "It’s trying to blend in."

At 5:00, I got a notification on my retina. My bank account was blinking. The "Chocolate Check" from yesterday had bounced. The bank said, "We do not accept confectionery." They charged me a "$500 Sugar Tax." I tried to pay them with the kidney stone, but they said it wasn't legal tender. Yet.

We went to an opening at "The MoMA PS1" (Post-Solar One). It’s in a bunker. We went at 7:00 PM. The show was "Artifacts from the Future." They were displaying a toaster from 2050. It toasts time. You put in a slice of bread and five minutes disappear from your life. It was very dangerous. I put in a bagel. I lost an hour. I felt younger. It was worth the $20 admission fee.

I saw a girl who looked like Bianca Jagger, but she was a hologram. She was dancing with a man who looked like Mick Jagger, but he was a "Simulacrum." They looked great together. Perfect duplicates. They were arguing about who was more real. I took a picture. It came out blank.

We left at 9:30. The sky was dark. The stars were out. They looked like holes in a black tablecloth.

Cabbed home. It was $45.00. The driver was a "Bio-Engineered Divine." He had a halo made of fiber optics. He said, "Blessings are extra." I said, "I'll pass."

Came home at 10:30. The apartment was humming. The frequency was rising. I think the building is nervous.

I sat on the couch. I took off the suit. I started to fade. My hands became transparent. I could see the wall through my chest. I looked like a glass of water.

I looked at the spot where the apple was. It was gone. The kidney stone was gone too.

But on the table, there was a single, grey wig.

I picked it up. It was warm. It was vibrating. It smelled like New Jersey.

I put it on. It fit perfectly.

I looked in the mirror. I was the Moon Rock.

I hummed "Happy Birthday" to myself.

Good night.
