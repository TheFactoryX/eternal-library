Friday, March 06, 2026

Woke up at 8:45 AM. The sky was the color of a bruised peach. Purple and yellow and sort of sad. I tried to get out of bed, but I couldn't. I was hovering. About two inches off the mattress. The "Moon Rock" was vibrating so hard it was lifting me up. I felt like a balloon. I had to grab the bedpost to pull myself down. I put on my jeans. They were the "Acid Wash" ones, but they were actually washed with acid. They have holes in the knees. Very breezy.

Cabbed to the Factory at 9:30. The streets were wet. It rained "Digital Rain" last night. Green code dripping from the clouds. The gutters were full of pixels. The cab ride was $45.00. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of Truman Capote. He was very small. He was sitting on phone books. He said, "Andy, darling, that's a wonderful wig. Is it a bird?" I said, "No, it's a secret." He said, "I'll write it in my tablet." He typed on his dashboard. The meter made a *ding* sound every time he gossiped.

When I got to the Factory, the tiny door was back. But today it was on the ceiling. I had to take the elevator to the roof and then climb down a ladder. I scraped my elbow. It leaked silver fluid. I must be a machine now. Or maybe a cyborg. I don't know.

Inside, the "Hole in the Wall" was covered with glass. Sammy had put a velvet rope around it. He was charging admission. $50.00 a look. It was sold out. People love to look at nothing. It validates their own emptiness. I counted the cash. We made $2,000.00 before breakfast.

I went to the back to work on the "Electric Chair" series. I’m updating it. I painted a chair made of fiber optics. It lights up when you sit in it. I call it "The Hot Seat." It’s very comfortable. I sat in it for an hour. I felt warm. I felt judged. I liked it.

Around 11:00, the "Portrait of a Noun" buyer showed up. He was a "Simulacrum" of a young man, maybe 25, but he had a very old face. He looked like a tree. He said, "I have the island." He handed me a stick. A USB stick. He said, "It's all in there. The palm trees. The sand. The sun." I plugged it into my temple. *Click*. I saw the ocean. It was very blue. I put the stick in my pocket. I hope I don't format myself.

Lunch was a "Simulation of a Sandwich." It looked like a ham on rye, but it tasted like electricity. It was $25.00. I didn't eat it. I just licked the wrapper. It had a metallic aftertaste.

At 2:00 PM, the "MirrorWall" rang. It was "The Kid" again. This time, he was inside the screen. He was standing in my studio. He was looking at me. He said, "Andy, come out here. The water is fine." I touched the glass. It rippled. I pulled my hand back. I didn't want to get wet.

We went to an opening at "The Gagosian Moon-Base." We had to take a shuttle. It was a real spaceship. It smelled like bleach and money. The gravity was low. I had to wear weighted boots. $500.00 to rent them. My feet felt like lead. But I floated when I walked. It was very graceful. I felt like a dancer. Or a ghost.

The show was "Self-Portraits of People Who Don't Exist." It was very crowded. I saw a "Simulacrum" of Jackie O. She was wearing a helmet made of diamonds. She said, "Andy, do you remember Dallas?" I said, "I remember the lunch." She said, "It was a bad day for hats."

I had a "Sputnik Spritzer." It was $150.00. It had orbiting fruit. The cherries spun around my head. I tried to catch one with my mouth. I missed. I hit a "Simulacrum" of Mick Jagger. He spilled his drink. It wasn't a drink. It was motor oil. He lapped it up. He said, "It keeps the gears lubricated."

On the way back, the shuttle pilot was a "Simulacrum" of my mother. She was driving fast. She said, "Andrew, put on your seatbelt. Space is dangerous." I said, "I'm already dead, Julia." She said, "That's no excuse to be untidy."

Cabbed home. It was $60.00. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of me. But this time, he was wearing a suit made of screens. They were playing all my movies. *Sleep* was on his left lapel. *Empire* was on his right. He said, "Good work today, Andy." I said, "I didn't do anything." He said, "Exactly. You're a pro."

Came home at 11:30 PM. The apartment was spinning. Or maybe I was. I took off the weighted boots. My feet felt light. I floated up to the ceiling. I hit my head. *Bonk*.

I grabbed the bedpost and pulled myself down. I tucked the Moon Rock under the pillow. It was pulsing. *Thump-thump*. It sounded like a heart.

I lay down. I looked at my hand. It was fading again. I could see the wall through my skin. I turned off the light.

I watched the dark.

It was the best color I've seen all day.
