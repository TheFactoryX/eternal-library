Woke up at 8:00 AM. The silver paint from the ceiling had dried into a sheet. It was heavy. It was draped over the duvet like a lead blanket. I felt like I was sleeping inside a wrapped piece of candy. A very expensive, very heavy candy. I couldn't move my arms. I had to wiggle like a worm to get out. *Squirm*. I fell onto the floor. The floor was solid again. The house had fixed itself during the night. The gap was gone. The Smurfs were gone. It was just boring old wood. I felt lonely. $0.00.

Put on the "Chameleon" wig. It’s made of fiber-optics. It changes color to match the room. I walked into the kitchen. It turned white. I walked into the bedroom. It turned beige. I’m invisible. I’m a part of the furniture. It’s very good for avoiding people, but bad for getting served at brunch.

Cabbed to the Factory at 9:45 AM. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of Candy Darling. She was perfect. Too perfect. Her skin was porcelain. Her eyelashes were made of nylon.

"You look beautiful, Candy," I said.

"I'm a doll, Andy," she said. "I'm being sold at the gift shop."

"Maybe I'll buy you," I said. "Do you take American Express?"

"I only take compliments," she said. $12,000.00.

When we got to the Factory, the floor was gone. Completely gone. It was just a hole. A deep, dark hole. It went all the way down to the center of the earth, I think.

"It's the 'Void' collection," Bob said. He was hovering over the edge. "We're digging for the core."

"I hope it's hot," I said. "I'm cold."

We put on jetpacks. Little silver backpacks. We floated around the Factory. It’s like swimming in air. I bumped into a Campbell’s Soup can. It was floating too. *Bonk*.

"Gravity is optional today," Sammy said. He was doing somersaults in the corner.

At 11:00 AM, the rat floated by. He was wearing a tiny aviator hat and goggles.

"I'm an ace," the rat said. "I'm flying missions."

"Where are you going?" I asked.

"To the bottom," the rat said. "I heard there’s cheese down there. Magma cheese. It’s very sharp."

"Don't burn your whiskers," I said.

The squirrel was there. He was drilling into the wall with a jackhammer. *Vrrrmm*.

"I'm tunneling to China," the squirrel said. "Or maybe to the bathroom. I forget."

At 12:30 PM, Jean-Michel called. He was in the air somewhere. His voice was coming from the clouds.

"Andy?" he said. "I'm painting the sky. I'm using clouds. I'm moving them around with a giant fan."

"Is it abstract?" I asked.

"It's cumulus," he said. "I'm making a portrait of you. It’s going to rain on everybody."

"I love the rain," I said. "It washes the car."

At 1:00 PM, we went to lunch. We couldn't go out because we were hovering. We ordered from "KFC". They sent up a bucket of chicken by drone. The drone dropped it. *Splat*. The chicken pieces floated up. They were crispy.

"I'll take a wing," I said. I grabbed a drumstick out of the air. $35,000.00.

"It's fried," Bob said. "It's greasy."

"It's necessary," I said. I wiped my hands on a floating napkin.

At 3:00 PM, I worked on the "TV" paintings. I painted a television set. Then I turned it on. The screen was real. It was showing me painting the television.

"It's a feedback loop," Sammy said. "You're inside the TV."

"I'm always on TV," I said. "It’s just a matter of tuning in."

At 4:15 PM, the Man in the Grey Suit came in. He was wearing a parachute.

"We have a liability issue," the Man said. "The hole. If someone falls in, do we pay for the funeral?"

"We charge admission," I said. "It's a thrill ride."

"The government says it's a mine," the Man said. "They want royalties on the minerals."

"I'm the only mineral here," I said. "I'm a gem."

"Sign here," the Man said. He floated a contract over to me.

At 6:00 PM, we went to a party. It was at "The Plaza Hotel". But the hotel had been turned into a giant beehive. The walls were made of honeycomb. Yellow and sticky. Everyone was dressed like bees. Black and yellow stripes.

"It's the 'Hive' opening," the doorman said. He was a giant queen bee. *Buzz*.

"I'm allergic to work," I said.

We went inside. It was warm. The air smelled like flowers. I saw a girl named Diana. She was wearing a dress made of actual bees. They were crawling on her. They were her clothes.

"It's organic," she said.

"It's biting," I said.

"It’s texture," she said.

At 8:00 PM, they served dinner. It was a giant pot of honey. No spoons. We had to dip our faces in. *Slurp*.

"I'm sticky," Bob said. He was covered in gold. "I feel like a statue."

"You look expensive," I said. "Lick your lips."

"I can't move my lips," Bob said.

At 9:30 PM, the bees started to swarm. *Zzzzzzt*. They formed a giant shape in the air. It was a profile of me. My wig. My nose. My glasses. It was a living portrait.

"It's beautiful," I said. "Take a picture."

"I can't," Sammy said. "My camera is stuck in the honey."

Cabbed home at 11:00 PM. $60,000.00. The driver was a swarm of bees. They held the shape of a taxi. It was bumpy. *Buzz, bump*.

Went into the townhouse. Everything was normal. The silver paint was gone. The floor was there. It was too normal. I felt like I was in the wrong place.

I went to the bathroom. I looked in the mirror.

The mirror reflected the back of my head. I was looking at my own wig.

"Wrong way," I said.

I turned around. The mirror showed the front. But it wasn't me. It was a machine. A metal box with a screen.

"Time for bed," the machine said.

I turned off the light. $0.00.
