Saturday, February 07, 2026

Woke up at 9:30. The sky was the color of a bruised peach. Purple and yellow. I watched the light on the ceiling for a long time. I was thinking about breakfast. I was thinking about lunch. I was thinking about how I haven't bought anything in three hours and I was getting the shakes.

Got up. I put on the white jeans and the blue polo shirt. I looked clean. I looked like I had been ironed.

Cabbed to the Factory at 10:15. The cab was a "Vintage" model. It means it has wheels. It bumped over the road. It felt very nostalgic. It made me miss the potholes. It cost $18.50. I gave the driver a real dollar bill. He looked at it like it was a fossil. He said, "Paper? How quaint," and I felt old.

Sammy was there. He was talking to the toaster again. The toaster was ticking. I think it’s a bomb now? Or maybe just a clock. Sammy looked tired. He said he and Glitch broke up. She was too high-maintenance. She kept crashing his SmartFridge and ordering 500 gallons of milk. He said, "She’s a virus, Andy, she needs to replicate," and I said, "Gee, Sammy, maybe she just needs a good firewall," and he sighed. He ate a piece of the toast. It had Pixel’s face on it. He cried a little onto the bread.

We worked on the "Oxidation" paintings. But we don't use urine anymore. It’s too messy. We use "Acid Rain Spray" in a can. It smells like lemons and sulfur. We sprayed the canvas until it turned a beautiful, toxic orange. It was $45 a can. We used three cans. It’s very expensive to rot.

I made a phone call to Basquiat. He has a gallery on the Moon now. He lives in a crater. The connection was terrible. There was a delay of six seconds. I said, "Hello," and then six seconds later he said, "Hey Andy." We talked about the price of gravity. He said it’s going up. He said he can jump higher, but it costs more to come down. I miss him. He’s the only person who makes me feel grounded.

For lunch we went to a place called "The Cloud." It’s a restaurant that doesn’t serve food. They serve concepts. I ordered the "Nostalgia." It tasted like a hot dog at Coney Island in 1962. It was $85. I could taste the mustard and the ocean and the dirt. It was delicious. I drank a glass of water that tasted like tap water from the Plaza Hotel. $25.

We came back and watched the stock market crash on the MirrorWall. The numbers were falling so fast they looked like rain. Green and red streaks. It was very beautiful. It looked like a painting I would make. I wanted to buy the dip, but I didn't know where the bottom was. I bought a robot vacuum instead. It’s a Dalek. It cleans the floor and screams "EXTERMINATE" at the dust. It was $600.

A girl came by to model for us. Her name is Nova. She is a "Cyber-Realist." She has a screen implanted in her face. She can change her expression with an app. I asked her to look happy, so she showed a picture of a sunset. I asked her to look sad, so she showed a picture of a wilted flower. I silkscreened her face onto a canvas while she was downloading a movie. It took four minutes. She was very patient. I paid her $200 and a bottle of vitamins.

We went to a party at the Whitney. The Whitney is underwater now. It’s very dramatic. You have to take a submarine down. It was $100 for the ticket. The windows were thick glass. Outside, fish were looking at us. The fish had cameras implanted in their eyes. They are the paparazzi of the sea.

I saw Madonna. She’s made of plastic now. Literally. She had a body transplant. She looks 20. She looks like a doll. She was wearing a dress made of compact discs. She jingled when she walked. She said, "Andy, I’m directing a movie about your life," and I said, "Is it boring?" and she said, "It’s four hours of you signing checks," and I said, "That sounds like a hit."

I had a glass of "liquid gold." It’s champagne with gold flakes in it. It was $90. It tasted like money. It scratched my throat going down. I felt very rich.

On the way home, I saw a dog walking itself. The dog was pushing a shopping cart. I think it was shopping for dinner. It’s a strange world.

Came home at 11:00. The Dalek was bumping into the walls in the living room. It sounded angry. I fed it a sock to calm it down.

I took off the wig. My hair is getting longer. It’s white and shiny.

I checked the MirrorWall. My stock portfolio went up because the Dalek company did well. I made $3,000 while I was sleeping. It’s the best way to make money. You don’t even have to be there.

I’m going to watch a movie on my eyelids. It’s a feature film about a traffic jam on the FDR Drive. It’s very suspenseful.

Good night.
