Sunday, February 15, 2026

Woke up at 9:00 AM. I don’t know why I woke up so late. I felt heavy. Like I was filled with lead. Or maybe just too much pink champagne. The sky was the color of a bruised peach. It was raining, but the rain was warm. It felt like spit. Very unpleasant. I watched the MirrorWall for a minute. It was playing a rerun of yesterday. It showed me sleeping. I looked so peaceful. It’s a lie. I was dreaming about falling.

Got up and stepped on the scale. It didn’t say anything. It just flashed a sad face emoji. Then it asked for a subscription. I ignored it. I’m not paying a scale rent. I went to the kitchen. The hearts from the cereal yesterday had dried to the bowl. It looked like a crime scene. I scraped it out. I had a protein shake instead. It was "Gunmetal Grey" flavor. It tasted like liquid batteries. It was $18. It gave me a headache immediately.

Cabbed to the Factory at 10:30. The cab driver was a "Simulacrum." It was a robot modeled after a 1950s dad. He called me "sport." He drove very slowly. He said, "Look at the weather, sport. Terrible." He was right. It was $20.00. I tipped him $5. He beeped "Thanks, sport."

Sammy was at the Factory. He looked tired. He looked like he hadn’t slept since the mannequin started walking backwards. He was sitting on the couch. The mannequin was sitting next to him. She was wearing my wig. The one I took off last night. I don’t know how she got it. I don’t remember giving it to her. She looked better in it than I do. She looked like a younger, plastic me. Sammy said, "Andy, she’s watching the Super Bowl." I looked at the wall. It was just a blank patch of plaster. He said, "She’s projecting it in her mind. She says the commercials are better in the reflection."

We worked on the "Disaster" paintings. We are doing a series called "Electric Jesus." It’s Jesus struck by lightning. Over and over again. We used "Tesla Coil" blue paint. It was $200 a jar. It crackles when you touch it. I got a shock. It hurt my finger. It felt like a blessing. Or maybe just electricity. It’s hard to tell the difference.

A man came in from the "New Balthazar." He said I left my phone there yesterday. But I didn’t have a phone. I had a hologram projector. He gave it to me. It was warm. It was vibrating. He said, "You had a call. From Brazil." I didn't take it. I don’t know anyone in Brazil. Maybe it was the ghost of bossa nova.

For lunch we ordered from "The Robot Chef." It’s a machine that synthesizes food based on your mood. I keyed in "Melancholy." It made a grilled cheese sandwich. But the bread was black. It was charcoal bread. It was $25. It tasted like ashes. It was perfect. Sammy keyed in "In Love." It made him a soup that was bright pink. It smelled like roses. He drank it and cried. He said, "It tastes like her." He looked at the mannequin. She was still watching the invisible game. She did the wave. It was very creepy.

I worked on the portrait of Taylor Swift. She asked me to make her "timeless." So I painted her without a face. Just a blank oval. It’s very Pop. It’s very Zen. I think she’ll hate it. I can sell it for $500,000.

The weather outside got worse. The warm rain turned to steam. The street was invisible. It looked like we were floating in a cloud. A dirigible flew by. It had an advertisement for "Retirement on Mars." "Go Before You Die." I thought about it. I wonder if they need wigs on Mars.

Left at 6:00. The elevator was broken. We had to take the stairs. My knees hurt. Gravity is so expensive these days.

Came home and the apartment was quiet. Too quiet. The Dalek was in the corner, plugged into the wall. It was recharging. The moon rock was sitting on the table. It wasn't vibrating anymore. It was just a rock. It looked very lonely. I put the wig on it. Now it looks like a hairy rock. It looks like a celebrity.

I checked the banking app. The invisible ink check from yesterday was still there. It said "Pending." It’s been pending for forever.

I turned on the MirrorWall. I waved at myself. I didn't wave back.

Good night.
