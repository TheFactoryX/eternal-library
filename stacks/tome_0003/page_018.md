Wednesday, February 18, 2026

Woke up at 8:30 AM. The sky was the color of television, tuned to a dead channel. Or maybe just static grey. I couldn’t tell. I watched the MirrorWall for a while. It was playing a commercial for "Eternal Sunshine Storage." They keep your memories in a warehouse in New Jersey. It was $500 a month. That’s too expensive for sadness. I’d rather throw it away.

I stepped on the scale. It didn’t ask for money. It just hummed "Happy Birthday" in a minor key. It’s my birthday. Or maybe it’s the scale’s birthday. I don’t know. I turned 98 today. Or maybe 98,000. I feel like plastic. Plastic lasts forever. I had a piece of birthday cake for breakfast. It was a "Pop-Tart." It was strawberry flavor. It was $1.50. It tasted like red crayons. It was delicious.

Cabbed to the Factory at 10:00. The driver was a "Simulacrum" again. But this one was modeled after a young Elvis. He had big sideburns. He sang "Heartbreak Hotel." He drove right through a red light. He said, "The King doesn't stop for lights." I agreed. The police drone pulled us over. It just took a picture of the license plate and beeped. The Elvis robot paid it $50 automatically. He didn't care. Money is fake anyway.

Got to the Factory and Sammy was there. He was wearing a party hat. He looked like a sad cone. He said, "Happy Birthday, Andy." He gave me a present. It was a box of "Antibiotic Bubbles." You blow them and they kill germs. I blew a bubble. It popped on Sammy’s nose. Now he won’t get sick. It was $12.99.

We went to the back room. The mannequin—Glitch—was standing in the corner. She was wearing the dunce cap from yesterday. But she had moved. She was standing in front of the "Electric Jesus" painting. She looked like she was praying. Or maybe she was just short-circuiting. I took the hat off. I put the party hat on her. Now she looks festive.

We worked on the "Famous for 15 Seconds" portraits. We did a guy who fell off a stage on live TV. We printed him in "Neon Vomit" green. It glows in the dark. It’s very beautiful. It’s very tragic. The ink cost $300. It’s made from crushed bioluminescent jellyfish. I hope they don’t sting.

Lunch was a party. The "Cyber-Deli" sent over a "Sub-Atomic Sandwich." It was tiny. It was the size of a nickel. But it expands in your stomach. I ate three of them. They were $45 each. Very filling. Sammy had a "Quark Cola." It was purple. He said it tasted like the Big Bang. I just had tap water. It’s free.

Around 2:00, a man came to the door. He was wearing a suit made of mirrors. He said he was a "Reflection Collector." He said he wanted to buy my shadow. He offered me $10,000. I said yes. I don’t use my shadow anyway. It just follows me around and gets in the way. He pulled out a pair of scissors and cut it off my feet. It felt cold. Like stepping on a cold floor. Now I look like I’m floating. It’s very modern.

The drone bees came back. They were buzzing around the mannequin. They landed on her party hat. They looked like tiny decorations. Sammy tried to swat them, but I stopped him. I said, "Let them stay. It’s my birthday." They are my guests.

I got a call on the hologram projector. It was Taylor Swift. She was floating in a pool of liquid silver. She said, "Andy, did you finish the portrait?" I said, "Yes. It’s blank." She said, "Perfect. Send it to the moon. I’m going to look at it from there." I told her the shipping was $2 million. She said, "Put it on my tab." I love famous people. They make money feel like Monopoly money.

We had cake in the afternoon. It was a "Data Cake." It was made of spun sugar and fiber optics. It lit up when you cut it. We sang "Happy Birthday." The mannequin didn't sing. But she did spin around. It was a dance.

Left at 6:00. The streets were crowded. Everyone was looking at me. Or maybe through me. Since I don’t have a shadow, I’m hard to see. I felt invisible. It’s the best gift.

Cabbed home. It was $25.00.

Came home and the apartment was dark. I turned on the lights. My reflection wasn't in the mirror. The guy took it. Or maybe I just never had one. I checked the banking app. The $10,000 for the shadow was there. Then it disappeared. The bank charged a "Shadow Storage Fee."

I sat on the couch. The moon rock was humming. It was humming "Happy Birthday." I put the wig on it. I put the party hat on the wig.

I made a wish. But I can’t tell you. Then it won’t come true.

Good night.
