Friday, March 13, 2026

Woke up at 7:00 AM. The room was spinning. Not the room. The jar. The feather was spinning so fast it was drilling a hole in the glass. I threw it in the trash. I didn't want to deal with the noise. *Whirr-whirr*. It sounded like a dentist's drill.

I looked in the mirror. My face was peeling. Just a little bit, on the left cheek. It looked like dried glue. I pulled a piece off. Underneath, it wasn't skin. It was silver foil. Shiny. Crinkly. I panicked. I put a Band-Aid on it. A big one. A "flesh-tone" one. But they don't make my flesh tone anymore. They make "Beige #4." I looked like a patchwork quilt. I put on a wig. A new one. "The Bob." It was $600.00. It’s synthetic hair. Very shiny. Like plastic.

Breakfast was silence. I ate a "Pop-Tart." They were $25.00 a box. I toasted it. It burned. It smelled like burning sugar. It smelled like the 70s.

Cabbed to the Factory at 9:00 AM. It was raining. But the rain was green. Radioactive rain? Or just St. Patrick’s Day early? The cab ride was $65.00. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of Mick Jagger. He had big lips. He was dancing in the seat. He said, "Andy, start me up." I said, "You're already started." He drove through a red light. *Screech*.

The Factory was quiet. Too quiet. Usually, the "Superstars" are sleeping on the couch. But the couches were empty. The air smelled like ozone. The "Car Crash" paintings were vibrating. I touched one. It was warm. Like a fever.

Sammy came out of the back office. He was holding a fishbowl. Inside the bowl, there was no water. Just data. Floating numbers. "Where is the bird?" I asked. Sammy pointed to the fishbowl. "It evolved," he said. "It turned into code. It’s swimming in the blockchain now." He tapped the glass. The numbers formed a smiley face. :) It cost $4,000.00 to feed it this morning. We bought "Server Space." It’s expensive to keep a pet in the cloud.

I went to the studio to work. I started a new painting. "Electric Chair (In Color)." But I couldn't find the red paint. The tubes were empty. Deflated. Like used balloons. I used ketchup instead. Heinz. $8.00 a bottle. It smelled like fries. I squeezed it onto the canvas. It made a *splat* sound. Very violent. I liked it. It’s good to reuse materials. It’s very American.

Around 11:00 AM, the phone rang. It was a "Simulacrum" of Basquiat. He was glowing. He said, "Andy, I'm writing on the moon." He said, "I ran out of room in New York." I said, "Is the rent cheaper?" He said, "It's free. But the air is expensive." He sent me a photo of a crater. It was tagged with a crown.

Lunch was at "Serendipity 3." It’s a bunker now. They serve fear and frozen hot chocolate. I had the hot chocolate. It was $150.00. It was cold. It was "Cold Hot Chocolate." It was contradictory. It tasted like brownie batter and nostalgia. I sat with a "Simulacrum" of Candy Darling. She was wearing a dress made of CRT monitors. They were flickering. She said, "Andy, do I look real?" I said, "You look better than real. You look high-definition." She smiled. Her teeth were pixels. *Click-click*.

I bought a souvenir. A "Frozen Hot Chocolate" spoon. It was silver. I stole it. I didn't steal it. I put it in my pocket. It was $5.00. I’ll add it to the tab.

We went to an opening at "MoMA." But the museum was gone. It was just a frame. A giant frame in the middle of the street. You looked through it, and that was the art. The traffic. The bums. The billboards. It was a readymade. Duchamp would have loved it. I stood in front of it. People took pictures of me looking at the picture. It was meta.

I saw a "Simulacrum" of Lou Reed. He was leaning against the frame. He was wearing sunglasses. He said, "Andy, take a walk on the wild side." He pointed to the curb. There was a lion eating a parking meter. I said, "Is that the wild side?" He said, "That's the gentrified side. The wild side is in Queens."

I took a cab to Queens at 4:00 PM. I wanted to see the wild side. The bridge was crowded with people walking backwards. Returning to nature. Or Brooklyn. The cab ride was $120.00. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of a Troll. Under the bridge. He had green hair. He said, "None shall pass." I gave him $50.00. He passed.

Queens was blurry. Everything was moving fast. The birds were supersonic. *Whoosh*. I couldn't focus. My eyes were tired. The Rose-Colored Contacts were dry. I took them out. The world looked grey again. Sharp and grey.

I bought a hot dog from a robot. It was $40.00. The robot had a little hat. It said, "Enjoy your processed meat." I ate it. It was gritty. It tasted like pennies.

I came home at 6:00 PM. I was tired. I felt heavy. The gravity was increasing. I could feel it in my knees.

I went to the safe to check on the feather. It was gone. The trash can was empty. The jar was gone.

I sat on the bed. I took off the wig. My head was cold. I touched the Band-Aid on my face. It was crinkly. I peeled it off.

The mirror was gone. In its place was a screen. A video screen.

I looked at the screen. It was me. But it wasn't me. It was a "Simulacrum" of me. Sitting on a bed. Peeling a Band-Aid.

I waved. The screen-me didn't wave.

The screen-me looked at the camera. It smiled. It said, "So long, Andy."

Then the screen went black.

I looked around the room. The walls were flat. The texture was gone. The paint was drying
