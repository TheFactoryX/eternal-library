"It's breezy," Marilyn said. "The air conditioning is on high."

"You're transparent," I said. "I can see the seatbelt through your stomach."

"It's the 'Static' collection," Bob said. He was wearing sunglasses. "We're cancelling the signal."

"I can't hear myself think," I said.

"That's the point," Sammy said. "Thinking is overrated."

At 10:45 AM, the rat walked in. He was wearing a tiny hazmat suit.

"Is it radioactive?" I asked.

"No," the rat said. "It's just dead air. The channel went off the air."

The squirrel was digging in the static. *Burrow*. He was looking for a signal.

"I'm trying to find 'The Love Boat'," the squirrel said. "I think it's on channel 3."
