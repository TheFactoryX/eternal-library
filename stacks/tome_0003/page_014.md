Saturday, February 14, 2026

Woke up at 10:00. The sky was the color of pink cotton candy that’s been dropped on the floor. Dirty and sweet. I watched the MirrorWall. It was playing a countdown. "24 Hours of Love." It’s very stressful. I turned it off. I watched the blank screen for a while. It was very peaceful. Just me and the black glass. Like looking into a very deep purse.

Got up and stepped on the scale. It was playing a love song. It beeped and said, "You are priceless." It didn’t give me a number. It’s broken, or maybe it’s in love with me. I had a bowl of "Heart-Flakes." They’re cereal shaped like little hearts. They turn red when you add milk. It looked like a bowl of blood. It was very festive. It was $12. I ate it fast. I didn’t want to hear the hearts screaming.

Cabbed to the Factory at 11:00. The streets were crowded. Everyone was holding bouquets of roses. But the roses were plastic. They light up. People were giving each other batteries. That’s the new romance. "I’d be lost without you." It’s very practical. The cab was $25.00. I gave the driver an extra $5 because he didn't play the radio.

Sammy was there. He was taping a bouquet to the mannequin’s hand. The mannequin—Glitch—was facing the corner again. He said, "I got her flowers. She just shattered the reflection." I looked at the floor. The glass was glittering. I said, "She’s breaking your heart, Sammy. And the floor." He swept up the glass. He looked sad.

We worked on the "Hearts" paintings. We are doing them in "Velvet Red." It’s paint made from crushed rubies. It cost $4,000 for a bucket. It’s very heavy. We smeared it on the canvas. It looked like a wound. It looked like a kiss. I painted a heart that was bleeding money. It’s very appropriate for the holiday. I sold one immediately. A man bought it for his wife. He said, "She loves red." He paid $50,000. I think he loves money more than his wife.

A drone flew in the window. It was a "Cupid Drone." It shot a foam arrow at me. It hit me in the wig. It had a note attached. It said, "Be Mine." It was from the Time Magazine people. They want to put me on the cover again. "The Eternal Bachelor." I don’t know. Being eternal is so much paperwork.

For lunch we ordered in. Pizza. But it was heart-shaped. It had pepperoni arranged like a smiley face. It was $35. I took a bite and the smile broke. It was tragic. I ate the eyes. They were spicy.

Sammy put the mannequin in a chair. He sat opposite her. They had a date. He ate his pizza. She just sat there. He said, "Andy, she’s the best listener." I said, "That’s because she has no ears, Sammy. You’re talking to a plastic ghost." He looked at her. He said, "Yeah, but she understands."

I worked on the film. "Empire." But we are doing "Empire State Building" in real-time. Just the building. I filmed the smoke coming out of the chimneys. It looked like white ribbons. It was very boring. I loved it.

A package came. It was from the "Andy" currency people. The ones who fired me. They sent a Valentine. It was a check for $1 million. It was signed "Sorry." The ink was disappearing as I held it. It was "Invisible Ink." It vanished completely. So it was a check for nothing. It’s the most romantic gift I’ve ever received.

Left at 6:00. The sun was setting. It looked like a sliced orange. Very juicy.

Came home and the moon rock was still vibrating. It was shaking the table. The Dalek was shooting it with his plunger. I think they’re playing tag.

I checked the MirrorWall. The "Me" from the morning was gone. Just static.

I took the wig off. My hair is grey. I put the wig on the mannequin’s head. She looked like me. She looked better than me.

I had a glass of champagne. It was $0.00. I found it in the back of the fridge. It was flat. It tasted like time.

Good night.
