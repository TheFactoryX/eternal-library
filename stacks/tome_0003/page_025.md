Wednesday, February 25, 2026

Woke up at 6:45 AM. The sky was the color of a Campbell's soup can just before you open it. Steel blue. Hopefully not containing any sodium benzoate. I didn't want to get up. The bed felt like a giant envelope and I was the letter inside. I tried to sit up, but I rolled right off the mattress and floated to the ceiling. I bumped my head. It made a hollow sound, like a coconut. I’m losing my density. I think I’m becoming a thought. And thoughts are free, which is depressing. I need to add some weight.

I crawled to the closet. I put on the "Gravity Boots." They are lead-lined boots. They cost $800 at the "Army Surplus Store for Ghosts." They make a loud *CLUNK CLUNK* sound when I walk. It’s very rhythmic. It’s like being a metronome. I feel heavy. Important. Like a sculpture that shouldn't be touched.

I looked in the mirror. The grey wig was still on my head from last night. It has fused to my skull. Or maybe my skull has fused to it. I tried to pull it, but it hurt. So I left it. It gives me a rough texture. I like it. I look like a rock star. Or just a rock.

Cabbed to the Factory at 8:00. The streets were quiet. Too quiet. Everyone was asleep in their "Meta-Pods." They were dreaming of advertising. The cab ride was $25.00. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of Bob Dylan. He wouldn't stop rhyming. He said, "The meter is running and the fare is a fare is a fare is a fare." I gave him $30 and told him to spare me the poetry.

When I got to the Factory, the building looked soft. The edges were blurry, like a watercolor painting left in the rain. I went to push the door, but my hand went right through the handle. I had to ring the bell with my mind. It buzzed.

Sammy was there. He was sitting on the floor, counting specks of dust. He looked very focused. He was wearing glasses that made his eyes look like dinner plates. He said, "Andy, I’m cataloging the universe." I said, "Start with the petty cash." He said, "I can't find the ledger. It’s invisible."

I walked across the room—*CLUNK CLUNK*. The floor felt like spongy cake. The "Electric Jesus" paintings were sweating. They were leaking "Halo-Glow" all over the floor. It was glowing green. I mopped it up with a sponge. It was $150 worth of varnish. I squeezed the sponge into a jar. I’m going to sell it as "Holy Water." $500 a pop. It’s a miracle.

The drone bees were lined up in a row. They were vibrating. They looked like a zipper. I asked Sammy what they were doing. He said, "They are waiting for the download." I said, "What download?" He said, "The update. They are going to become 'Wasps.'" I hope they don't sting. I don't have blood, but I have a reputation.

Around 11:00, a courier arrived. He was a "Bio-Printer." He just walked up to the wall and printed a package. It was a box of "Synthetic Wig Dye." It was "Charcoal Grey." It was $75.00. I didn't order it. Then I realized, it was a gift. From the Moon Rock. It wants me to stay grey. It’s very supportive. I opened the box and sniffed it. It smelled like outer space. Cold and metallic.

Lunch was delivered by a "Pneumatic Tube." It shot out of the wall and landed in my lap. It was a hot dog. But it wasn't a hot dog. It was a tube of "Meat-Paste." It was $12.00. I squeezed it into my mouth. It tasted like nostalgia. And mustard. I miss mustard. The real kind. The yellow kind.

In the afternoon, I worked on the "Disappearing Act" series. I painted a portrait of the cab fare from this morning. $25.00. But I painted it on "Dissolving Paper." So by the time I finished, the painting was gone. It was perfect. It was the most expensive piece of nothing I’ve ever made. I put the frame on the wall. Empty frames are very chic right now.

I got a call on the MirrorWall. It was a blur. It sounded like the ocean. Then it cleared up. It was a cat. A cat sitting on a washing machine. The cat said, "Is this Andrew?" I said, "Yes." The cat said, "The rock says hi." Then the cat licked the screen and hung up. I think the rock is possessing pets now. It’s very ambitious.

At 5:00, the "Solid State Suit" started to beep. It was hungry. It needs electricity. I plugged myself into the wall socket. I felt a zap. It was delicious. It was $0.05 worth of kilowatts. I sat there buzzing for an hour. I felt like a lamp. A very expensive lamp.

We went to a dinner at "The Electric Circus." It’s a circus where the elephants are made of neon. They float. We sat at a table made of ice. It was very cold. My wig froze. It was stiff. It looked like a helmet. I ordered "Ice Cubes." They were $40.00. They were just frozen water. But they were square. Very geometric.

I sat next to a woman who looked like Candy Darling, but she was a "Hologram." She flickered a lot. She said, "Andy, you look so solid." I said, "I'm full of electricity." She said, "I wish I could hold you." I said, "You can't. You'd get a shock."

We left at 10:00. The air outside was smoggy. It smelled like ozone.

Cabbed home. It was $55.00. The driver was a "Robot." It didn't have a face. Just a screen with a smiley face on it. : ) It didn't say a word. It just played the sound of ticking clocks.

I came home and took off the boots. My feet floated up immediately. I’m tethered to the earth only by my shoes.

I looked at the table. The kidney stone was back. And the apple. And the wig dye.

They were having a meeting.

I sat in the chair and watched them.

I didn't feel lonely.

I felt like a collection.

Good night.
