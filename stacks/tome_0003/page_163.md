Woke up at 7:30 AM. The alarm didn't go off. It just screamed. Once. A loud scream that woke me up instantly. I think it’s a new feature. "Violence Alarm". Very effective. I got up and stepped on a shoe. It wasn't my shoe. It was a loafer made of glass. It crunched. *Crunch*. I hope it wasn't expensive. I hope it wasn't mine.

Put on the "Disco" wig. It’s made of tiny mirrors. I walked into the bathroom and the whole room lit up. *Disco-disco*. It’s like having a thousand eyes looking at you while you brush your teeth. It’s very vain. I like it.

Cabbed to the Factory at 8:45 AM. $75,000.00. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of Truman Capote. He was very small. He had to sit on a phone book to see over the wheel.

"All literature is a degree of bullying," Truman said. "And this traffic is bullying me."

"You're doing great," I said. "Don't let them push you around."

When we got to the Factory, the floor was gone. The office was floating in the middle of the room. Just a desk and a chair, hovering in the air. Like a magic trick.

"It’s 'Levitation Day'," Bob said. He was floating near the ceiling, stuck to a light fixture. "We're trying to rise above it all. Literally."

"How do I get to work?" I asked.

"Jump," Sammy said. "Just believe."

I jumped. I stayed in the air. *Whoosh*. I grabbed the edge of the desk. My feet were dangling. It’s very hard to type when you're floating. The keys kept floating away.

At 10:00 AM, the phone rang. It was ringing in mid-air. A hologram of a phone. I picked it up.

It was Jean-Michel. He was a crown. Just a gold crown, floating on a pillow.

"Andy," the crown said. "I'm just a symbol now. I don't have a head. It’s very liberating."

"Symbols are heavy," I said. "They carry a lot of weight."

"I'm going to Mount Rushmore," the crown said. "I want to see the presidents. They look like they need jewelry."

At 11:30 AM, I tried to paint. But the paint wouldn't stay on the canvas. It floated off. *Glob-glob*. Balls of color drifting around the room.

"It’s Zero-Gravity Art," Sammy said. "It’s about letting go."

"I can't let go," I said. "I have to sell it. How do I sell a cloud?"

"You put it in a bag," Sammy said.

I caught a glob of red paint. I put it in a Ziploc bag. "It’s 'Captured Emotion'," I said. "$5,000,000.00."

At 1:00 PM, we went to lunch. We went to "21". But it was an iceberg. The restaurant was a block of ice. We sat on ice cubes.

I ordered the "Burger". $300,000.00.

It came frozen. Solid rock. I had to lick it like a lollipop.

"It keeps longer this way," the waiter said. He was a penguin. "Freshness is next to godliness."

Taylor Swift was there. She was sitting in a block of ice. Like a Disney princess. She was typing on a keyboard made of icicles.

"I'm freezing time," Taylor said. "I'm preserving the moment."

"Aren't you cold?" I asked.

"I'm chilling," she said. *Click-click*.

At 3:15 PM, the squirrel came in. He was wearing a jetpack.

"I'm a flying squirrel," the squirrel said. *Vroom*. He flew into the wall. *Bonk*.

"Gravity is a drag," the squirrel said. He shook his head.

At 5:00 PM, a collector came to buy the "Captured Emotion" bag. It was a woman made of crystal. She was transparent. I could see her lunch inside her.

"I'll take it," the woman said. "I'm feeling red today."

She paid me in diamonds. Little chips of diamond.

"Is this enough?" I asked.

"It’s plenty," she said. "I'm rich. I'm made of money. Literally."

At 6:45 PM, the Man in the Grey Suit drifted in. He was helium. He was a balloon. A grey balloon.

"I'm full of hot air," the balloon said. His voice was high and squeaky. "Take me to the party."

"We can't tie you to a chair," I said. "You'll float away."

"Then hold my string," the balloon said.

At 8:00 PM, we went to a party. It was at "The Roof of the World". It was a platform suspended above the city. No walls. Just floor. Below us, New York was a grid of lights. *Sparkle*.

"Look at that," I said. "It looks like a circuit board."

"It’s just lights," Sammy said. "Electricity paying rent."

I held the string of the Man in the Grey Suit balloon. He tugged in the wind. *Tug-tug*.

"Don't let go," the balloon said. "I'm afraid of the stratosphere."

"I won't," I said.

At 10:30 PM, the DJ played a song. It was the sound of silence. But loud.

"This is 'The Sound of Money Not Spent'," the DJ said. "It’s the rarest sound of all."

I listened. It sounded like *ssshhh*. Very peaceful.

At 11:45 PM, the balloon string slipped. *Whoosh*. The Man in the Grey Suit flew up into the sky. He joined the stars. A little grey dot next to the moon.

"Goodbye," I said. I waved.

"He's gone," Sammy said. "Part of the skyline now."

"Like the Empire State Building," I said. "But smaller."

Cabbed home at 12:15 AM. $85,000.00. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of myself. He was wearing the "Disco" wig. He looked very tired.

"Long day?" I asked.

"You have no idea," he said.

Went into the townhouse. I took off the wig. My hair was flat. Static electricity. I looked in the mirror. I looked solid. Very heavy.

I opened the fridge. I took out a bottle of Chanel No. 5. I drank a capful. It tasted like flowers and expensive mistakes.
