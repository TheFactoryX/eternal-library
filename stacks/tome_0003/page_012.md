Thursday, February 12, 2026

Woke up at 6:00 AM. I don’t know why. I just felt electric. Like I was plugged into the wall. The sky was dark, the color of a videotape that’s been recorded over too many times. Just static and grey noise. I watched the MirrorWall for a while. It was just showing me myself, sleeping. It was very boring. I watched myself snore for ten minutes. I’m a terrible sleeper. I kick the covers.

Got up and stepped on the scale. It said "LOW BATTERY." It couldn’t even measure me. I felt invisible. I had two pieces of toast and a glass of orange juice. The toast was "Retro-Brand." It’s made from real wheat, not sawdust. It was $12 a slice. It tasted like 1975. It tasted delicious.

Cabbed to the Factory at 8:00. The cab was a "Slingshot" model. It shoots you over the traffic. I hate it. My stomach stayed on the ground while the rest of me went to 23rd Street. It was $25.00. I felt nauseous when I landed. I saw Sammy. He was pale. He looked like a sheet of paper. He said Glitch—the mirror—accepted the mannequin. He said she’s inhabiting it now. He said, "Andy, she’s walking around. But she walks backwards. Because that’s how mirrors work." I looked in the corner. The mannequin was there. It was facing the wall. It was very creepy. It was very chic. I said, "Don't let her answer the phone. I don't want to reflect the telemarketers."

We worked on the "Last Supper" series. We are doing a version where Jesus and the Apostles are all cats. It’s called "Meow Supper." We used a lot of "Holy Gold" paint. It’s paint made from ground-up credit cards. It was $300 a tube. It’s very decadent. We painted the fur carefully. It looked like money growing on a cat.

A man came in from the government. The Department of Aesthetics. He said the city is too ugly and they want to tax it. He asked me to design a "Smile" sticker to put on all the potholes. I said, "Potholes are already art. They’re concrete donuts." He didn't get it. He fined me $50 for wasting his time. I paid him with a "Meow Supper" sketch. He looked confused.

For lunch we went to Balthazar. It’s still there. It’s loud and full of people pretending to be on their phones. I had the onion soup. It was $40. It was hot. It burned my tongue. I saw Donald Trump’s ghost. He was at the next table. He was transparent. He was complaining about the soup. He said, "The onion soup is a disaster. Total disaster. Nobody makes soup like me." I waved at him. He waved through me.

We came back and the mannequin had moved. It was sitting on the couch. It was holding a copy of *Philosophy* magazine. Sammy said, "She’s educating herself." I said, "She’s going to realize we’re boring and leave." I offered her a potato chip. She didn't take it. Mirrors don't eat carbs. That’s why they’re so thin.

I worked on the portrait of Basquiat. It’s a collaboration. I paint the background, and the AI paints the chaos. We used "Neon Graffiti" spray. It smells like candy and gasoline. It was $85 a can. It looks like a subway accident. I think Jean-Michel would love it. Or hate it. Probably both.

A courier arrived with a package from the past. It was a time capsule I buried in 1985. I forgot where I buried it, but the city dug it up to build a new skyscraper. It contained a wig, a bottle of perfume, and a signed head of lettuce. The lettuce had turned into liquid. It was green slime. It was $5,000 worth of slime. I put it in a jar. I’m going to sell it as "The Essence of Decay."

Left at 7:00. It was snowing. But the snow was pink. It was "Valentine’s Dust" from the weather machines. It gets in your eyes and makes you see red. I hailed a cab. The driver was a hologram of Elvis. He sang "Love Me Tender" while driving through a red light. It was very romantic. It was $22.00.

Came home and watched the moon rock. It was sitting next to the jar of lettuce slime. They looked like a still life. "Rock and Slime." It’s the story of my life.

I checked the banking app. The "Andy" bill is worth $0.00. It’s worthless. But I bought a coffee with it anyway. The robot laughed at me. But it gave me the coffee. Coffee is $9.

I took the wig off. My scalp is tingling. I think it’s the pink snow. Or maybe it’s just fame leaking out of my ears.

Good night.
