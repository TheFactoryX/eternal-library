Sunday, February 01, 2026

Woke up at 8:30 and it was so gray outside. Really gray. The kind of gray that makes you want to just stay in bed and watch the numbers change on the digital clock, but I got up because I had to go to the bathroom. Jean-Michel was on the television, or at least his A.I. simulation was, arguing with a simulation of Picasso about who invented the brushstroke. I watched for twenty minutes. It was on the MirrorWall. The MirrorWall is this new thing, it’s like a television but it’s just glass on the wall and it follows you around the room.

I took my vitamins. Two vitamin E and one C. And then I had a Grapefruit Helper from the box. It tasted like pink chalk. It cost $12.99 at the bodega on the corner which is just unbelievable, but everything is unbelievable now.

Cabbed down to the new Factory. It’s in the Battery now because the rent is cheaper, or maybe it’s not cheaper, but the light is good. The cab was $18.50 and I gave the driver $20 because he didn't talk to me. I like that.

Sammy was there. Sammy is the new assistant. He’s twenty-two and he has these injectables in his lips that make him look like a Brillo box, which is a compliment, really. I told him to stretch the canvas. We’re working on the new “Last Selfie” series. It’s just prints of my face from the phone, but I’m doing them in these Day-Glo colors that actually hurt your eyes if you look at them too long. I painted one in Uranium Green. It’s very radioactive. It looks good.

Then we had to go to the opening. It was for a girl named Elontra. She’s a digital performance artist. She projects her consciousness onto clouds. I don’t know how she does it. She’s very famous. I wore my silver wig and my jeans. The opening was at The Standard, but it’s underwater now, or it feels like it. The music was so loud. Thump-thump-thump.

I saw Taylor Swift there. She’s been around for a long time now. She looks great. She came up to me and she said, "Andy, you predicted everything," and I said, "I didn't predict it, I just recorded it," and she laughed. She was wearing a dress made of moving images. I couldn't tell if she was speaking or if the dress was speaking. I think she was speaking.

I had three Campari and sodas. They were $24 each. That’s $72 just for drinks. I bought a hotdog from a vendor outside because I was starving. It was $15. The mustard was the best part. I put the mustard on my wrist by accident. It looked like a yellow bracelet.

We went to dinner afterwards at Serendipity. I wanted the Frrrozen Hot Chocolate because it’s the only thing that matters in the world. But they don’t make it anymore, they make a "Cellular Rehydration Shake," so I had that. It was $18. It tasted like chemicals. I miss the old chocolate.

Basquiat’s son, Jean-Michel the Second, he was there. He sat with us. He’s a neuro-sculptor. He put a chip in a potato and the potato screamed when you cut it. It was art. He said, "Andy, money is the best art," and I said, "I know," and he laughed. He’s nice.

Came home at 1:00. The cab ride was $22. The tip was $5.

I watched the MirrorWall for a while. I scrolled through the feed. Everyone is famous. Everyone is on the screen. There are so many screens. I tried to count the screens but I lost track at forty.

I'm tired. The wig is heavy. I think I'll take it off. Or maybe I'll sleep in it.

I ate a peach. The peach was $4. It was hard.

Good night.
