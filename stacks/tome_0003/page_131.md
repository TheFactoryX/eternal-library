Woke up at 7:00 AM. The sky is plastic today. Not soft plastic, but hard, brittle plastic. Like a toy. A toy sky. The sun is a bright yellow marble stuck in the ceiling. It looks like it’s going to fall down and crack the pavement. I hope it doesn't. Cleanup would be expensive. The clouds are made of blue polystyrene peanuts. They are tumbling around. *Rattle, clatter*. It’s very loud. The noise is terrible. $0.00.

Put on the "Plastic Wrap" wig. It’s made of transparent saran wrap. It’s very tight. It keeps my thoughts fresh. It seals in the flavor.

Cabbed to the Factory at 8:30 AM. $3,000.00. Inflation is getting worse. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of Walter Mercado. He was wearing a cape covered in mirrors.

"Today is a good day for love," Walter said. "And for investing in gold."

"Or plastic," I said.

When we got to the Factory, the building was inflatable. It was a bouncy castle. A giant, silver bouncy castle.

"Hop," Bob said. He was jumping up and down. *Boing, boing*. "It’s exercise."

"I’m too fragile," I said. "I might burst."

We went inside. The floor was rubber. I bounced. *Boing*. I couldn't control my legs.

"I’m weightless," I said.

At 10:00 AM, the rat arrived. He was inside a clear plastic bubble. A hamster ball.

"I'm rolling," the rat said. He spun into a wall. *Bonk*. "I can't get traction."

The squirrel was there. He was chewing on the frame of the building. *Gnaw, gnaw*.

"It tastes like chemicals," the squirrel said. "Delicious."

At 11:30 AM, I had a meeting with the lawyers. They were holograms. Beamed in from Delaware.

"The estate is suing you," the lawyer said. "For copyright infringement."

"I am me," I said. "How can I steal from me?"

"You’re using your image without a license," the lawyer said. "It’s a violation."

"That’s ridiculous," I said. "I’m public domain."

"They want 50% of your future earnings," the lawyer said. "Plus residuals on the wigs."

I signed the paper. "Just take the soup," I said. "Take the cans."

At 1:00 PM, we went to lunch. We went to "Serendipity 3". It was crowded.

"I'll have the 'Frrrozen Hot Chocolate'," I said. "$5,000.00."

The waiter brought out a block of frozen air. It was on a plate.

"It’s light," I said.

"It’s gluten-free," the waiter said.

Jean-Michel ordered a "Cheeseburger". It was a picture of a burger. A print.

"I can’t eat this," Jean-Michel said.

"Frame it," I said. "Hang it on the wall."

At 2:30 PM, I worked on the "Oxidation" paintings. I was painting with copper paint. But the paint wouldn't dry. It stayed wet. It dripped onto the floor. *Plop, plop*.

"It’s weeping," Sammy said. "The art is sad."

"It’s just wet," I said. "Give it time."

But it didn't dry. It formed a puddle. A green, metallic puddle. It reflected my face.

"I see myself," I said. "Upside down."

At 4:00 PM, the Man in the Grey Suit came in. He was holding a giant sewing needle. A crochet hook.

"Is it finished?" I asked.

"It needs a border," the Man said. He reached up and grabbed a cloud—the polystyrene cloud from the sky. He pulled it down. He started stitching it to the roof of the Factory.

*Sew, sew*. He stitched the sky to the building.

"Now we’re outside," I said. "And inside."

At 5:30 PM, the wind picked up. The plastic peanuts started swirling. It was a blizzard. A packaging blizzard.

"Close the window," I said.

"There are no windows," Bob said. "We’re wrapped."

At 7:00 PM, we went to a club. It was called "The Box". But the box was clear. It was a Plexiglas box. We were on display. People walked around us and looked in.

"Look at the artist," a woman said. She tapped on the glass. *Tap, tap*. "Does he bite?"

"Only if you’re expensive," I said.

I saw Madonna. She was vacuum sealed. She was inside a plastic bag with all the air sucked out.

"I’m preserving my youth," Madonna said. Her voice was muffled. "I’m a collector’s item."

"You look mint," I said. "Unopened."

At 9:00 PM, the rat got out of his ball. He chewed through the plastic floor. *Rip*. He escaped into the wild. Into the carpet.

"He’s feral now," I said.

At 11:30 PM, I went home. The townhouse was wrapped. Completely wrapped in plastic. Like Christo wrapped it. Or like leftovers.

I couldn't get in. The plastic was too tight.

"I’m sealed," I thought.

I found a gap. A tear in the wrapping. I squeezed inside.

I went to the kitchen. I opened the Campbell’s Soup. The can was plastic. The soup was powder.

"Add water," the label said.

I didn't have any water. The pipes were dry.

So I just ate the powder.

It tasted like red dust.

"Artificial," I thought.

I went upstairs. I took off the wig. My head was sweaty.

I looked in the mirror. My reflection was delayed. It lagged behind me by two seconds.

I waved. The reflection waved. Two seconds later.

"Hello," I said to the glass.

"Hello," the reflection said back.

I don't know who is real.

Me or the image?

Or the plastic wrapper?

So.
