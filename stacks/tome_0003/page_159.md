Woke up at 6:00 AM. Something was beeping. *Beep-beep*. It wasn't the alarm. It was the "1/2" I bought yesterday. It was vibrating in my pocket. I left it in the pants on the floor. The fraction was trying to become a whole. It was failing.

Put on the "Disco" wig. It’s made of mirrored tiles. Very heavy. Very dangerous. If I fall, I could cut myself. I put on the glasses with the one lens. The left eye is for the past. The right eye is for the future. I just want to see what’s for lunch.

Cabbed to the Factory at 7:30 AM. $35,000.00. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of Lou Reed. But he was singing bird songs. *Tweet-tweet*.

"Are you happy?" I asked.

"I'm practicing for the zoo," Lou said. "I'm going to be an exhibit. 'The Rock & Roll Musician in its natural habitat'."

"Do they feed you?" I asked.

"Only dirty looks," Lou said.

When we got to the Factory, the elevators were working. But they went sideways. We went to the 4th floor, but we ended up in the lobby of the Chrysler Building. A doorman opened the door. He was made of chrome. He was very shiny.

"Wrong floor," I said. We took the stairs back down.

At 9:00 AM, the space was still white. From the "Blank Slate" series. But Bob had drawn a grid on the floor in silver tape. It looked like a tennis court. Or a prison.

"We're playing 'Memory Tennis'," Bob said. He threw a ball of aluminum foil over the net. It didn't come back.

"I lost," I said.

"You forgot," Bob said.

At 10:30 AM, the rat came in. He was wearing a tiny suit. He looked like a businessman.

"I'm going to Wall Street," the rat said. "I'm investing in cheese."

"Buy the orange kind," I said. "It's plastic. It lasts forever."

"It's the future," the rat said. He ran into the wall. *Bonk*. "I'm late."

At 11:45 AM, a delivery truck arrived. It was from the "Cloud". They brought me a bag of wind. It was very light.

"It's from the Man in the Grey Suit," the driver said. "He said he's floating over New Jersey."

"Is it raining?" I asked.

"Just exhaust," the driver said. $12.00 delivery fee.

I opened the bag. A breeze hit my face. It smelled like turnpikes.

At 12:30 PM, Jean-Michel called. His face was a scribble. He had drawn over himself with a black marker.

"Andy," the scribble said. "I'm erasing myself. I'm becoming invisible. It's the ultimate career move. Nobody can criticize you if they can't see you."

"Can you still sign checks?" I asked.

"I can sign the air," the scribble said.

"Don't fade away too much," I said. "I need you for the party."

At 1:00 PM, we went to lunch. We went to "Cipriani". But it was underwater. The restaurant had flooded, but the tables were still there. We sat in diving suits. *Bubble-bubble*.

I ordered the "Scampi". $125,000.00.

The waiter swam over. He was a merman with a mustache.

"Fresh from the ocean floor," he said. He put a plate of sand in front of me.

"It's crunchy," I said. I ate the sand. It tasted like time. Like tiny rocks.

"It's organic," Sammy said. His voice was muffled by his helmet.

At 3:00 PM, I worked on the "Rorschach" paintings. But instead of paint, I used inkjet cartridges. I squeezed them. *Squish*. Black and blue ink spilled everywhere. It looked like a bruise.

"It's emotional," Bob said. "It looks like a feeling."

"It looks like a traffic accident," I said. "Very romantic."

At 4:30 PM, Taylor Swift came in. She was wearing a dress made of teleprompter screens. They were scrolling lyrics. "I knew you were trouble when you walked in."

"I'm an installation," Taylor said. "Read me."

"I'm illiterate," I said. "I just look at the pictures."

She spun around. The text blurred. It looked like a barcode.

"You're a product," I said. "Very commercial."

"I'm art," she said. "I'm a limited edition."

I signed her forehead with a marker. "Now you're a Warhol." Value increased by 50%.

At 6:00 PM, the squirrel ran in with a nut. But it was a bomb. A tiny bomb with a fuse.

"It's a conceptual bomb," the squirrel said. "If it explodes, everyone gets an idea."

"Put it in the safe," I said. "With the money."

At 7:30 PM, we went to a premiere. It was for the movie "Empire 2026". It's a 24-hour film of the Empire State Building. But the building is made of glass. You can see inside. You can see people working. People sleeping. People waiting.

We sat in the theater. It was dark. I ate popcorn. $500.00. The popcorn was just kernels. No corn. Just hard seeds.

"It's minimalist," the girl next to me said. She was a "Simulacrum" of Marilyn Monroe. She was stitched together. "It hurts my teeth."

"That's the point," I said. "It's about endurance."

At 9:45 PM, I fell asleep. I dreamed I was a can of soup. I was on a shelf. I was red. I was delicious.

At 11:15 PM, I woke up. The screen was still showing the building. Nothing had happened. The lights were still on.

"Nothing is the best thing," I said. "You don't have to do anything."

Cabbed home at 11:45 PM. $48,000.00. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of me. He was wearing the "Disco" wig.

"Goodnight, Andy," the driver said.

"Goodnight, Andy," I said.

I tipped him 10%. He looked disappointed.

"Complete," I said.
