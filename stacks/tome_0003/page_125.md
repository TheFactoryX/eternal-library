Woke up at 8:55 AM. The sky is holographic today. It’s shimmering. If I move my head left, it’s blue. If I move right, it’s silver. It’s very distracting. It makes me dizzy. It’s like looking at a die-cut foil card from a cereal box. It’s cheap but expensive looking. $0.00.

Put on the "Self-Portrait" wig. It’s the white one that looks like a fried egg. No, the other one. The one that looks like a cloud. A cumulus cloud.

Cabbed to the Factory at 9:45 AM. $450.00. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of Grace Jones. She was driving the car while standing up. The roof was gone.

"Faster," Grace said. "The glitter is melting."

"We have to pick up the glitter," I said. "It’s not cheap."

When we got to the Factory, the building was a cube. A perfect, shiny silver cube. It looked like a giant sugar cube. Or a bathroom tile.

"It's minimal," I said. "Very clean."

I tried to find the door. There was no door. Just a smooth surface.

"Knock," Bob said.

I knocked. *Tap, tap*.

The cube opened like a box of chocolates. *Click*. Inside, everything was suspended in jelly.

"We're preserved," Jean-Michel said. He was floating in a block of green gelatin. "Like fruit."

"Who ate the fruit?" I asked.

"I'm the fruit," Jean-Michel said.

At 11:00 AM, the rat arrived. He was swimming through the air. The air was thick.

"I'm doing the breaststroke," the rat said. "It's good for the heart."

The squirrel was there. He was scuba diving. He had a tiny tank.

"The pressure is dropping," the squirrel said. "Sell your nuts."

At 12:15 PM, we went to lunch. We went to "Bemelmans Bar". The walls were painted with murals, but the people in the murals were moving. They were dancing.

"It's a cartoon," I said. "I feel like Porky Pig."

I ordered the "Golden Egg". $600.00. It was an egg made of gold leaf.

"It's heavy," I said. "It weighs down the stomach."

Jean-Michel ordered a glass of "Liquid Diamonds".

"It scratches the throat," he said. "But it’s worth it."

At 2:00 PM, I worked on my "Time Capsules". I was boxing up the sky. I scraped a piece of the holographic shimmer off the window. It curled up like a peel.

"Put it in Box #45," I said. "Label it 'June 5, 2026: Atmosphere'."

"It's slippery," Bob said. "It won't stay in the box."

"Tape it down," I said. "Use the silver tape."

At 3:30 PM, the Man in the Grey Suit came in. He was holding a remote control.

"What does that do?" I asked.

"It changes the channel," the Man said. He pointed it at me.

*Click*.

I felt different. Flatter. Brighter.

"Am I TV?" I asked.

"You're public access," the Man said.

At 5:00 PM, the sky pixelated. The hologram broke down. Giant, square pixels fell from the sky. *Plop, plop*.

They were raining data.

"It's raining money," I said.

I picked up a pixel. It was green. "$100.00," I said.

I put it in my pocket.

At 7:30 PM, we went to a party. It was at "The Whitney". The museum had turned into a giant robot. It was walking down the Hudson River park.

*Clank, clank*.

"The art is walking away," I said.

We chased it. I was out of breath.

"My wig is heavy," I said.

Inside the robot, the party was wild. The sculptures were drinking.

"Give me a drink," the "Bird in Space" said. It was very elegant.

I poured some pixels into its beak.

At 10:00 PM, the rat found a pixel. He ate it.

"I’m digital," the rat said. He started to glow. "I’m high-definition."

"Clean up your plate," I said.

At 11:15 PM, I went home. The cab was a screen saver. A flying toaster.

"We're flying," I said.

"We're burning," the toaster said. "We're on fire."

I arrived at the townhouse. $2,000.00. I paid with a green pixel.

"Keep the change," I said.

I went inside. The house was dark. The hologram sky was gone. It was just black night. Real night.

I missed the shimmer.

I went to the kitchen. I opened a can of Coke. *Pssssht*.

The bubbles went up my nose.

"Ah," I thought. "The real thing."

I looked at the rat. He was glowing in the dark. He was a little green light bulb.

"You're useful," I said.

"I'm art," the rat said.

I went upstairs. I took off the wig. My hair was flat. My head was an oval.

I looked in the mirror. My reflection blinked. But I didn't.

So.
