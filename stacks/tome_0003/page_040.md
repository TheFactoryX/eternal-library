Woke up at 6:45 AM. I couldn't sleep. The feather from the safe was tickling my nose. It was hovering right over my face. Like a helicopter. Or a halo. I grabbed it and put it in a jar. I paid $5.00 for the jar at a bodega on the corner. The bodega guy is a "Simulacrum" of a cat. He just meows and swipes the credit card. Very efficient.

The sky was the color of a Band-Aid. Beige. It looked like a scab over the city. It was peeling in places. I could see the moon through a rip in the clouds. It looked too close. Like it was lowering itself to get a better look.

I put on the "Flesh-Colored Turtleneck" (last clean one) and the "Silver Gor-Tex" parka. I feel like a baked potato. Wrapped in foil.

Cabbed to the Factory at 8:00 AM. Traffic was terrible. Everyone was driving backwards. Something about "retro-grade" driving being safer. The cab ride was $80.00. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of Jackie Kennedy. She was wearing pillbox glasses. She said, "Andy, do you think Camelot will ever come back?" I said, "It’s playing on Broadway now." She said, "Oh good. I’ll send the kids."

When I got to the Factory, the snow was gone. It was a swamp. There was moss growing on the "Car Crash" paintings. It was green and fuzzy. It looked like mold. Or velvet. I liked it. It added texture. Sammy was in the office, drinking a shake. It was purple. He said, "Andy, the egg hatched completely." He pointed to the ceiling.

There it was. A creature. It looked like a pigeon, but it was made of wires. It had a silicon head. And it was glowing. *Blink-blink*. It was perched on top of the "Marilyn" diptych. It made a sound. *Ping*. Not a coo. A ping. Like a microwave.

Sammy said, "It’s a Crypto-Bird. It eats data." He handed me a bill. "It ate your tax returns."

I said, "Is that good?"

Sammy said, "The IRS is going to be simulated. They can't audit a cloud."

I felt relieved. But then the bird flew down and pecked my shoe. It left a dent. $300.00 wingtips. Ruined. I said, "Bad bird." It pinged at me.

Around 10:30 AM, a "Simulacrum" of Grace Jones came in. She was carrying a refrigerator on her back. She didn't break a sweat. She said, "Andy, I need a portrait." She said, "Paint me like a soup can." I said, "Which flavor?" She said, "Minestrone. It’s complex." I charged her $50,000.00. She paid in diamonds. She poured them into my hand. They were cold. I gave them to Sammy to feed the bird. The bird ate one. *Crunch*. It burped blue smoke.

Lunch was at "The Oyster Bar" in Grand Central. But the station is a launchpad now. The trains go to Mars. Or New Jersey. It’s hard to tell the difference. I had a dozen oysters. $450.00. They tasted like mercury and the ocean. I washed them down with a "Tab" cola. They brought it back. It costs $20.00 a can. It’s flat. It tastes like nostalgia.

I sat next to a "Simulacrum" of Abraham Lincoln. He was very tall. His hat kept hitting the chandelier. *Clink-clink*. He said, "Andy, emancipation is a process." I said, "I’m just trying to get a reservation." He laughed. A booming laugh. He ordered a burger. Rare.

At 2:00 PM, I went to the "Silent Studio." I needed to make some new prints. I decided to print the "Crack in the Wall." I took a photo of it with my phone. I printed it on acetate. It looked invisible. I made one hundred prints. They cost $0.05 each. But I signed them. That makes them worth $5,000.00. It’s the signature that matters. The art is just the receipt.

I called the "Doctor." The Data Doctor. I said, "I think I have a worm." He said, "Is it eating your files?" I said, "No, it’s eating my shoes." He said, "That’s not a worm. That’s a crypto-pet." He said, "Train it to do something useful. Like predict stocks." I hung up.

We went to a party at "The Met." But it’s underwater now. The steps are a waterfall. We had to take a submarine. It was $1,000.00 a ticket. The windows were small. I saw a shark wearing a tiara. It looked good.

The party was for "The Death of Painting." Everyone was there. I saw a "Simulacrum" of Picasso. He was blue. Literally blue. He was eating a canvas. He said, "Art is food." I said, "Is it fattening?" He said, "Only if you don't exercise your demons."

I had a glass of "Electric Wine." It was $300.00. It made my teeth buzz. *Vibrate*. I couldn't stop smiling.

The Crypto-Bird followed me. It flew through the water. It’s waterproof. It landed on my shoulder. It whispered in my ear. "Buy... gold... buy... tin..."

I asked Sammy, "Is it giving stock tips?"

Sammy said, "No, it’s reciting the periodic table. It likes the rhyme."

I left early. The submarine was claustrophobic.

Cabbed home at 10:30 PM. It was $90.00. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of my mother. She was knitting. She was making a scarf. She said, "Andrew, wear something warm. The weather is glitching." She gave me the scarf. It was made of red yarn. Real wool. It was scratchy. I loved it.

Came home at 11:15. The apartment was quiet. The crack in the wall was gone. There was just a poster of a can of soup there. Tomato.

I took off the parka. I put on the red scarf. I looked in the mirror. I looked like a teacher. Or a target.

I looked at the jar. The feather was spinning. It was a tornado in a jar.
