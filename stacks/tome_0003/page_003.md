Tuesday, February 03, 2026

Woke up at 7:45. I don’t know why I woke up so early. It was still dark. The sky was that color of a bruised plum. I lay in bed and listened to the silence. The city is so quiet now that the cars don’t make noise, they just hum. It’s like living inside a computer.

I got up and weighed myself. The scale is digital and it talks to you. It said, "Your body mass index is optimal," and I felt thin for a second. Then I stepped off and I felt heavy again. I took a vitamin B-12 shot. It hurts but it makes the colors brighter.

Breakfast was a cup of hot water with a lemon slice. The lemon was $6. It was a very small lemon. It’s shocking what fruit costs now. I miss the days when you could just buy a bag of lemons and throw them away if you wanted to.

Cabbed to the Factory at 9:30. The cab driver was a human. I was so surprised. He had a mustache. I gave him a $10 tip just for having hair. The ride was $16.50.

Sammy was there with a girl named Pixel. Pixel is a hologram. She looks solid, but if you try to touch her hand, your hand goes right through it. It’s very romantic. Sammy says he’s in love with her. I said, "Is she expensive to maintain?" and he said, "She runs on crypto," and I said, "That’s the best kind."

We worked on the Marilyn paintings. Not the old Marilyn, the new Marilyn. She’s an actress who was born in a lab. She’s been cloned from the original cells. She’s exactly the same, except she has six fingers on her left hand, which is considered an asset now. She’s on the cover of "Vogue" this week. We’re silkscreening her face in neon pinks and electric blues. We ran out of the pink ink so we had to mix it with some red latex paint we found in the closet. It looks wet. It looks great.

Rupert Murdoch’s grandson called back. The one who wants the Uranium Green selfie. He offered $45,000. I told him I’d think about it. I think I’ll sell it. I can buy a lot of lemons for $45,000.

For lunch we went to a place called The Cloud. It’s a restaurant that doesn’t serve food, it serves smells. You smell the steak, you smell the potatoes, and a little pill goes down your throat and makes you feel full. It was $150 for the smell. I still felt hungry, so I bought a candy bar from the machine in the lobby. It was $8. The chocolate was white. It tasted like vanilla and disappointment.

I met a man named X Æ A-12. He’s Elon Musk’s son. He’s twelve years old and he owns a bank. He wanted to interview me for his podcast. He asked, "Mr. Warhol, is art dead?" and I said, "Art is whatever you can get away with," and he looked at his watch and said, "Time is money," and he left. He’s very serious. I like him. He didn’t shake my hand, he just scanned my face with his phone. *Bloop.*

Came back to the Factory and painted a shoe. Just one shoe. A high heel. It was black. I painted it white. Then I painted it black again. It felt like I was doing something, but I don’t know what.

I went to a party at a gallery in Chelsea. The gallery is inside an old water tower. You have to climb a ladder to get in. I hate ladders. I paid a boy $20 to pull me up in a basket.

The party was for a robot artist named Unit 734. It paints with its own blood, which is oil. It’s very conceptual. I saw Lindsay Lohan there. She’s the President of the United States now, or at least she was wearing a sash that said "President." She looked very rested. She was drinking a glass of milk. I said, "Madame President," and she said, "Call me Lindsay," and she smiled. Her teeth are so white they glow in the dark.

I had a glass of synthetic wine. It’s made from recycled grapes. It tastes like grape juice but it burns. It was $22.

I left early. The air filtration system in the water tower was making a whistling noise and I couldn't hear anyone think.

Came home and ordered a pizza. The pizza came in a square box. The pizza was square. It was $25. I tipped the drone $5.

I watched the MirrorWall. I watched myself scrolling. I saw a video of a cat playing the piano. The cat was better than most humans.

I’m going to bed. I’m tired. The wig feels tight today.

Good night.
