Wednesday, February 11, 2026

Woke up at 9:30. The sky was the color of a bruised peach. Purple and yellow and sore. I watched the ceiling for a while. The MirrorWall was stuck on an infomercial for a machine that irons your wrinkles while you sleep. It looked like a coffin. I ordered one. It was $4,000. If I wake up smooth, it’s worth it. If I wake up flat, that’s art too.

Got up and stepped on the scale. It didn't say anything. It just sighed. A long, electronic sigh. I think I depressed it. I had a cup of coffee. The coffee maker is psychic now. It brews based on your mood. Mine made a cup of mud. Thick, brown mud. It tasted like the earth. It was $8. I guess I’m grounded.

Cabbed to the Factory at 10:30. The cab was a "Vintage" model. An old Tesla with a human driver. He was wearing a mask of Abraham Lincoln. He didn’t say a word, just hummed "Hail to the Chief." It was very patriotic. It was $16.00. I gave him a $5 tip because I like the hat.

Sammy was there. He was talking to the sheet covering the haunted mirror. He was whispering. He looked guilty. I asked him what he was doing. He said, "Negotiating." He said Glitch wants a body. She said she’s tired of being 2D. She wants to walk around. I told him, "Sammy, bodies are expensive. They break. Mirrors last forever." He just nodded and went back to whispering.

I went into the studio to work on the "Jackie" paintings. We are adding tears today. Real tears. We hired a girl to cry into a bucket. She’s a professional crier. Her name is Tiffany. She charges $50 an hour. She thinks about her ex-husband and the money drips out. It’s very efficient. We caught the tears in a jar and mixed them with the diamond dust. It’s very emotional. It’s very expensive. One tear hit the canvas and looked like a star falling.

A package came. It was from the "Andy" currency people. They sent a crate of the new bills. They are holographic. When you tilt them, my wig blows in the wind. It’s very animated. I took a stack out to buy lunch. I tried to pay the delivery drone with my own face, but it beeped and said, "Insufficient Likeness." I don’t know what that means. Maybe my nose is too small in real life.

For lunch we went to a place called "The Cloud." It’s a restaurant on a blimp. We floated over the city. I had a salad. It was made of air and foam. It was $90. I’m still hungry. I ate the menu. It tasted like paper.

Sammy said he’s going to give Glitch a mannequin. He found one in the trash. It used to be a mannequin for a clothing store, but the AI took its job. It had no arms. He said, "She won’t be able to hug anyone, but she can still reflect." I said, "That’s love, Sammy. Dangerous and limbless."

We came back and I filmed a tape test. I just pointed the camera at the moon rock. I filmed it for an hour. It didn't move. It was the most boring thing I’ve ever seen. It was perfect. I’m going to call it "The Durability of Stone." I think it will be a hit.

I signed 50 copies of *Interview* magazine. The cover is a picture of a robot dog. Inside, there’s an interview with the robot dog. It says, "Bark." That’s the whole interview. It’s very profound. It captures the struggle of the modern age.

Left at 6:00. The streets were wet. The rain from yesterday—the confetti—had turned into a pulp. Like wet newspaper. It looked like the city was dissolving.

Came home and the moon rock was on the floor. The Dalek must have knocked it off. It didn't break. Rocks are tough. I put it back on the pile of bills. It looked satisfied.

I turned on the TV. The news said they found a new planet. It’s made entirely of plastic. They’re going to call it "Planet Coca-Cola." I’m going to paint it red.

I checked my banking app. The "Andy" bill crashed. It’s worth $5 now. I’m poor again. It’s a relief. Being rich is too much work.

I took the wig off. My hair is growing back grey. It’s catching up to the wig. Soon I won't need it. I'll just be me.

I had a cup of hot water. It was $0.00. The best things in life are free. But the second best are very expensive.

Good night.
