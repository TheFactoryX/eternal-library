Wednesday, February 04, 2026

Woke up at 10:00. The sky was the color of television static. It’s very soothing. I just lay there watching the light change on the ceiling. I dreamed about Brigitte Bardot again. She was made of gold leaf and she was melting, but in a good way. Like ice cream.

Got up and had a hair of the dog. Not a real dog. A drink. It’s a new thing called a “Nostalgia Tonic.” It tastes like 1985. It tasted like cigarettes and hairspray and desperation. It was $35 for the bottle. I drank it straight from the bottle in the refrigerator because I couldn't find a glass. The dishwasher is broken. It washes the dishes but then it eats them. I lost three good plates yesterday.

Put on the sunglasses. Even inside. I like how the world looks through the dark glass. It makes everything look like a movie.

Cabbed to the Factory at 11:00. The cab was a floating bubble. You don’t feel the bumps. You just float over the potholes. It’s very smooth. It cost $22.50. I gave the driver, who was just a voice, a five-star rating because he didn’t ask me about my tattoos. I don’t have any tattoos. Can you imagine? Me with a tattoo? It would ruin the brand. I’m a blank canvas.

Sammy was there. He was crying. His eyes were leaking actual tears, not digital ones. He said Pixel left him. She uploaded herself into a toaster because she said the toaster had more “depth.” I told him, "Gee, Sammy, you have to be careful with digital girls. They can go anywhere." I gave him a hug. He felt soft. He’s been eating too much of the foam.

We went to the bank. The bank is now a vending machine. You put your card in and it spits out cash or art. I withdrew $500. It cost $12 to withdraw my own money. That’s business.

Then we went to the opening. It was for a friend of mine, Jeff Koons. But it’s not the Jeff Koons from the 80s. It’s a clone. They grew him from a polyp. He’s very polite. The show was at the Guggenheim, but the Guggenheim is upside down now. I don’t know why. Maybe they wanted to catch the rain.

The show was called “Balloon Dog (Red) Exploding.” It was just videos of balloon dogs popping. Over and over again. Pop. Pop. Pop. It was very violent. I loved it. I stood in the back and watched the people watching the videos. Everyone was holding their phones up, recording the screen. It’s screens all the way down.

I saw Yoko Ono. She’s immortal now. She just glows. She was wearing a suit made of whispers. She looked at me and said, "Andy, give me a penny for your thoughts," and I said, "Inflation is too high," and she smiled. She still has the best smile.

We went to lunch afterwards at a place called The Pause. It’s a restaurant where you pay to sit in a white room and nobody talks to you. It’s $100 an hour. It’s the best deal in the city. I just sat there and thought about money. I thought about how I used to paint dollar bills, and now I just think about them and they appear in my account. It’s much cleaner. No paint stains.

Rupert Murdoch’s grandson sent a drone over to pick up the Uranium Green painting. Sammy boxed it up. We wrapped it in bubble wrap that has lasers in it. The drone lifted it up and zoomed away. I checked my watch. $45,000 appeared in my balance instantly. It was a rush. Better than sex. Cheaper, too.

Came back to the Factory and did some interviews. I sat in the red chair and looked at the camera. The interviewer was a 3D model of a newscaster. She asked, "What is the secret to your longevity?" and I said, "I don't die, I just change formats," and she nodded. She didn’t get it. That’s okay.

I painted a soup can. Just one. I painted it purple. Then I ate a can of tomato soup for dinner. It was $9. It tasted like metal. I miss the old soup.

Took a cab home. The cab was $28. Traffic is bad because of the weather. It’s raining money. Literally. The weather drones are dropping coins to stimulate the economy. You have to wear a helmet.

Got home and took off the wig. My head is so white. It’s like a cue ball.

I’m going to watch the MirrorWall. I’m going to watch the numbers go up.

Good night.
