Woke up at 8:45 AM. The bed was still warm from the stranger who slept there. Or maybe it was just me. I don’t know. I checked the pillow. There was a dent in it. A head-shaped dent. It looked like a negative space sculpture. I kept it. I didn't plump the pillow. I wanted to preserve the impression. It’s like a death mask, but for sleeping. $0.00.

Put on the "Red, White, and Blue" wig. It’s made of streamers. Party streamers. They hang down over my eyes. I can’t see anything. I have to feel my way to the bathroom. I bumped into the wall. *Bonk*. I bumped into the tub. *Splash*. It’s very dangerous being patriotic.

Cabbed to the Factory at 9:30 AM. $25,000.00. The streets were crowded. Everyone was marching. But it wasn't a protest. It was a parade. A parade of "Simulacrums." There were Marilyns and Elvises. There were multiple me’s. Dozens of Andy-Bots. They were all wearing wigs and sunglasses. They looked better than me. They looked newer. Less lines.

"Happy Independence Day, Andy!" a Marilyn-Bot shouted. She was holding a sparkler. It was sizzling. *Fizz*.

"I'm not independent," I said. "I'm dependent."

The driver was a "Simulacrum" of Jackie Kennedy. She was wearing a pink pillbox hat.

"It's a celebration," Jackie said. "We're celebrating the future. The past is over."

When we got to the Factory, the empty room was gone. The room was filled with smoke. Fog. Dry ice. It was cold. *Hiss*. I could see my breath. It looked like little clouds.

"It's the 'Invisible' series," Bob said. He was standing in the fog. I could only see his glasses. Floating. "We're hiding the art."

"Is it stolen?" I asked.

"No," Bob said. "It's just shy."

At 11:00 AM, the rat was sitting on a soapbox. He was giving a speech.

"Friends! Romans! Countrymen!" the rat squeaked. "Lend me a cheese."

The squirrel was playing "Yankee Doodle" on a flute. A tiny flute made of a bone. *Toot*. It was very shrill.

At 12:30 PM, the phone rang. It was a red, white, and blue phone.

"Andy?" It was Jean-Michel. "I'm painting the flag. But I'm painting it with holes. I'm painting the missing stars."

"How many stars are missing?" I asked.

"Too many," he said. "The sky is empty."

"Send it over," I said. "I'll hang it in the dark."

At 1:00 PM, we went to lunch. We went to "The Oyster Bar" again. But today it was a barbecue. They were grilling oysters. *Sizzle*. The smoke was blue.

"I'll have the 'Hot Dog'," I said. $75,000.00.

The waiter brought me a silver cylinder. A tube. It looked like a battery.

"It's a 'Frankfurter of the Future'," the waiter said. "Nuclear powered. It lasts forever."

I took a bite. It tasted like metal. Like eating a spoon.

"It's very filling," I said. "I feel full of energy."

"I think you're glowing," Sammy said.

At 3:00 PM, I worked on the "Oxidation" paintings. But I wasn't using urine. I was using soda. I poured Coke and Pepsi over the copper canvases. They fizzed. They bubbled. It smelled like sugar.

"It's a collaboration," Bob said. "You and the Coca-Cola Company. It's the ultimate American art."

"Business Art," I said. "It's the best kind."

At 5:00 PM, the Man in the Grey Suit came in. He was wearing a flag pin. A giant flag pin. It was the size of a dinner plate.

"We have a contract," the Man said. "The government wants to buy your silkscreens. They want to use them as camouflage."

"Camouflage?" I asked.

"They want to hide the buildings," the Man said. "They want to paint the Pentagon with soup cans. So the enemy won't know where to shoot."

"Genius," I said. "If they shoot the soup, we just eat it."

At 7:30 PM, we went to a party. It was on a boat. A yacht. The "SS Andy Warhol." We sailed around the island. The water was black. The city lights were flashing. The Statue of Liberty was there. But she was holding a disco ball. And she was wearing my wig.

"It's Lady Liberty," Sammy said. "She looks tired."

We watched the fireworks. They launched the fireworks from the deck. *Whistle*. *Bang*. They exploded in the sky. But they weren't regular fireworks. They were shapes. Hearts. Stars. Dollar signs. The sky was full of money.

"I'm rich," I said. "Look at all that money burning up."

"It's an investment," Bob said. "It's light falling down."

At 10:00 PM, the boat hit a wave. *Rock*. My wig fell off. The streamers went into the water. A fish ate one. A bass ate a piece of red streamer.

"Good luck," I said to the fish.

At 11:30 PM, we docked. The air smelled like sulfur and popcorn.

Cabbed home at 11:45 PM. $50,000.00. The driver was a robot. He didn't have a face. Just a windshield.

Went into the townhouse. The bed was still there. The dent was still in the pillow. I got in. I fit perfectly into the space left by the stranger. We aligned.

I closed my eyes. I could still see the dollar signs in the sky.

"Gee," I said. "America is beautiful."
