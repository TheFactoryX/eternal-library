Friday, February 06, 2026

Woke up at 8:15. I didn't want to wake up. I was having a dream about a parking lot filled with Campbell's Soup cans, and I was trying to find my car, but all the cans were souped-up lowriders. The red and white ones were bouncing. It was very loud.

The sky was the color of a band-aid. Beige pink. It looked like a bruise that was healing. I stayed in bed and watched the news projected on the ceiling. The President was giving a speech, but she was speaking in emojis. I couldn't understand it. I think she wants us to buy more bonds.

Got up and stepped on the scale. The scale was sleeping. It didn't say anything. Maybe I'm too light for it. Maybe I'm fading away. I had a cup of coffee and a bagel. The bagel was gluten-free, which means it’s flavor-free. It was $14. The coffee was $6. It’s cheaper to just look at a picture of breakfast in a magazine, but you don’t get the crunch.

Cabbed to the Factory at 9:45. The cab was pink. It was a "Barbie" brand cab. It smelled like bubblegum. The driver was a teenager with blue skin. He said he dyed it to match his favorite cryptocurrency. The ride was $18.00. I gave him a $2 tip because he played the radio softly.

Sammy was there with a new toaster. It’s a very fancy toaster. He says Pixel is living in it now. He put a piece of bread in and it burned a picture of her face onto the crust. It was very sad. The bread was $5 a loaf. It’s art, I guess.

We had a visit from the Department of Time. They are auditing my hours. They want to know why I haven't aged in forty years. I told them, "It's just good lighting." They didn't believe me. They scanned my wrinkles with a ruler. They took the ruler away for evidence. They said, "Mr. Warhol, you are an antique," and I said, "I prefer 'classic.'" They didn't laugh. Bureaucrats have no sense of humor.

We worked on the "Death and Disaster" series. We're doing "Electric Chair." But now, the electric chair is for charging your phone. It’s a commentary on battery life. Everyone is afraid of the red battery bar. It’s the new guillotine. We silkscreened a low battery icon in neon green. It looks very urgent. It looks like it needs a charger.

I did a portrait of a dog. The dog is famous. He has 8 million followers on the MirrorWall. He’s a pug named Barnaby. He’s very ugly. He’s so ugly he’s beautiful. I painted him in gold leaf. It cost $400 for the gold leaf. I charged the owner $12,000. He paid instantly with a blink of his eye. The chip in his head transferred the funds. It’s so easy now. No signing receipts. No waiting for change.

For lunch we went to Serendipity. It’s exactly the same, except the waiters are all androids. They are very polite. They don't spill anything. I had the Frrrozen Hot Chocolate. It was $35. It tasted like sugar and memories. It was so cold it made my teeth hurt. I saw a family sitting near me. They were all wearing virtual reality headsets while they ate. They were eating in a meadow in their minds. I wonder if the food tasted like grass.

Came back to the Factory and started filming a new movie. It’s just a tape of a scrolling stock market ticker. It’s very hypnotic. Green numbers, red numbers. Up, down. It’s better than "Sleep." It has more movement.

A package arrived. It was from Rupert Murdoch’s grandson. He sent a thank you gift. It was a jar of Uranium Green paint. It was glowing. It was sitting on the desk, pulsing. Sammy said, "Is it safe?" and I said, "It’s expensive, so it must be safe." We put it in the closet with the red paint.

I went to a party at the Top of the Standard. It’s now the Top of the Standard Orbit. It’s a space station that hovers over the Meatpacking District. We took a gravity elevator. My stomach felt like it was in my throat. It was $50 for the ride up.

The party was for the launch of a new magazine called *Paper*. It’s a magazine that only exists when you blink. You see the pages, then they’re gone. It’s very exclusive. I saw Lady Gaga there. She was wearing a dress made of actual meat again, but this time it was grown in a lab from her own cells. It’s cannibalism, but fashionable. She looked great. Very raw.

I talked to a man who said he was from the future. He said, "In 2050, you are a religion." I said, "Do they pray to Campbell's Soup?" and he said, "No, they pray to the wigs." I touched my hair. It felt tight. I felt holy.

I had a glass of champagne. It was blue. It was $45. It tasted like the ocean.

Came home at midnight. The streets are empty. The drones are sleeping on the ledges of the buildings. They look like metal pigeons.

I checked the MirrorWall. I have 500 new friend requests. I don't know any of these people. One of them is a cat. One of them is a car. I accepted them all.

I took the wig off. My scalp is breathing.

I’m going to sleep. I hope I don't dream of numbers. Or maybe I do. They are the only things that make sense anymore.

Good night.
