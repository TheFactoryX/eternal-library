Thursday, February 05, 2026

Woke up at 8:30. The sky was that color of silver polish. Very shiny. I like it when the weather matches the furniture. I stayed in bed for a long time watching the ceiling projector play a live feed of a beach in Bali. It was very relaxing. The waves were crashing. I turned the sound down because the ocean is too loud. It’s just water moving around. It doesn’t have to be so dramatic about it.

Got up and took a ginseng pill and a vitamin C pill. I chewed the vitamin C. It tasted like oranges. It was $4 for the bottle. I put on the white jeans and a black turtleneck. I looked like a waiter. I looked like an artist. I looked like a waiter pretending to be an artist. It’s a good look.

Cabbed to the Factory at 10:00. The cab was driven by a mannequin. It’s a new service. Dummy Cabs. They just program the mannequin to drive to the destination. It doesn’t talk. It doesn’t want a tip. It just stares ahead with its plastic eyes. It was $12.00. I felt bad not tipping, so I left a gum wrapper on the seat. It adds texture.

Sammy was there. He was eating a bag of "Air." It’s these puffs of flavored nothing. He was eating the "Cheeseburger" flavored air. He looked happier. He said he met a new girl. Her name is Glitch. She’s a virus. Not a biological virus, a computer virus. She manifests as a static buzz in the room. I think she’s nice. She doesn’t interrupt you. I said, "Is she expensive?" and he said, "She costs a fortune in antivirus software," and I said, "Love is expensive," and he nodded.

We worked on the "Disaster" paintings. We’re doing a series of car crashes, but the cars are self-driving, so the crashes are very polite. They just gently nudge each other. It’s very boring. We had to add some fire. We used red paint. $80 for the gallon. It’s worth it for the drama.

I made a phone call to Brigitte Bardot. Or at least, I called the number. It’s a recording. It says, "Thank you for calling. Please leave a message after the bark." I hung up. I didn’t know what to say.

For lunch we ordered in. We ordered from a place that sends you the ingredients and you look at them and then they send you the cooked food. It’s called "Looking." We looked at the raw fish for twenty minutes. Then we ate the sushi. It was $120. It tasted like fish. I don’t know what I expected.

A collector came by. His name is Mr. Z. He’s a clone of a billionaire, but he’s only twelve years old. He wears a suit made of gold mesh. He wanted to buy the Marilyn with the six fingers. He offered $2 million. I said, "I’ll have to ask Sammy," and Mr. Z said, "I don’t have time," and he pointed a remote at the painting and downloaded it. He just took the data. He left the canvas. He said, "The canvas is primitive. I only want the image." I guess he stole it? I don’t know. He left a check for $50,000 on the table anyway. I put it in my pocket. It’s better than nothing.

I went to the opening of the new "Studio 54." It’s not a place. It’s a frequency. You have to tune your brain to it. It was very crowded. I saw Bianca Jagger. She’s a hologram now, projected onto a white horse. The horse is a hologram too. She entered the room and everyone clapped. She looked fantastic. Very translucent.

I danced for a while. I just stood in the corner and moved my head to the beat. The beat was very fast. *Thump thump thump.* It made my heart hurt.

I saw a girl who looked like Candy. I said, "Candy?" and she turned around and she was made of wires. She said, "No, I’m Hardware. Candy is Software now." I don’t know where Candy went. Maybe she’s in a toaster too.

I had two glasses of champagne. The champagne was glowing green. It’s radioactive they say, but it makes you feel very warm. It was $40 a glass. Worth it for the warmth.

We left at 10:00. The cab ride home was $35. The driver was a robot this time. A real robot. Metal arms. He drove very fast. I said, "Don't crash," and he said, "Crashing is inefficient."

Came home and checked the MirrorWall. My bank account is vibrating. It’s shaking. I don’t know why. Maybe it’s nervous.

I ate a peach. It was $5. It was soft.

I’m going to bed. I’m going to dream about electric sheep.

Good night.
