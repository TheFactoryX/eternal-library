# Friday, July 10, 2026

> Written at 2026-07-10 23:59:59

Woke up at 7:15 AM. The "whole number" I made last night was on the nightstand. It looked fat. It looked satisfied. I put it in my pocket. It felt heavy, like a wallet full of coins. I looked in the mirror. The reflection was looking back. It looked tired. I guess I’m tired too.

Put on the "Dollar Sign" wig. It’s made of green copper wire. It conducts thoughts. If I stand near a computer, I can download the internet directly into my brain. It gives me a headache.

Cabbed to the Factory at 8:30 AM. $44,000.00. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of Diana Vreeland. She was wearing purple sunglasses.

"Blue jeans are the most beautiful thing since the gondola in Venice," she said. She drove right through a red light. *Honk-honk*. "Style is everything, darling."

When we got to the Factory, the "Blank Slate" was gone. The white room was filled with stuff. Mountains of stuff. Old televisions, stacks of newspapers, mountains of wigs. It looked like a garage sale in a palace.

"We're doing 'Clutter Art'," Sammy said. He was buried under a pile of *Interview* magazines from 1985. "It’s about accumulation. It’s about having too much and wanting more."

"It looks like my closet," I said. "Very nostalgic."

At 9:45 AM, the squirrel was there. He was holding a tiny microphone.

"I'm a journalist now," the squirrel said. "I'm writing an exposé on nuts. 'The Hard Truth'."

"Don't crack under the pressure," I said.

At 10:30 AM, I tried to work on a portrait. But the paint was dry. The tubes were empty. I squeezed them, but only dust came out. *Poof*. Grey dust.

"It's vintage paint," Bob said. "It's from the 20th century. It's extinct."

"Extinction is very popular," I said. "Like dinosaurs."

I painted a canvas with the dust. It was a picture of nothing. Just grey.

"It's called 'Shadow'," I said. "It’s $2,000,000.00."

At 12:00 PM, the phone rang. It was a video call from Jean-Michel. The screen was just a black square with a white line drawing of a crown.

"Andy," the drawing said. "I need a favor. I'm at the dentist. He's drilling my teeth gold. I need someone to hold the mirror."

"Gold teeth are good for the economy," I said. "You can eat with money."

"It hurts," the drawing said. "Art is pain."

At 1:15 PM, we went to lunch. We went to "The Odeon". But it wasn't a restaurant. It was a subway car. We sat on the seats. The train was moving, but the windows were screens showing the same street corner over and over. A man selling pretzels. *Loop-loop*.

I ordered the "Club Soda". $85,000.00.

It came in a dirty glass.

"It adds character," the waiter said. He was a robot with a tray for a head.

"It's gritty," I said. "Very downtown."

Taylor Swift was there. She was eating a sandwich wrapped in a page from a gossip magazine.

"I'm consuming my own publicity," she said. "It tastes like paper."

At 3:00 PM, a man came in to buy the "Shadow" painting. He was a "Simulacrum" of a Banker. He had a pig’s face.

"I'll take it," the Pig said. "I like the color. It matches my soul."

He paid me in acorns. Big, shiny acorns.

"Are these legal tender?" I asked.

"They will be," the Pig said. "After the crash."

At 4:45 PM, the Man in the Grey Suit drifted in the window. He was a cloud now. A little, grey cloud. He was raining on the rug. *Drip-drip*.

"I missed you," the cloud said. "It’s lonely up there. The birds are very rude."

"We missed you too," I said. I put a bucket under him to catch the rain. "Maybe you can rain on the 'Oxidation' paintings. Save me the soda."

At 6:30 PM, we went to a party. It was at "Studio 54". But the disco ball was missing. There was just a hole in the ceiling. And the music was backwards. *Reppu-reppu*. People were dancing in reverse. Un-dancing.

I tried to dance, but I kept walking backwards. I bumped into a girl. She was wearing a dress made of television static. Snow. *Shhhhh*.

"Nice dress," I said. "What channel?"

"It's the news," she said. "It’s all bad news. It’s very slimming."

At 8:00 PM, the DJ put on a record. It was a picture of a record. It didn't make noise. It just spun.

"This is a silent disco," the DJ said. "Listen to the money changing hands."

I heard the sound of cash registers. *Cha-ching*. It was the most beautiful sound I ever heard.

At 10:30 PM, the Pig Banker came back. He bought the bucket of rain from the Man in the Grey Suit.

"I'll take the weather," the Pig said. "I'm going to irrigate my desert."

"I hope it grows," I said.

At 11:45 PM, I went home. The townhouse was dark. The "whole number" in my pocket had broken again. It was back into two halves.

"I guess you can't keep things together," I said.

Cabbed home at $55,000.00. The driver was a Simulacrum of a ghost. He didn't have a face. Just a sheet.

"Boo," he said.

"Boo yourself," I said.

I went into the kitchen. I opened a can of Campbell's Soup. Tomato. It was red. It was soup.

I ate it cold. It tasted like 2026. It tasted like the future.

I put the empty can on the table. It’s art now.

Goodnight.
