Woke up at 8:00 AM. The walls were still breathing. *Whoosh, whoosh*. It’s like living inside a lung. A very expensive lung. I tried to find my glasses, but they had slid off the nightstand because the floor was tilted. The townhouse is listing. Like a ship. Or maybe the earth is just lopsided today. I slid down the hallway. *Swoosh*. It’s fun, like a slide, but hard to keep your wig on. $0.00.

Put on the "Static" wig. It’s made of steel wool. It stands straight up. It makes the radio stations in my fillings play all at once. I can hear Country Western and Rap at the same time. It’s a very interesting sound. Very avant-garde. My head feels like a radio tower. I’m receiving signals from Mars.

Cabbed to the Factory at 9:30 AM. The streets are covered in the diamond dust from the ketchup yesterday. It’s very slippery. The sunlight hit the diamonds and the whole city turned blinding white. I put on my sunglasses. The sunglasses were made of chocolate. *Melt*. They leaked down my face. Now I look like I’m crying mud. $15,000.00. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of Grace Jones. She was flat. 2D. She looked like a playing card.

"Turn left," I said.

"I can't turn sideways," Grace said. "I have no depth."

"We're all flat," I said. "Money is flat."

When we got to the Factory, the mercury floor was gone. In its place was grass. Real, green, growing grass. It was up to my knees.

"It's a lawn," Bob said. He was sitting on a riding mower. He was cutting a pattern in the grass. A soup can pattern.

"I feel like I'm in the country," I said.

"It's an indoor park," Sammy said. He was planting tulips in the corner. "It's gentrification."

At 11:00 AM, the rat came out of the bushes. He looked different. He was green. He had a leaf on his head.

"I'm photosynthesizing now," the rat said. "I don't need cheese. I need sun."

He stood on his hind legs and opened his mouth to the skylight. *Ahhh*.

"It's cheaper," I said. "Groceries are so expensive."

The squirrel was there. He was digging a hole. He buried a gold bar.

"I'm saving for the winter," the squirrel said.

"Put it in a bank," I said.

"Banks fail," the squirrel said. "Dirt is forever."

At 12:15 PM, Jean-Michel called. He was yelling. There was a lot of noise in the background. *Splat, splat*.

"Andy?" he said. "I'm painting with tomatoes. It's a food fight."

"Is it red?" I asked.

"It's a salad," he said. "I'm using dressing as a medium. Vinaigrette."

"Don't shake it," I said. "It'll spill."

At 1:00 PM, we went to lunch. We didn't go far. We just sat on the grass in the Factory. I had a delivery from "The automat". It came down a chute. *Clunk*.

I opened the little glass door. I took out a sandwich. It was a "Fluffernutter". $35,000.00.

I took a bite. It tasted like purple.

"It's artificial," I said.

"It's chemical," Bob said. "It's better than nature."

At 3:00 PM, I worked on the "Time Capsules". I was filling boxes with junk. I put in a wig, a press release from 1968, a slice of pizza, and a remote control.

"Seal it," I told Sammy.

He taped it up. "When do they open it?"

"In fifty years," I said. "Or whenever they get bored."

"I'm bored now," Sammy said.

At 4:45 PM, the Man in the Grey Suit came in. He was wearing golf shoes. He spiked up the grass. *Chunk, chunk*.

"We have a tax problem," the Man said. "The government says the grass is a farm. We need to pay agricultural subsidies."

"Will we get a check?" I asked.

"We have to pay," the Man said. "It's a luxury lawn."

"Charge it to the Foundation," I said. "Art is nature."

At 6:30 PM, we went to a party. It was at "The Frick Collection". But the museum was a zoo. The animals were in the frames. The lions were in the landscapes. The tigers were in the portraits.

"Look at that Rembrandt," I said. "The lion has very good lighting."

"That's a portrait of the King of England," a guard said. He was holding a shovel.

"It looks like a cat," I said.

We went into the garden. There was a real elephant. He was wearing a tuxedo.

"Is that Halston?" I asked.

"No," the elephant said. "I'm just an elephant."

He lifted his trunk and ate a peacock. *Gulp*.

"It's very messy," I said. "Nature is so violent."

At 9:00 PM, the elephant sprayed water on us. *Spray*. It was champagne. Dom Pérignon.

"It's expensive water," I said. I opened my mouth. *Yum*.

Bob tried to ride the elephant. The elephant sat on him. *Squish*.

"I'm part of the collection now," Bob said. His voice was muffled.

At 11:00 PM, we left. I found a peanut in my pocket. I ate it. It tasted like ivory.

Cabbed home at 11:30 PM. $18,500.00. The driver was a real person. I think. He didn't talk. He just drove.
