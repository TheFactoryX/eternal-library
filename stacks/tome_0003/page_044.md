Monday, March 16, 2026

Woke up at 6:00 AM. I couldn't sleep. The glitter rain from yesterday had dried on the windows. It looked like gold leaf. I felt like I was inside a locket. I tried to sit up, but my left ear felt heavy. David Bowie owns it now. It was ringing. *Rebel Rebel*. It was playing inside my head. I think he turned the volume up. Or maybe my brain is just a radio receiver now. I put my hand to my ear. It felt cold. Ceramic.

I got out of bed. My feet didn't make a sound. No *click-clack*. I looked down. I was floating. Just an inch off the floor. Zero gravity? Or just good posture? I floated to the kitchen. The Corn Flakes box was empty. I ate the box. It tasted like cardboard and ink. $12.00 worth of cardboard. Very fibrous.

I looked at the newspaper. The "New York Post." The headline was: **ANDY CRASHES, MARKET SOARS**. My stock price went to $20,000.00 overnight, then dropped to $0.05, then went back up to $15.00. I’m volatile. I need an algorithm to calm me down.

I put on the "Silver Gor-Tex" parka. It felt sticky. The glitter had bonded with the fabric. I looked like a disco ball. I tried to wash it off in the sink, but the water turned to wine. Red wine. Expensive wine. $500.00 a glass. I drank a handful. It was good. It tasted like the Last Supper.

Cabbed to the Factory at 8:00 AM. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of a slot machine. He kept making dinging noises. *Ding-ding-ding*. Every time we hit a pothole, coins spilled out of the dashboard. I filled my pockets. I made $40.00 in tips just by sitting there.

The Factory was loud. The walls were vibrating. The "Car Crash" paintings were screaming. Not a noise. Just visual screaming. The colors were clashing. Red vs. Blue. Orange vs. Green. It gave me a headache. Or a "head-ache" as the kids say.

Sammy was running around. He looked frantic. He was holding a fire extinguisher. He was spraying white foam on the "Electric Chairs." "They're overheating!" he yelled. "The left ears are catching fire!"

I looked at the painting. The painted figures were grabbing their ears.

"It's the Bowie effect," Sammy said. "He's playing your ear too loud. The resonance is shattering the art."

I touched my ear. It was hot. I called David. I dialed his number with my mind. *Ring-ring*.

"Hello?" he said. His voice sounded like velvet.

"Turn it down," I said. "You're breaking the merchandise."

"But it's a good song, Andy," he said. "It's the sound of the future."

"It's the sound of a lawsuit," I said. "Turn it down or I'll buy the rights to silence."

He laughed. *Ziggy Stardust*. The ringing stopped. The paintings stopped screaming. The foam dripped onto the floor. It looked like shaving cream.

At 10:00 AM, I had a meeting with a "Simulacrum" of Steve Jobs. He was wearing a black turtleneck. No, he *was* a black turtleneck. A floating turtleneck. He didn't have a body. Just a neck and a head.

"We need to talk about the branding, Andy," he said. "Andy Warhol Inc. needs a logo. Something simple. An icon. Maybe a can? Or a wig?"

"I am the logo," I said.

"We need to digitize you," he said. "Put you in the cloud. Then you can be everywhere. In every phone. In every toaster."

"I like toasters," I said. "Do they have good lighting?"

"Perfect lighting," he said. "RGB lighting. You can change your skin tone to match the bread. White for Wonder. Wheat for Wheat."

I signed the contract. I used the ketchup from Friday. It was dried up, but it still made a red mark. The contract was for $1,000,000,000.00. One billion dollars. I didn't feel anything. Just a *bloop*. A sound effect. Like a computer deleting a file.

Lunch was at "The Odeon." But it’s a drive-through now. You drive your car into the dining room and eat at the table. I don't drive. I took a cab. The cab drove onto the sidewalk. *Crunch*. We ran over a "Simulacrum" of a pigeon. It didn't die. It just re-spawned. *Respawn*.

I ordered the "Steak Tartare." It was raw data. It looked like cubes of blue Jell-O. It cost $300.00. I ate it with a silver spoon. I tasted like electricity. Like sticking your tongue on a battery. I liked it. It was clean. No chewing. Just swallowing.

A "Simulacrum" of Madonna was at the next table. She was wearing a cone bra that looked like a satellite dish. She was picking up signals from Mars. She said, "Andy, I love your new ear. It’s very chic." I said, "It’s leased."

She handed me a baby. It was a "Simulacrum" of baby Jesus. He was wearing a Rolex. He said, "Money is the root of all evil." I said, "No, money is the flower. Evil is the fertilizer." I gave him a $100.00 bill. He ate it.

I came home at 3:00 PM. The apartment was quiet. Too quiet. The silence was loud. *Tinnitus*.

I went to the bathroom. I looked in the mirror. Or I tried to. The mirror was a video screen again. It showed a loading bar. *Loading Andrew... 99%*.

I waited. I stared at my face. My face. The wig. The glasses. The skin.

The bar filled up. *100%*.

The screen flickered.

The image changed.

It wasn't me.

It was a can of soup. A Campbell's Soup can. Tomato.

The can looked at me. It had a label. And the label said: **ANDY**.

I opened the cabinet. There were cans everywhere. Rows of them. All with my face on them.

I opened the fridge. It was full of me. Cans of me. Boxes of me. Bottles of me.

I took out a can. It was cold. It was heavy. It had a price tag on it. *$0.50*. I used to be $0.50
