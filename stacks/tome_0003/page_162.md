# Sunday, July 12, 2026

Woke up at 8:30 AM. The room was spinning. *Whoosh*. Not like a record, but like a washing machine. I held onto the bedposts. I felt like a sock. A dirty sock.

Put on the "Halftone" wig. It’s made of tiny dots. From far away, I look like a photograph. Up close, I look like a mistake. I put on the glasses with the TV screens for lenses. They were showing static. *Shhh*. It’s very grounding.

Cabbed to the Factory at 9:45 AM. $60,000.00. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of Edie Sedgwick. She had chopsticks in her hair. Not real chopsticks. They were lasers. *Bzzzt*.

"I'm going to a picnic," Edie said. "We're eating lasers."

"They're very low calorie," I said. "Just photons."

When we got there, the elevator opened into a swimming pool. The Factory was flooded. But the water was blue paint. Oil paint. Very thick.

"We're doing 'Immersion'," Sammy said. He was doggy-paddling in the corner. "It’s like getting inside the canvas."

"I can't swim," I said. "I sink."

"That's the point," Sammy said. "You become part of the composition. 'Drowning in Beauty'."

I took off my shoes. I dipped a toe in. It was cold. $150,000.00 worth of cold.

At 11:00 AM, the squirrel came in. He was wearing scuba gear. Tiny flippers.

"I'm looking for the Titanic," the squirrel said. "I heard it went down somewhere near the photocopier."

"It’s over there," I said. "Under the boxes of 'Interview' back issues."

The squirrel dove into the paint. *Splash*. Blue ripples.

At 12:15 PM, Jean-Michel called. The screen was just a texture. Bumpy. Like asphalt.

"Andy," the texture said. "I'm painting a road. I'm painting a highway straight to heaven. Do you think God needs a toll pass?"

"He needs a publicity agent," I said. "He's been quiet too long."

"I'm putting a crown on the exit sign," Jean-Michel said.

At 1:30 PM, we went to lunch. We went to "The Russian Tea Room". But it wasn't a restaurant. It was a launching pad. We sat in rockets. The chairs were very pointy.

I ordered the "Borscht". $250,000.00.

It came in a tube. I squeezed it into my mouth. It tasted like beets and outer space.

"It’s fuel," the waiter said. He was an alien with three eyes. "For the trip."

"Where are we going?" I asked.

"Away," the alien said.

Taylor Swift was in the rocket next to me. She was texting on a phone made of glass. The text was appearing on her face.

"I'm writing a new song," Taylor said. Her cheek said *'Love is a Battlefield'*. "It’s about the war. The heart war."

"Is it catchy?" I asked.

"It’s viral," she said. "It spreads through air kisses."

She blew a kiss to me. *Mwah*. My chest felt itchy.

At 3:45 PM, I worked on the "Time Capsules". I found a box from 1980. It was filled with cookies. I opened it. They were fresh.

"They haven't aged," I said. "Preservatives are the eighth wonder of the world."

I ate a cookie. It tasted like peanut butter and nostalgia. $0.50.

At 5:00 PM, the Man in the Grey Suit came in. He was a raincoat. Just the coat. No person inside. It walked in on its own. The sleeves were moving.

"I'm drying off," the coat said. "The humidity is terrible for my fabric."

"You should hang yourself up," I said.

"I'm afraid of heights," the coat said.

At 6:30 PM, we went to a party. It was at "The Whitney". But the museum had been turned inside out. The art was on the outside. The building was wrapped in canvas. It looked like a present.

"We're celebrating 'The Unveiling'," the curator said. He was wearing a mask made of money. Real dollar bills. "Don't look at the art. Be the art."

I stood next to a fire hydrant. It was painted gold.

"Are you part of the show?" I asked the hydrant.

"I'm a retinal piece," the hydrant said. "I make your eyes water."

At 8:00 PM, the Pig Banker was there. He was auctioning off the air.

"Lots of 1,000 cubic feet," the Pig squealed. "Do I hear 5 million?"

"I'll take a breath," I said. "Just one."

"Sold to the man in the wig," the Pig said.

I inhaled. It tasted like perfume and exhaust. Very New York.

At 10:15 PM, the fireworks started. But they were silent fireworks. Just colors. Blobs of paint exploding in the sky. *Ploom*. Red. *Ploom*. Silver.

"It’s the 'Splatter' series," Bob said. "Jackson Pollock in the sky."

"It’s messy," I said. "I hope it doesn't rain."

It rained paint. Blue drops fell on my wig. *Drip-drip*. Now I'm part of the series.

Cabbed home at 11:30 PM. $70,000.00. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of a vacuum cleaner. He made a *whirring* sound. He sucked up the fare.

Went into the townhouse. The "Time Capsule" box was on the table. I put the cookie wrapper inside. I sealed it.

"History," I said.

I looked in the mirror. My reflection was back. It looked tired. It looked like me.

I took off the "Halftone" wig. The dots came off on my forehead. Now I have freckles.

I brushed my teeth. The toothpaste was blue. It tasted like the future.

I got into bed. The sheets were cool. I felt like a can of soup on a shelf. Waiting to be opened. Or maybe just waiting.

Goodnight.
