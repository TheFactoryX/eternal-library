# Sunday, June 21, 2026

> Written at 2026-06-21 23:59:59

Woke up at 9:00 AM. The sky is commercial today. It’s not blue. It’s a giant billboard. Actually, it’s a digital display. The clouds are scrolling text. First it said "Drink Coca-Cola," then it flashed a preview of the new "Robo-Monroe" movie. The sun was a spotlight. It had a lens flare the size of Central Park. I put on my darkest sunglasses. The prescription ones. Even then, I could read the fine print on the horizon. "Batteries not included." I wonder who changes the bulbs. God? Or Con Edison? $0.00.

Put on the "Billboard" wig. It’s a blonde wig, but the fibers are fiber-optics. They glow and flash corporate logos. *Nike. McDonald's. Warhol.* I’m a walking advertisement for myself. I felt very bright. Very loud.

Cabbed to the Factory at 10:30 AM. $4,200.00. The traffic was stopped because a blimp was landing on 5th Avenue. It was delivering a pizza. The driver was a "Simulacrum" of Grace Jones. She was wearing a helmet with a spike on it.

"Is it safe?" I asked.

"Darling," Grace said. "Safety is just a theory."

She drove on the sidewalk. We knocked over a mailbox. *Clang*.

"It's fine," she said. "The mail is mostly spam anyway."

When we got to the Factory, the walls were talking. They were covered in little speakers. Thousands of them. They were all whispering.

"Buy me... Take me... I'm on sale..."

"It's the soft sell," Bob said. He was wearing a suit made of price tags. He rustled when he moved. *Swish, swish*.

"I'm a bargain," Bob said. "Marked down."

At 11:00 AM, the rat arrived. He was carrying a briefcase. A leather attaché.

"I'm going public," the rat said. "Rat Inc. I'm issuing an IPO."

"What's the stock symbol?" I asked.

"VERMIN," the rat said. "It's going to the moon."

He handed me a prospectus. It was written on a cracker. I ate it. "Tastes like chalk," I said. "Good investment."

The squirrel was there. He was throwing acorns at the speakers. *Ping. Ping.*

"I'm trying to hit the mute button," the squirrel said. "The ads are too loud."

At 12:30 PM, Jean-Michel called. He was on a video screen embedded in a coffee mug.

"Andy?" he said. "I'm painting the subway."

"Is it dirty?" I asked.

"It's priceless," Jean-Michel said. "I drew a crown on every rat. They're royalty now."

"Send me the bill," I said.

At 1:00 PM, a model came in. She was beautiful. Very thin. Pale.

"This is Venus," Sammy said. "She's a drone."

She hovered over the floor. Her propellers were disguised as hoop skirts. *Whirrrrr*.

"I love her," I said. "She doesn't eat."

"She just needs charging," Sammy said. "Plug her in."

I tried to plug a cord into her ear. She zapped me. *Zap*.

"She's temperamental," I said. "A real artist."

At 3:00 PM, I worked on the "Money" paintings. I was painting dollar bills. But the paint was turning into real dollars. I painted a portrait of Hamilton, and he winked at me.

"It's alchemy," I said.

"It's inflation," the Man in the Grey Suit said. He was standing in the corner. He looked like a bank vault. "You're devaluing the currency."

"I'm increasing the value," I said. "Art is money. Money is art."

I painted a stack of hundreds. It weighed ten pounds. I put it in my pocket.

"I'm rich," I thought. "Again."

At 5:30 PM, the sky changed. The scrolling text turned into a giant "For Sale" sign.

"Who is buying the world?" I asked.

"Bezos," Bob said. "Or maybe an alien."

"I hope they keep the restaurants," I said.

At 7:00 PM, we went to dinner. We went to "The Four Seasons". The pool in the middle was filled with coins. Pennies and nickels. No water. Just metal.

"I'll have the 'Golden Goose'," I said. "$15,000.00."

The waiter brought a goose wrapped in gold leaf. It was shiny.

"Do I eat the foil?" I asked.

"It's the only part with value," the waiter said.

I ate the foil. It crunched. *Crunch, crunch*. It tasted like metal. Like licking a battery.

"My teeth hurt," I said.

"It's the taste of success," Bob said. He was eating the price tags off his suit. "I'm delicious."

At 9:00 PM, we went to a party. It was the "Summer Solstice rave". It was held in a planetarium. The DJ was a computer. It played beats based on the rotation of the planets.

"This is Jupiter's turn," the DJ said. *Boom, boom, boom*.

Very heavy. Very loud.

Bianca Jagger was there. She was riding a white horse. But the horse was a hologram. It kept flickering. It turned into a cow for a second. Then back to a horse.

"Nice horse," I said.

"It's virtual," Bianca said. "No mess."

I tried to pet it. My hand went through its nose. "Cold," I said.

At 11:30 PM, the party stopped. The projection of the sky in the planetarium shut down. We were just in a round room with a machine.

"Time to go," I said.

Cabbed home at 11:45 PM. $5,000.00. The driver charged me extra because the car was a limited edition. Number 5 of 5.

I went into the townhouse. I sat in the kitchen. I looked at the Campbell’s Soup. "Cream of Mushroom".

I opened the can. I poured it into a bowl.

It was full of tiny diamonds. Little sparkling chips.

"I can't eat this," I said.

"It's sprinkles," I thought. "Expensive sprinkles."

I took a bite. *Crunch*. It broke my tooth.

I spit the tooth into the bowl. It looked like a pearl.

"Art," I thought.

I swallowed the soup. It scratched my throat all the way down.

"Carbon," I thought. "I'm turning into a diamond."

I went upstairs. I didn't brush my teeth. I wanted to keep the diamond.
